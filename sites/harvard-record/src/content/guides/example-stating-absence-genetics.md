This worked example applies the guide on [stating an absence](/guides/method-stating-an-absence/)
to the genetics material in this site's record. It adds no facts. Each statement restates a row in
the [claim ledger](/ledger/), with its caveat. The point is narrow: "no record found" is a claim
about a search, and it has to be written as one.

## Step 1: find the rows that state an absence

The ledger gives an absence its own evidence state, nothing on record, defined on the [methodology
page](/methodology/) as: we found no public record of this. Absence of a record does not show
absence of the thing. Two rows on the genetics material carry that state.

[Ledger G-10](/ledger/#G-10) records that no public record shows a shared genetic database, a
research output or an operational program connecting the DNA-related items [@cnn-2026-thakuria].
Its caveat reads: a statement of absence across the sources reviewed. The row lists two sources,
CNN's reporting on the June 2014 invoice and the New York Post's reporting on the test-kit emails
[@nypost-2026-kits].

[Ledger L-03](/ledger/#L-03) records that no source reviewed alleges a violation of the Genetic
Information Nondiscrimination Act in connection with this material [@gina-statute]. Its caveat is
two words long: a statement of absence.

## Step 2: read the scope that travels with the absence

Both caveats limit the claim to the sources reviewed. That limit is the claim. G-10 does not say
that no database, output or program exists. It says the site looked in the sources it lists and did
not find one. A reader who has a source the site has not reviewed may know more, and the [correction
guide](/guides/method-correcting-your-own-work/) explains how to handle that.

The wording on the [DNA and genetics record](/dna-genetics/) follows the same form: the record shows
no shared database, no research output and no operational program. The subject of that sentence is
"the record," not the world.

## Step 3: separate three kinds of absence

The genetics material contains three different absences. They should not be written the same way.

The first is an absence across a body of sources, as in G-10. It answers a question that spans
several items: does any record join them?

The second is an absence inside a single documented item. [Ledger G-04](/ledger/#G-04) records that
the June 2014 invoice priced further work to a total of $193,400, including a $160,000 item called
"The Venus Project" [@cnn-2026-thakuria]. Its state is stated intent, and its caveat reads: no
record of payment or performance. The invoice itself is on the record. What is missing is any record
that the priced work was paid for or carried out. The person who sent the invoice, Joseph Thakuria,
described the idea as preliminary and unfunded. That statement is his account, and it sits in the
same row as the absence.

The third is an absence of later knowledge about something that is documented. [Ledger
G-02](/ledger/#G-02) records that cell lines containing Epstein's DNA were created in 2013, and that
their present whereabouts are unknown [@stat-2026-mystery]. The creation is documented activity,
from a single outlet. "Unknown" is the reported state of knowledge. It does not mean the cells were
destroyed, kept or used, and a sentence that implied any of those would add a fact.

## Step 4: notice absences carried in caveats

Some absences are not separate rows. They ride in the caveats of activity rows, and they must be
repeated wherever the claim is.

Ledger G-07 records that in April 2017 Karyna Shuliak asked for two 23andMe kits to be sent to Noam
and Valeria Chomsky, and that two kits bought on Amazon were shipped [@nypost-2026-kits]. Its caveat
says receiving a kit implies nothing about the recipient, and that no research use is described.
That second clause is an absence. It limits what the shipment shows: a retail product was sent.
Nothing in the row describes a sample, a result or an analysis.

## Step 5: write the absence without suggestion

The method gives a short list of forms that work.

- "We found no public record of a shared genetic database or research output connecting these items,
  in the sources reviewed."
- "The invoice priced further work. We found no record of payment or performance."
- "The present whereabouts of the cell lines are unknown, according to the reporting."

And forms to avoid. "There is no database" overstates the search. "Notably, no output has surfaced"
adds a hint that the absence is meaningful without saying why. "Nothing was ever done with the
material" turns an absence into a negative finding the sources do not make.

## Absence cuts both ways

The same discipline protects the people in the record. An absence of evidence of a research program
does not show that one existed in secret, and the site does not write as though it might. Equally,
an absence of a record of payment for the larger proposals does not establish that they were refused
or abandoned; the row stays at stated intent. The reporting states that Dr Thakuria is not accused
of wrongdoing, and the recipients of the test kits are not implicated in anything. Where the record
is silent, the page says it is silent and stops.

## Record it in the ledger

Each absence the site relies on has its own row or its own caveat, its own sources and its own
scope. That way a reader can see exactly which searches the statement rests on and can check it. For
the template, see the [claim ledger row template](/guides/template-claim-ledger-row/).
