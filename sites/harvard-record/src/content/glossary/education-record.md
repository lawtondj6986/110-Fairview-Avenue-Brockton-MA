---
term: "Education record"
short: "Under FERPA, a record that is directly related to a student and maintained by a school or by a party acting for it."
category: "Law and compliance"
related: ["ferpa", "hipaa", "redaction"]
---

Education record is the central definition in FERPA. A record qualifies if it is directly related to
an identifiable student and is maintained by the educational institution or by someone acting on its
behalf. The format does not matter. Transcripts, disciplinary files, financial aid files, emails
held in a student's file, and video can all be education records.

The definition has exclusions. Notes kept by one staff member for personal use and not shared are
outside it. So are records created and kept by a campus law enforcement unit for a law enforcement
purpose, employment records of people who are not employed because they are students, certain
medical and psychological treatment records of adult students, and records about a person created
after they have left the school, such as alumni fundraising records.

These boundaries explain much of what does and does not appear in public documents. A campus police
report is generally not an education record, but a copy of the same report sent to a dean's office
for a disciplinary case becomes one. A document with student names removed may be releasable when
the original is not, provided the students cannot reasonably be identified from what remains.
