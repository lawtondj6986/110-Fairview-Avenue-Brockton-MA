The Office for Human Research Protections, known as OHRP, administers the HHS version of the Common
Rule, the federal regulations for research involving human subjects found in 45 CFR Part 46. It
registers institutional review boards, manages the Federalwide Assurances that institutions file,
issues guidance and provides education on the rules.

OHRP also evaluates allegations that institutions have not complied with the regulations. When it
looks into a complaint it typically writes to the institution, asks for information and may issue a
determination letter describing any noncompliance and the corrective actions expected. Some of
these letters are published. Institutions are required to report certain events to OHRP, including
serious or continuing noncompliance and unanticipated problems involving risks to participants.

OHRP is distinct from the Food and Drug Administration, which oversees clinical investigations of
products it regulates under its own human subjects rules, and from the Office of Research Integrity,
which handles research misconduct. Its authority rests on federal support or the institution's
assurance, so research outside that scope may not be within its reach.
