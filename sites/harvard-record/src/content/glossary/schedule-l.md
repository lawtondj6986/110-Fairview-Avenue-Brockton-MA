Schedule L is titled Transactions With Interested Persons. It is where a Form 990 filer reports
certain dealings with people who have influence over it, such as current and former officers,
directors, trustees, key employees, substantial contributors and their family members or businesses.
Its parts cover excess benefit transactions, loans to or from such persons, grants or assistance
that benefit them, and business transactions above reporting thresholds set in the form's
instructions.

For a reader studying donor influence, Schedule L is one of the few public places where a donor who
is also a trustee, or a trustee's company that sells services to the institution, may appear in a
filing. Entries usually give the person's relationship to the organization, the amount and a short
description.

The schedule is not a complete list of relationships or conflicts. Reporting turns on definitions and
thresholds in the instructions, which change from time to time, and on the filer's own
interpretation. A transaction listed there is not improper by being listed; many are ordinary and
approved under a conflict of interest policy. Check the instructions for the year of the filing.
