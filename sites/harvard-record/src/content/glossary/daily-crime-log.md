term: "Daily crime log"
short: "A running, publicly available record of reported crimes that every college with a campus police or security department must keep under the Clery Act."

The daily crime log is a chronological list of crimes reported to a campus police or security
department. Any institution covered by the Clery Act that has such a department must maintain one.
Each entry records the nature of the crime, the date and time it occurred, its general location, the
date it was reported, and the disposition of the complaint if known.

An entry must be made within two business days of the report. The log for the most recent sixty days
must be open to public inspection during normal business hours, and older portions must be made
available within two business days of a request. An institution may temporarily withhold an entry
where disclosure would jeopardize an ongoing investigation or a person's safety, and must not
include information that identifies a victim.

The log is broader than the annual statistics in two ways. It includes all reported crimes, not only
the offenses the Clery Act lists, and it covers the department's patrol area as well as Clery
geography. It is narrower in another way, because it holds only what was reported to the police or
security department, not what other campus officials learned. An entry is a record of a report. It
does not show that a crime was proven.
