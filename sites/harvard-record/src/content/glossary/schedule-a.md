Schedule A is attached to the Form 990 filed by section 501(c)(3) organizations. Its title refers to
public charity status and public support. The filer states which category of public charity it
claims to be. Educational institutions with a regular faculty, curriculum and enrolled students,
hospitals, and organizations that receive a substantial share of their support from the general
public or government are among the categories. Supporting organizations, which exist to support
another charity, also report here.

For organizations that qualify through public support, the schedule includes a calculation showing
how much of their revenue came from broad public sources over several years. A university usually
qualifies as a school and does not need that calculation, but an affiliated foundation or institute
might.

The schedule matters because public charities and private foundations face different rules. Private
foundations are subject to rules on self-dealing, minimum payouts and excise taxes that do not apply
to public charities. Schedule A does not list donors; that is Schedule B, whose donor names are not
publicly disclosed for most filers.
