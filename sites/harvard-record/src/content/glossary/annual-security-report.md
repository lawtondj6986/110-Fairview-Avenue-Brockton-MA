The annual security report, or ASR, is the main public document the Clery Act produces. Each covered
institution must publish one by October 1, distribute it to current students and employees, and tell
prospective students and employees that it is available. Institutions with on-campus student housing
also publish a fire safety report, often in the same document.

The report has two parts. The statistical part gives counts for the three most recent calendar years
of listed offenses: criminal homicide, sex offenses, robbery, aggravated assault, burglary, motor
vehicle theft, arson, hate crimes, dating violence, domestic violence and stalking, and, under a
2024 amendment, hazing, plus arrests and disciplinary referrals for weapons, drug and liquor law
violations. Counts are broken out by location category. The policy part describes how to report
crimes, how the campus is policed, how warnings are issued, what prevention programs exist, and what
procedures apply in cases of sexual assault and related offenses.

The figures are counts of reports, sorted by the year in which the report was made, which may not be
the year the incident occurred. Low numbers do not establish that a campus is safe, and a rise can
reflect greater willingness to report. The policy statements are often more informative than the
tables, because they show what the institution has committed to do.
