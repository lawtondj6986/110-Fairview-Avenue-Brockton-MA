---
term: "Title IX coordinator"
short: "The employee a school must designate to coordinate its compliance with Title IX, including its response to reports of sex discrimination and harassment."
category: "Law and compliance"
related: ["title-ix", "supportive-measures", "formal-complaint", "responsible-employee"]
---

Federal regulations require every school that receives federal funds to designate at least one
employee to coordinate its efforts to comply with Title IX. That person is the Title IX coordinator.
The school must publish the coordinator's name or title and contact details so that anyone can make
a report.

The coordinator usually receives reports of sexual harassment and other sex discrimination, explains
the options to the person who reports, arranges supportive measures, and oversees the investigation
and resolution of complaints. Under the 2020 regulations the coordinator can also sign a formal
complaint when the person affected chooses not to, if the school decides an investigation is needed.
Many institutions have deputy coordinators in individual schools or departments.

The coordinator is an administrator, not an advocate for either party and not a confidential
resource. Information given to the coordinator is treated as a report to the institution. The
coordinator is also not a law enforcement officer. A report to the coordinator is separate from a
report to police, and a person may make one, both, or neither.
