## What it means

A rent roll is the tenant-by-tenant ledger of a property's income: who occupies what, for how much, until when. It is the primary document a buyer and lender use to verify the income underlying the NOI, and it is cross-checked against leases and estoppel certificates during due diligence.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

A South Shore mixed-use building's rent roll often shows apartments at below-market rents held by long-term tenants and a storefront on a handshake. The buyer's underwriting, and the price, depend on reading that roll honestly rather than accepting the seller's pro forma.

## In practice

A four-tenant Whitman rent roll shows three leases expiring within fourteen months and one tenant two months behind. The buyer prices in a lease-up period and a vacancy reserve and asks for estoppels from all four before the P&S.
