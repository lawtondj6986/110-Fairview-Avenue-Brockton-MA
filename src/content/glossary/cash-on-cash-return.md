## What it means

Cash-on-cash return is the pre-tax cash flow a property produces in a year, after paying the mortgage, divided by the total cash invested: down payment, closing costs, and any up-front repairs. It answers the question an investor actually asks: what does my money earn?

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Leverage makes cash-on-cash swing, and on the South Shore, where cap rates and interest rates are often close together, the return can be thin in year one and grow as rents escalate and the loan pays down. Underwrite year three, not just year one.

## In practice

Cash invested $350,000; NOI $84,000; annual debt service $62,000; cash flow $22,000. Cash-on-cash return is 6.3 percent in year one, rising as rents step up.
