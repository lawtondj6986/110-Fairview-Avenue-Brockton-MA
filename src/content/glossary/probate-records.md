## What it means

Probate records are the public files of the Massachusetts Probate and Family Court on the administration of estates: wills, inventories, accounts, petitions for appointment, and the decrees that pass title to heirs. The same court handles trusts, guardianships, and name changes. Dockets are indexed on MassCourts by county; documents are at the division clerk. Family matters are largely restricted and are not part of ICS's work.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Title on the South Shore runs through estates constantly. A Brockton two-family deeded in 1971 to a couple who both died intestate can have 9 heirs with fractional interests. Probate tells you who holds them and whether the estate was ever closed. For a buyer or lender, that is the difference between a 45-day closing and a two-year quiet-title action. ICS reads estate files for ownership and authority only, never for employment, credit, or tenant decisions.

## In practice

A buyer offers $610,000 for a Plymouth lot listed by "the estate of" the owner. The Plymouth Probate and Family Court docket shows the petition was filed 14 months ago but no personal representative has been appointed, and 2 heirs filed objections. Until the court acts, nobody has authority to sign a deed. The buyer keeps the offer open with a 90-day outside date and no deposit at risk.
