## What it means

An instant quote calculator collects a few inputs about a job and returns an immediate price range, capturing the prospect's contact information in the process. It answers the question every visitor has first, what will this cost, and converts curiosity into a qualified lead while competitors are still asking for a callback.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

QuoteBrain is ICS's version, included in every JobSite build and configured per trade. The pattern is proven across the South Shore: a visitor who gets a number stays, and a lead who arrives with a number closes faster.

## In practice

A Hingham painting company adds a calculator for exterior painting by square footage and stories. Site leads rise from 9 to 27 a month, and the owner spends less time quoting jobs that were never in budget.
