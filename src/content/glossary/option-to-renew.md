## What it means

An option to renew gives the tenant, not the landlord, the right to extend the lease for an additional term, on terms the lease sets out. It protects a tenant's investment in a location and gives it leverage it would not have at expiration.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

For a South Shore business whose customers know the address, two five-year renewal options on a five-year lease are worth more than a dollar off the rent. We negotiate for them on every tenant representation and for clear fair-market language when fixed rent is not available.

## In practice

A dental practice signs seven years with two five-year options at 95 percent of fair market rent. It controls the location for up to seventeen years while committing to only seven.
