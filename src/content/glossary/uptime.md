## What it means

Uptime is the share of time a website, phone system, or other service is available and working, expressed as a percentage. A site that was unreachable for 4 hours in a 30-day month had about 99.4% uptime. Hosting providers and software vendors often promise a minimum figure in a service level agreement.

## Why it matters for a local business

A down website does not announce itself. Ads keep spending, the booking link in every text goes nowhere, and the owner in Plymouth hears nothing until a customer mentions it. Cheap shared hosting and plugin-heavy sites fail more often, usually after an update. JobSite sites are built as static pages on a global network with few moving parts, and the same thinking applies to quote calculators and booking forms that take leads at 2 a.m.

## In practice

A Fort Lauderdale AC company's site goes down from Friday 6 p.m. to Monday 8 a.m. in July: 62 hours. It was spending $90 a day on ads, so about $230 went to a dead page. The site normally produces 5 leads a day. Roughly 13 leads were lost in peak season.
