## What it means

A modified gross lease sits between a gross and a triple net lease. The parties pick which operating costs the rent covers and which the tenant pays directly. Because the term has no fixed meaning, two modified gross leases can be economically very different.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Small industrial condos and flex bays across the South Shore, from Avon to Hanover, are often leased modified gross, with the tenant paying utilities and interior upkeep and the landlord handling the roof, lot, and taxes. Ask for the exact split in writing before you compare rates.

## In practice

A $14 modified gross rate where the tenant pays only utilities can be cheaper than a $12 NNN rate with $5 of pass-throughs. The rate alone tells you nothing.
