## What it means

Paid media is any channel where a business pays for exposure: search advertising, social advertising, Google Local Services Ads, and display or video placements. It is the most controllable lever in marketing and the easiest to waste, because it amplifies whatever system receives the traffic.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS treats paid media as the last layer, switched on once a South Shore client's site converts and follow-up is automated. Inside Market Lock it is managed monthly with the territory in mind: the ads run where the exclusivity is, and nowhere else.
