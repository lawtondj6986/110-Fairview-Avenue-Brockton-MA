## What it means

Conversion tracking is the setup that detects when a visitor does something valuable, such as calling, booking, or requesting a quote, and records where that visitor came from. It uses tags on the website, tracking phone numbers, and events fired by forms and schedulers. Without it, marketing spend is judged on clicks and impressions, which pay no bills.

## Why it matters for a local business

A roofing company in Rockland spends $3,000 a month across Google ads, a few Facebook campaigns, and a Yelp listing. Without conversion tracking, the owner knows only that the phone rang. With it, she knows that Google search produced 22 booked estimates, Facebook produced 4, and Yelp produced 1 at three times the cost. ICS wires conversion tracking into every JobSite and Lead Engine build so the dashboard shows booked work by source, not just visits.

## In practice

A Plymouth landscaper added call tracking numbers per channel, form events, and a tag on the QuoteBrain calculator's "request this quote" button. In 60 days the data showed 71 percent of booked jobs came from organic search and the Google Business Profile, while a $900 a month directory listing had produced two calls and zero jobs. He canceled the directory, moved $600 of it into review requests and photos, and bookings rose 18 percent the following quarter.
