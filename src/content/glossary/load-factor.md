## What it means

The load factor is the ratio of a building's rentable area to its usable area, expressed as the percentage of common area allocated to each tenant. It converts the space a tenant occupies into the space a tenant pays for.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore professional buildings vary widely in load because many were converted from other uses. Two buildings on the same Quincy block can carry 12 and 22 percent factors. Our Lease Deal Analyzer accepts either rentable or usable input so the comparison stays honest.

## In practice

Usable 1,500 square feet, load factor 18 percent: rentable 1,770 square feet. At $24 per rentable foot the annual rent is $42,480, or $28.32 per usable foot.
