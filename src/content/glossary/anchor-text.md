## What it means

Anchor text is the visible, clickable text of a hyperlink. In the sentence "see our commercial snow plowing page", the linked words are the anchor text. It tells the visitor what they will get if they click, and it gives search engines a short label for the page on the other end.

## Why it matters for a local business

Small business sites are full of links that say "learn more" and "click here". Those links waste a signal. A site with 30 service and town pages that link to each other using plain descriptive phrases is easier for a customer to use and easier for a search engine to sort out. On a JobSite build, each service page and service area page is linked with words that name the service and the town.

## In practice

A Taunton electrician has 14 links across the site pointing to the panel upgrade page, 11 of them reading "learn more". Changing them to phrases like "200-amp panel upgrades" and "panel upgrades in Taunton" takes 20 minutes. Over the next 8 weeks the page moves from position 14 to position 6 for its main search.
