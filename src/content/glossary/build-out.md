## What it means

Build-out is the construction that makes a space usable for a specific tenant: walls, ceilings, flooring, lighting, HVAC distribution, electrical, plumbing, data, and finishes. It ranges from a light refresh of second-generation space to a full fit-out of a vanilla shell. The lease spells out who designs it, who builds it, who pays, and when the clock on rent starts relative to completion.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Much of the South Shore's inventory is older flex and strip retail in Rockland, Abington, and Brockton, where "move-in ready" often means the last tenant's layout. A realistic build-out budget decides whether a deal pencils. Construction pricing in eastern Massachusetts has run well above the national average, and a town's permit review can add weeks. Get a contractor to walk the space before you sign the letter of intent, not after.

## In practice

A dental practice leases 2,500 square feet of former office space in Hanover. The build-out, with plumbing to six operatories, a lead-lined X-ray room, and new HVAC zoning, prices at $140 per square foot, or $350,000. The landlord offers a $50 per square foot allowance, $125,000, on a ten-year lease. The tenant finances the remaining $225,000 and negotiates four months of free rent to cover the construction period before it can see patients.
