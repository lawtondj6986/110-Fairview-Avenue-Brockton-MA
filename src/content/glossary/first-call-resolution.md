## What it means

First-call resolution is the percentage of customer contacts that are completely handled in the first interaction. No "let me check and call you back," no transfer to someone who is out, no second call from the customer asking again. It applies to calls, texts, and chat equally.

## Why it matters for a local business

Every unresolved call creates two more: the callback you owe and the call the customer makes when the callback is late. In a small office that doubles the phone load. The usual cause is not staff skill. It is that the person answering cannot see the schedule, the invoice, or the job notes. An ICS setup puts the booking calendar from JobSite and the conversation history from Lead Engine in one place, so the answer is on the screen.

## In practice

A Brockton dental office takes 60 calls a day and resolves 41 on the first contact, or 68 percent. The other 19 are mostly insurance and scheduling questions waiting on one person. After giving the front desk the schedule and a list of standard answers, resolution reaches 85 percent and callbacks fall from 19 a day to 9.
