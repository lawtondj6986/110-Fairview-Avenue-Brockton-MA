## What it means

A trade area is the geographic zone from which a business draws most of its customers. For retail it is shaped by drive times, barriers, and competition; for service businesses it is the area a truck can profitably reach. Defining it from real customer data rather than a radius is the first step in both site selection and local marketing.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore trade areas follow the roads and the town lines. A Bridgewater contractor's customers come from the Bridgewaters, Raynham, Middleborough, and Halifax, not from a circle that includes half of Brockton. ICS uses the same customer map to choose a building and to choose the towns a Market Lock territory should cover.
