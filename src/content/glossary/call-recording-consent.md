## What it means

Call recording consent is the legal permission required before a phone conversation is recorded. Some states need only one party's consent, meaning the business can record its own calls without telling the caller. Massachusetts is not one of them. Under M.G.L. c. 272, Section 99, secretly recording a call is a crime, and the statute reaches both wiretapping and the quiet "record" button on a tracking line. Florida is also an all-party state. This is not legal advice: talk to counsel before you record anything.

## Why it matters for a local business

Call tracking, AI receptionists, and missed-call text-back all touch the phone line, and vendors often ship with recording enabled because it is useful for coaching staff and settling "you said, I said" disputes over quotes. A Brockton auto shop that flips that switch without a consent announcement has created legal exposure on every call. ICS configures Lead Engine phone lines with recording off by default, and when a client wants it on, the line plays a notice before the call connects and the setting is documented.

## In practice

A Quincy dental office wanted recordings to review how front-desk staff handled new-patient calls. The practice's attorney approved a short announcement ("this call may be recorded for quality") played before ringing through. Roughly 2 percent of callers hung up at the notice. The office kept recordings for 90 days, used them in two coaching sessions a month, and saw new-patient bookings from calls rise from 41 percent to 55 percent over a quarter.
