## What it means

Voice search is a query spoken aloud to a phone assistant, car system, or smart speaker, which then answers with a short spoken response and sometimes a single result on screen. Because there is room for one or two answers rather than a list, voice search strongly favors businesses whose listings are accurate and complete and whose web content answers specific questions directly.

## Why it matters for a local business

Someone driving down Route 3 with a check-engine light asks the car to find a mechanic in Plymouth. The system reads back one or two shops with good ratings, correct hours, and a phone number it trusts. If your Google Business Profile says closed when you are open, or your hours differ from Yelp, you are not in that answer. ICS treats voice search as part of the same work as local SEO and answer engine optimization: clean listings, direct-answer content on JobSite pages, and structured data that machines can parse.

## In practice

A Weymouth plumbing company audited its listings and found four different phone numbers and three sets of hours across Google, Apple Maps, Yelp, and its own site, left over from a move and an old tracking number. ICS corrected every listing, added hours and service structured data, and rewrote the top of each service page to answer "how much does X cost in Weymouth" in one sentence. Within three months, calls tagged as coming from Apple Maps and Google Assistant rose from about 4 a month to 19.
