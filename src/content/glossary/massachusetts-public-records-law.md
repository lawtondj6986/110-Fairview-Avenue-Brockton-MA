## What it means

Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 66 and the 2016 reforms establish the public's right to inspect and copy government records, with defined response deadlines and limited exemptions. It is the state-level tool for obtaining records that agencies hold but do not publish, and it is central to thorough research on property, process, and public figures.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

For a South Shore property, the building department's permit file and the health department's septic records are often the most informative documents in existence, and they are obtained by request. ICS files them routinely as part of commercial due diligence and political research.
