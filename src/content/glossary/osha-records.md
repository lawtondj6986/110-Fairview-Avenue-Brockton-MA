## What it means

OSHA records are the federal government's public data on workplace safety enforcement: when an employer was inspected, why, what was cited, how each violation was classified, and what penalty was assessed and finally paid. In Massachusetts, federal OSHA covers private employers, while public sector workplaces fall under a state program.

## Why it matters in a dossier

For construction, manufacturing, and trades businesses, safety history is operating history. It affects insurance costs, bid eligibility, and the odds of a job shutting down. We search the legal name, trade names, and prior entities, since records attach to the establishment as it was named at inspection. We report initial and final outcomes, because the gap between them matters.

## In practice

An owner planning a $1.4 million renovation in Brockton shortlists 3 general contractors. OSHA searches show 2 with no inspections in 10 years. The third has 4 inspections and 6 citations, including 2 classified as serious for fall protection, with final penalties of $21,000. The owner asks all 3 for their safety programs and weighs the answers. Each citation is listed with its inspection number and current status, so the contractor can address the specifics and not a rumor.
