## What it means

An AI receptionist is a voice and text agent that picks up when your team cannot. It answers common questions, collects the caller's name, address, and need, books an appointment on your calendar, and sends a summary to a person. Unlike voicemail, it holds a conversation and closes the loop, and unlike a call center, it costs a fraction of one employee.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Hanover plumber working under a sink cannot answer the phone, and most callers will not leave a voicemail. They call the next listing. An AI receptionist inside Lead Engine answers on the second ring, books the visit into the JobSite calendar, and texts the owner the details. The lead is kept without hiring front-desk staff, and it works at 9 p.m. on a Sunday when the furnace quits.

## In practice

A two-truck HVAC company in Rockland was missing about 30 calls a week during the summer rush. After adding an AI receptionist, 22 of those callers were answered, 14 booked a service visit, and the average ticket was $410. That is roughly $5,700 a week in work that used to go to a competitor, for a monthly cost of a few hundred dollars. The owner reviews the call summaries each evening and overrides anything the system got wrong, which is rare after the first two weeks.
