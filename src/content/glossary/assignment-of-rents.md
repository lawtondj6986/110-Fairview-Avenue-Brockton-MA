## What it means

An assignment of rents is a document, recorded alongside the mortgage or built into it, in which a borrower assigns the property's leases and rental income to the lender as additional collateral. The borrower normally keeps the right to collect rent until a default. After a default, the lender can step into the landlord's position on collections.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Nearly every bank loan on an income property here includes one, from a six-unit retail strip in Weymouth to a multi-tenant industrial building off Route 24 in Brockton. It is recorded at the registry of deeds, so it shows up in any title search. Buyers assuming a loan and tenants signing an SNDA should both know it is there.

## In practice

A strip center in Quincy collects $28,000 a month from five tenants and carries a $2,100,000 loan. The owner misses two payments. The lender sends each tenant a notice to pay rent to a lockbox account, and within 30 days it controls the $28,000 monthly income while the default is worked out or the foreclosure proceeds.
