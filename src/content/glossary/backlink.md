## What it means

A backlink is a link on someone else's website that points to yours. Search engines read each one as a signal that another site found your page worth referencing. Quality counts far more than quantity: a link from a relevant, trusted, local source carries more weight than dozens from sites with no connection to your trade or area.

## Why it matters for a local business

For a local business, the most useful backlinks are local. A link from the Rockland youth hockey sponsor page, the South Shore Chamber directory, or a supplier's "find a dealer" page tells a search engine you are a real business in a real place. Those links also send actual customers. Proof Engine case studies give partners and suppliers something concrete to link to.

## In practice

A Hanover remodeler has 12 backlinks, 9 of them from generic directories. Over 6 months the owner adds 8 more: 2 supplier dealer pages, the chamber, a Little League sponsorship, and 4 from a local news story on a restored storefront. Calls from organic search rise from 11 a month to 19.
