## What it means

Retargeting delivers advertising to people who have previously interacted with a business, typically by visiting its website, by using tracking pixels or uploaded customer lists. Because the audience has already shown interest, response rates are higher than for cold advertising.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

In an ICS Market Lock system, retargeting is a supporting player: a small monthly budget reminding people in the client's towns who looked at a service page or received a quote. It works best alongside the follow-up sequence, not instead of it.
