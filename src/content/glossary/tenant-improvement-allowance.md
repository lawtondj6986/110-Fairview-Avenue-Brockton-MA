## What it means

A tenant improvement allowance is a landlord contribution, usually quoted per square foot, toward the cost of preparing space for a tenant: walls, flooring, lighting, HVAC distribution, sometimes plumbing for a medical or restaurant use. The landlord treats it as an investment repaid through the rent.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Second-generation South Shore retail and office space often needs less than a tenant fears, and landlords here are more willing to do the work themselves than to cut a check. Ask for the work letter in writing: who builds what, to what spec, by when.

## In practice

A $20 per square foot allowance on 2,500 square feet is $50,000 toward the build-out. On a five-year lease, the landlord is effectively financing $10,000 a year through your rent.
