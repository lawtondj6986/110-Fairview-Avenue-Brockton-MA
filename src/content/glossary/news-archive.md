## What it means

A news archive is an organized, searchable body of past reporting: newspapers, broadcast transcripts, trade publications, and wire stories. Archives may be run by the publisher, by a licensed database vendor, or by a library. Coverage, date range, and search quality vary widely between them.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Local papers cover what no database indexes: the 2009 select board fight, the ribbon cutting, the business that closed overnight. A story gives names, dates, and quotes that point to primary records. It also shows what the public already knows, which matters for reputation and for campaigns. We note the publication, the date, and whether the story was corrected or followed up.

## In practice

Researching a developer active on the South Shore, we search 20 years of regional coverage and find 47 articles. Six mention a stalled condominium project from 2011. That lead takes us to the registry and the Superior Court, where the foreclosure and 2 contractor suits are documented. The dossier cites the court records, with the articles listed as how they were found. Three of the 47 stories carried later corrections, which we note, and none of those three is used to support a finding.
