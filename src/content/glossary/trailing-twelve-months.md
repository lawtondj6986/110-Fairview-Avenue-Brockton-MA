## What it means

The trailing twelve months statement is an operating history covering the twelve months immediately preceding the analysis. It is the preferred starting point for underwriting because it reflects actual performance, and buyers normalize it by removing one-time items and adding missing costs such as management and reserves.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Many South Shore sellers of small commercial property have never produced a T-12; the building ran out of a checkbook. ICS rebuilds one from source documents for every listing we take, because a documented history consistently produces a higher price than an undocumented promise.
