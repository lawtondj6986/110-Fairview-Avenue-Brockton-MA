## What it means

Media monitoring is the continuous or retrospective collection of a subject's public footprint in news and published communications. It provides the statements, positions, and reputational history that records alone do not, and it is a primary input to both political research and reputational due diligence.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore local news, from the Brockton Enterprise to town-level outlets and public meeting archives, is a rich and under-searched record. ICS's research pulls from it systematically, with every item archived and dated.
