## What it means

A hard money loan is short-term financing from a private lender or fund, secured by real estate and sized mostly on the property's value and the exit plan. The borrower's income matters less than with a bank. In exchange for speed and flexibility, the rate and fees are high and the loan-to-value is conservative.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Banks here usually need 45 to 60 days and an appraisal, an environmental report, and a stabilized property. Foreclosure auctions, estate sales, and vacant mill buildings in Brockton or Taunton do not wait that long. Private lenders fill that gap. The risk is the back end: if the renovation, lease-up, or refinance runs late, extension fees and default interest add up quickly.

## In practice

An investor wins a vacant 8,000 square foot building in Brockton at auction for $500,000 with 30 days to close. A private lender advances $325,000, 65 percent, at 12 percent interest-only with 2 points. That is $6,500 at closing and $3,250 a month. After 10 months of work and leasing, a bank refinance pays it off.
