## What it means

A chat handoff is the transfer of a conversation from an automated chat agent to a human at the business. A proper handoff passes along the full transcript, the customer's contact details, and a short summary of what they need. It also sets an honest expectation with the customer about who will respond and when.

## Why it matters for a local business

Automated chat is good at hours, service areas, booking, and basic questions. It should not be quoting a custom job or calming an upset customer. The handoff is where most chat setups fail: the customer asks for a person and gets a loop. ICS chat agents are built with fixed handoff rules, and the conversation lands in the Lead Engine inbox with a summary so the owner can reply in one message.

## In practice

A Fort Lauderdale dental office's chat handles 200 conversations a month. About 150 end in a booking or an answered question, and 50 hand off to the front desk with a two-line summary. Average time for staff to pick up a handoff during office hours is 6 minutes. After hours, the customer is told a person will text back by 9 a.m.
