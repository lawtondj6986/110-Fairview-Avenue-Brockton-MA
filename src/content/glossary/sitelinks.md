## What it means

Sitelinks are the additional links that appear under a website's main listing in search results, pointing to specific inner pages such as Services, Contact, or Book Online. Search engines generate them automatically. They show up most often when someone searches for a business by name, and paid ads have a separate, manually configured version called sitelink assets.

## Why it matters for a local business

Sitelinks make a branded result take up far more of the screen, which pushes directories and competitors further down. They also let a customer jump straight to booking or the phone number. A site with one long home page and no real inner pages gives a search engine nothing to offer. A JobSite build with distinct service, area, and booking pages gives it clear candidates.

## In practice

A Quincy law office with a single-page site shows one plain result for its name. After a rebuild with 8 distinct pages, sitelinks for Practice Areas, Attorneys, Contact, and Consultation appear within 6 weeks. Clicks on the branded result rise from 410 to 520 a month, and 90 of them land directly on the consultation page.
