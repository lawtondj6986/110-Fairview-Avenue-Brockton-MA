## What it means

A pipeline is the structured view of every open opportunity by stage. It makes the sales process visible, shows where leads stall, and gives automation the hooks it needs: a quote that has sat for four days triggers a follow-up because the pipeline knows it is a quote and knows it is four days old.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The first ICS dashboard a South Shore client sees usually reveals the same thing: a pile of quotes nobody followed up on. Building the pipeline is how that pile becomes a list, and the list becomes jobs.
