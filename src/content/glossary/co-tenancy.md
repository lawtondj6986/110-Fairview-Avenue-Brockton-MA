## What it means

A co-tenancy clause ties a tenant's obligations to the presence of other tenants. If a named anchor closes or overall occupancy falls, the tenant pays reduced rent or may terminate. It recognizes that a small store's traffic often depends on neighbors it does not control.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Along Route 18 and Route 53, small tenants live and die by the grocery or pharmacy anchor next door. A clause tied to that specific neighbor is the realistic ask; a full institutional co-tenancy clause is not. Knowing the difference saves negotiating capital.

## In practice

A fitness studio's lease provides that if the adjacent supermarket closes and is not replaced within 180 days, rent falls to 50 percent until it is, and the studio may terminate after a year.
