## What it means

Comparable sales are the evidence behind a value opinion. Appraisers and brokers select recent, similar, arm's-length transactions and adjust for differences in size, condition, location, and timing to arrive at an indicated value for the subject property.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore a good comp for a 7,000 square foot industrial building might be in the next town, two years old, and adjusted for the fact that it had a newer roof. Thin data rewards judgment and punishes anyone who pulls three sales from a website and calls it analysis.

## In practice

Three recent Rockland and Abington flex sales at $168, $181, and $190 per square foot, adjusted for condition and lot size, support a value of roughly $178 per square foot for a subject building of similar vintage.
