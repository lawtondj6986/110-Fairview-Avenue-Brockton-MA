## What it means

Key person risk describes the vulnerability of an enterprise or transaction to the loss, failure, or misconduct of a single critical individual. In small-business and real estate transactions it is often the dominant risk, and it is assessed by researching the person's public record rather than the entity's balance sheet.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Buying a South Shore business with its building usually means buying a relationship with the seller through a transition, a lease, or seller financing. Knowing the seller's record, obligations, and other ventures before closing is what ICS's brief delivers.
