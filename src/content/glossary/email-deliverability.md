## What it means

Email deliverability is the rate at which your messages reach the recipient's inbox rather than bouncing or being filtered to spam. It depends on domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), sender reputation, list quality, and content. A message that is never seen cannot convert, no matter how good the offer is.

## Why it matters for a local business

Past customers are a local business's cheapest source of new work, and email is the cheapest way to reach them. A Weymouth HVAC company with 2,000 past customers and a fall tune-up offer loses most of that value if 40 percent of the messages land in spam. ICS sets up authentication on every Lead Engine domain, sends from the business's own domain, and keeps the list to people who gave their address during a real transaction, which is the definition of first-party data.

## In practice

A Brockton dental practice sent a recall email to 1,800 patients from a generic address with no DMARC record: 22 percent opened and 6 appointments were booked. After authenticating the domain, warming it for two weeks, and removing 300 dead addresses, the same offer reached 1,500 patients, 46 percent opened, and 31 booked. The difference was about $14,000 in production from a channel that cost nothing to send.
