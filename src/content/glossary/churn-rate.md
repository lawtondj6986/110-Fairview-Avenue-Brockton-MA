## What it means

Churn rate is the share of customers lost over a period, usually a year. For subscription or maintenance-plan businesses it is exact: cancellations divided by starting members. For everyone else it is defined by a lapse rule, such as a customer who has not returned within a normal buying cycle. It is the number that customer lifetime value depends on most.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Hanover HVAC company with 1,200 maintenance-plan members and 25 percent churn must find 300 new members every year just to stand still. Cutting churn to 15 percent saves 120 customers, and keeping one is far cheaper than buying one with ads. Lead Engine handles the follow-up cadence that reduces churn: service reminders, seasonal check-ins, and review requests that keep the relationship warm between jobs.

## In practice

A Quincy dental practice found that 28 percent of patients seen in one year did not return the next. Each lapsed patient represented about $650 a year in production. The practice set up automatic recall texts at six months and a short reactivation sequence at twelve. Churn fell to 19 percent within a year, which kept roughly 110 patients and about $71,000 of annual revenue in the building. No new patients were needed to produce that gain.
