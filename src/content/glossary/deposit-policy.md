## What it means

A deposit policy is the written rule for money collected before work starts: the amount or percentage, when it is due, what it is applied to, and the terms for refund or forfeiture. It covers both job deposits, such as a third down on a kitchen, and booking deposits, such as $50 to hold a Saturday appointment.

## Why it matters for a local business

A deposit does two jobs. It funds materials, and it filters out customers who were never serious. A policy that lives only in the owner's head gets applied unevenly and argued about later. JobSite booking can require the deposit at the moment of scheduling, and QuoteBrain can show the deposit amount on the quote itself, so nobody is surprised. In Florida markets, check county licensing rules with your attorney as well.

## In practice

A Hanover fence installer quotes $9,600 and collects a 30 percent deposit of $2,880 by text-to-pay when the customer approves. The policy states the deposit is refundable within three business days and that special-order materials are not refundable once ordered. Cancellations after approval drop from 9 a year to 2.
