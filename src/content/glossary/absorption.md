## What it means

Absorption measures demand: the square footage leased minus the square footage vacated in a market during a quarter or year. Positive absorption means a tightening market, negative absorption a loosening one. It is one of the three numbers, with vacancy and rent, that describe a market's direction.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore small-bay industrial has shown steady positive absorption for years, which is why a 3,000 square foot bay in Rockland or Avon leases quickly and why owner-users who wait for a bargain rarely find one. Small retail is more mixed and more block-by-block.

## In practice

A submarket with 2,000,000 square feet of industrial space sees 60,000 square feet leased and 20,000 vacated in a quarter: net absorption is 40,000 square feet, and the vacancy rate falls.
