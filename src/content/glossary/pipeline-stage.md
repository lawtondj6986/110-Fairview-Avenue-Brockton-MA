## What it means

A pipeline stage is one of the defined steps a sales opportunity moves through, from first contact to won or lost. The set of stages is the pipeline. Each opportunity sits in exactly one stage at a time, with a dollar value and a date it entered, so the business can count deals by stage, measure how long they linger, and see where they die.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Hanover general contractor carrying 40 open estimates in his head knows roughly what is pending and nothing about why jobs are slipping. Stages make the pattern visible. If 60 percent of lost deals die at "quote sent" after 10 days with no contact, the problem is follow-up, not pricing. ICS builds the pipeline into Lead Engine with automated nudges keyed to each stage: a text two days after a quote, a call task at seven, a last check at fourteen.

## In practice

A Marshfield deck builder set up seven stages and tracked a season. Of 140 qualified leads, 112 got estimates, 96 received quotes, and 41 signed, a 43 percent quote-to-close rate. The data showed quotes over $25,000 closed at 28 percent while those under $15,000 closed at 61 percent, and average time in "quote sent" before signing was 9 days. He added a financing option to the larger quotes and a day-9 follow-up call, and the big-job close rate rose to 39 percent the next season.
