## What it means

A CRM is the database and workflow engine for a business's relationships with prospects and customers. It stores contacts and history, assigns tasks, and, when connected to automation, sends follow-ups and reminders without a human remembering to. It turns a sales process from habit into a system.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The typical South Shore contractor keeps leads in a phone, a text thread, and a clipboard. Nothing is wrong with that until the fourth lead in a day is forgotten. The CRM is not bureaucracy; it is the memory that lets a two-truck company follow up like a company with a sales department.

## In practice

A Hanover landscaper's CRM records a quote sent March 3, sends an automated check-in March 6 and March 12, flags the lead for a personal call March 18, and logs the signed contract March 20. No one had to remember any of it.
