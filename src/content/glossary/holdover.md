## What it means

Holdover occurs when a tenant remains in possession after the lease term expires without a new agreement. The lease's holdover clause sets the consequences, typically a sharply higher month-to-month rent and exposure to damages, to push tenants to either renew or leave on time.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Construction delays on a new South Shore location are normal, and permitting delays are more normal still. A tenant who negotiates a modest holdover rate and a sixty-day cushion when signing the original lease saves themselves from paying double rent during the move.

## In practice

A tenant's lease ends June 30 at $6,000 a month; the new building is not ready until September. At a 200 percent holdover rate the two extra months cost $24,000 instead of $12,000.
