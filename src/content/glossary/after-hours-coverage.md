## What it means

After-hours coverage is the plan for handling inbound contacts when nobody is at the desk: evenings, weekends, holidays, and the lunch hour when the phone rings and everyone is on a job. It defines what happens to a call, a text, a web chat, and a form at those times, who or what responds, how fast, and what gets booked versus held for the morning.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Weymouth family with a leaking water heater at 7:30 p.m. calls three plumbers from the Google local pack. Two go to voicemail. The third answers, by AI receptionist or an instant text, confirms it can come in the morning, and books the slot. The other two never knew the call existed. ICS treats after-hours as the default state of a small business, not the exception, and configures Lead Engine so a missed call becomes a text within 10 seconds and the AI receptionist can book directly into the calendar.

## In practice

A Quincy plumbing and heating company logged its calls for a month: 310 inbound, 128 of them outside 8 to 5 on weekdays. Of those 128, the company had answered 9. ICS added text-back plus an AI receptionist for after-hours with next-morning booking and an emergency escalation to the on-call tech. The next month, 121 after-hours calls produced 84 conversations, 47 booked appointments, and 6 emergency dispatches averaging $720. The owner estimated the after-hours work alone at about $31,000 for the month.
