## What it means

Gift card liability is the total outstanding balance of gift cards and certificates a business has sold that customers have not yet used. On the balance sheet it is a debt: the business has the cash and owes the service. The portion that is never redeemed is called breakage.

## Why it matters for a local business

December gift card sales feel like profit and are not. A salon in Hanover that sells $30,000 of cards in December and spends the cash will be delivering $30,000 of services in January through March with no new money coming in for them. Owners who track it in a notebook also lose track of what is owed, which becomes a problem at sale or audit. JobSite booking can redeem card codes at checkout so the balance stays accurate.

## In practice

A Plymouth restaurant sells $42,000 in gift cards from Thanksgiving to New Year's. By March 31, $27,300 has been redeemed, leaving a $14,700 liability on the books. The owner sets aside 35 percent of holiday card cash in a separate account to cover food and labor cost on redemptions, and reviews the outstanding balance monthly.
