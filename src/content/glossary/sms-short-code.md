## What it means

An SMS short code is a five or six digit number that businesses lease to send and receive text messages at high volume. It is the kind of number in "text JOIN to 55512". Carriers review and approve each short code program in advance, which allows fast, large sends. The alternatives are registered local ten-digit numbers and toll-free numbers.

## Why it matters for a local business

The choice of number type affects cost, deliverability, and how the message feels. A short code suits a regional chain sending 100,000 promotional texts a month. A contractor in Rockland sending appointment reminders and quote follow-ups wants a local number the customer can reply to and call. Lead Engine texts from a registered local number for that reason, with consent captured and opt-outs honored automatically.

## In practice

A South Shore restaurant group with 3 locations prices a dedicated short code at roughly $500 to $1,000 a month plus setup. Its list is 2,800 subscribers and it sends 4 texts a month, 11,200 messages. A registered local number handles that volume for a fraction of the cost.
