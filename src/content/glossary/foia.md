## What it means

The Freedom of Information Act gives any person the right to request records from federal executive branch agencies, subject to nine exemptions. It is slower and more contested than most state laws but reaches records, from environmental enforcement to federal contracting, that no other method does.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

In ICS's practice, FOIA is a supplementary tool: an EPA or OSHA history on an industrial property, a federal contracting record for a counterparty. It is used when the question warrants the wait, and disclosed in the methodology either way.
