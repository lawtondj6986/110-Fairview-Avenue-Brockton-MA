## What it means

Average handle time is total time spent on customer calls divided by the number of calls handled. It counts talk time, hold time, and the wrap-up work after the call: notes, scheduling, sending the confirmation. It is a workload measure, not a quality score on its own.

## Why it matters for a local business

In a five-person shop the person answering the phone is usually also doing dispatch, billing, and parts runs. If each call eats seven minutes, forty calls a day is most of a full-time job, and the overflow goes to voicemail. Lead Engine handles the repeat questions and the missed-call text-back, and JobSite booking takes the routine appointments, so the calls that reach a person are the ones that need one.

## In practice

A Weymouth plumbing office logs 38 calls a day at an average of 6.5 minutes: about 4.1 hours of phone time. Half the calls are "where is my tech" and "can I book a cleaning." Moving those to an on-my-way text and a booking link cuts live calls to 21 a day. Handle time on the remaining calls goes up to 8 minutes, and total phone time drops to 2.8 hours.
