## What it means

A lease abstract distills a lease into its operative terms: parties, premises, dates, rent schedule, expense responsibilities, options, deposits, use restrictions, and critical deadlines. It is a working document for landlords managing a building, buyers evaluating one, and tenants who need to remember when their option notice is due.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Multi-tenant South Shore buildings often change hands with leases that nobody has summarized. The abstracts ICS prepares for an offering memorandum frequently surface terms the seller had forgotten, from a tenant's purchase option to an expense cap, that change the buyer's underwriting.
