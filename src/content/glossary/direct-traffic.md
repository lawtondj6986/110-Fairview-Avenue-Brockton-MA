## What it means

Direct traffic is the label analytics tools give to website visits that arrive without any information about where they came from. It includes people who type the address or use a bookmark. It also includes any click where the source was stripped or never passed along, which is why the number is usually larger and murkier than owners expect.

## Why it matters for a local business

A business that sends follow-up texts, review requests, and printed QR codes will see much of that activity land in direct unless the links are tagged. The owner then concludes the texts do nothing. Truck wraps, yard signs, and radio also show up as direct or branded search. Lead Engine tags its outbound links so follow-up traffic is reported as follow-up, not as a mystery.

## In practice

A Quincy auto shop sees 1,200 visits a month, 480 of them direct, or 40%. After tagging the links in its service-reminder texts and the QR code on invoices, direct drops to 290. The other 190 visits now appear as text and invoice traffic, and they book at 12%, the best rate on the site.
