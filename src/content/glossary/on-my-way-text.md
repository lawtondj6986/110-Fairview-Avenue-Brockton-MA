## What it means

An on-my-way text is a message sent when a technician is dispatched to a job. It tells the customer who is coming and the expected arrival time, often with a live tracking link. It is triggered by a status change on the dispatch board or a tap in the tech's phone, not typed by hand.

## Why it matters for a local business

The four-hour arrival window is the most hated part of hiring a service company. An on-my-way text replaces it with a real time, cuts the "where is my tech" calls that tie up the office, and reduces arrivals at empty houses. It also sets up the review request: customers who were kept informed leave better reviews. Lead Engine sends it from the same number the customer already texted, so replies land in one thread.

## In practice

A Brockton garage door company runs 22 jobs a day. Before the text, the office took 9 status calls a day and techs hit 2 not-home visits a week. After, status calls drop to 2 a day and not-home visits to about 2 a month. At roughly $95 in lost time per wasted trip, that alone saves about $570 a month.
