## What it means

Title V of the Massachusetts Environmental Code governs on-site sewage disposal. It requires inspection of septic systems when property is sold or when use changes in ways that increase flow, and it sets the design standards for repairs and new systems.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Sewer is the quiet dividing line in South Shore commercial real estate. A Pembroke or Halifax building on septic has a hard ceiling on how much restaurant seating or medical use it can support, set by the system's design flow. Ask about sewer before you ask about rent.

## In practice

A buyer of a Hanson building planning a 60-seat restaurant learns the septic system is designed for a 1,200 gallon per day office use. Upgrading it is a six-figure project, which reprices the building.
