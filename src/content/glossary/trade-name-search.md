## What it means

A trade name search is the process of identifying who uses a particular business name, and under what legal structure, by checking the available public registries. In Massachusetts that means the Secretary of the Commonwealth's corporate database for entity names, the business certificate files of individual city and town clerks for DBAs, and the federal and state trademark registers for protected marks. No single index covers all three.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Small businesses on the South Shore trade under names that rarely match their legal entities. "Hanover Auto Care" may be an LLC named for its owner, a sole proprietor with a Hanover DBA, or a Norwell corporation with a second location. Resolving the name to the entity is the first step in any due-diligence brief, and it also matters for a Market Lock client, who needs to know which competitor actually holds a category in a town.

## In practice

A Lead Engine client in Weymouth wants to trade as a name already appearing on three trucks in town. The Secretary of the Commonwealth database shows no entity by that name. The Weymouth clerk has a business certificate from 2021 for it, filed by a sole proprietor, still valid for two more years. The client picks a different name and avoids a dispute that would have cost more than the signage.
