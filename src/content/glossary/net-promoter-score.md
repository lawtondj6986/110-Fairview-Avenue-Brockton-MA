## What it means

Net Promoter Score is a customer loyalty measure based on a single survey question: on a scale of 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend us to a friend? Respondents are grouped into promoters (9 to 10), passives (7 to 8), and detractors (0 to 6), and the score is the percentage of promoters minus the percentage of detractors.

## Why it matters for a local business

NPS gives a local business an early warning before problems show up in public reviews. A Weymouth dental practice that asks every patient the question by text after a visit learns about a rude front-desk interaction in hours, not when it appears on Google. Proof Engine sends the survey to every customer and sends every customer the same public review link; detractors also get a call from the owner. Inviting only the happy ones to review publicly would be review gating, which violates Google's policy.

## In practice

A Hanover HVAC company surveyed 420 customers over a quarter: 68 percent promoters, 22 percent passives, 10 percent detractors, for an NPS of 58. The 42 detractors shared one theme: no arrival-time updates. The company added an automated "technician on the way" text. The next quarter's NPS was 71, detractors fell to 5 percent, and the public rating rose from 4.6 to 4.8 stars.
