## What it means

The Registry of Deeds is the public repository of recorded real estate documents for a county. In Massachusetts each county registry maintains an online index and images, making it possible to reconstruct a property's ownership, financing, and encumbrance history from a desk.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS lives in the Plymouth and Norfolk registries: they supply the comparable sales behind every broker opinion of value and the ownership chains behind every due diligence brief. A South Shore owner can look up their own building's history in ten minutes, and should.
