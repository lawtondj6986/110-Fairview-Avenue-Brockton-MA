## What it means

Counterparty risk is the exposure a party takes on by relying on another party's performance: a buyer who may not close, a tenant who may not pay, a partner who may not be who they appear. Commercial intelligence exists to measure and reduce it before commitments are made.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore deals are often done on reputation, and reputation is often right. When it is wrong, the cost is a dead deal or a bad tenant. ICS's brief is the inexpensive step that confirms the reputation before the signature relies on it.
