## What it means

An exclusive use clause prohibits the landlord from leasing other space in the property to a competing business, as defined in the clause. It protects a tenant's investment in a location from being undercut by a next-door competitor and is one of the most valuable provisions a retail tenant can win.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The same logic that makes a center exclusive valuable is what ICS applies to marketing with Market Lock: one business per category, per town. A South Shore operator who insists on exclusivity in their lease should insist on it from their marketing firm too.

## In practice

A salon in a Hanover plaza secures an exclusive for hair services. When a franchise barbershop wants the end cap a year later, the landlord must decline or face a 50 percent rent abatement under the clause.
