## What it means

Missed-call rate is the percentage of inbound calls to a business that are not answered by a person: they ring out, hit voicemail, or are abandoned while on hold. It is measured from the phone system's call log, total unanswered divided by total inbound, and is most useful broken out by hour of day and day of week.

## Why it matters for a local business

Roughly 80 percent of callers who reach voicemail at a small business do not leave a message, and most of them call the next name on the list. A Brockton HVAC company missing 30 percent of 200 monthly calls is handing 60 conversations a month to competitors on Route 24. ICS measures missed-call rate before any Lead Engine install because it sets the baseline. Fixing it is usually the fastest return in the whole system, faster than any new ad spend.

## In practice

A Quincy plumbing company pulled 90 days of call logs: 612 inbound calls, 214 missed, a 35 percent rate, concentrated between noon and 2 p.m. and after 4:30. ICS added missed-call text-back and an AI receptionist for after hours. In the next 90 days, 198 calls were still not answered live, but 171 got an instant text and 112 of those replied. Booked jobs from inbound calls rose from 148 to 203, with no change in ad spend.
