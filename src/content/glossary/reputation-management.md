## What it means

Reputation management is the system a business uses to generate honest reviews consistently, monitor what is said about it across Google, Yelp, Facebook, and industry sites, respond to every review, and fix the operational problems reviews reveal. Done well it is a feedback loop, not a public relations exercise.

## Why it matters for a local business

For a local business, reviews are the first thing a prospect checks, often before the website. A Hanover roofing company with 4.9 stars on 340 reviews wins the comparison against one with 4.7 on 40 before either answers the phone. Proof Engine handles the mechanics: a review request to every customer after every completed job, monitoring across platforms, draft responses for the owner to approve, and a monthly report. Every customer is asked and every customer gets the same link, because asking only happy customers is review gating and violates Google's rules.

## In practice

A Rockland auto shop had 61 Google reviews averaging 4.3, with the three most recent negative and unanswered. Over six months the shop asked every customer at pickup, replied to all reviews within a day, and fixed the scheduling problem the complaints kept naming. It reached 218 reviews at 4.7 stars, and calls from its profile rose 64 percent with no change to its ad budget.
