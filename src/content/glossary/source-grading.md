## What it means

Source grading assigns a two-part rating to every item of information: the historical reliability of its source and the independent credibility of the information itself. The Admiralty or NATO system, letters A through F and numbers 1 through 6, is the common standard, and it lets readers distinguish established fact from plausible report.
