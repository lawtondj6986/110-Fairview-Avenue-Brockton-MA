## What it means

A dispatch board is the scheduling screen a service business runs the day from. Rows are technicians or crews, columns are time, and each block is a job with a status: scheduled, en route, on site, complete. Unassigned work sits in a queue until someone drags it onto a row.

## Why it matters for a local business

Routing is where a field business makes or loses its margin. A tech who drives from Brockton to Plymouth, back to Rockland, then down to Kingston has burned two hours on Route 3 that nobody is paying for. A board that groups jobs by town and shows gaps makes those mistakes visible before the day starts. Bookings from JobSite and leads from Lead Engine should land on the board directly, not on a sticky note.

## In practice

A Taunton appliance repair shop with 3 techs averages 5.2 jobs per tech per day. After grouping jobs by zone on the board and filling gaps from the waitlist, the average rises to 6.1. At a $185 average ticket, that is about $500 a day in added revenue, or roughly $10,000 a month, from the same payroll.
