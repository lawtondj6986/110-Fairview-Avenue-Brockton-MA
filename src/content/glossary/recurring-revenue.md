## What it means

Recurring revenue is income that arrives on a regular schedule under an ongoing agreement. It is usually stated as monthly recurring revenue, or MRR, and tracked alongside churn, the share of that revenue lost each period. It is distinct from one-time project revenue and from informal repeat business.

## Why it matters for a local business

A contractor who starts every month at zero has to sell the whole month again. One who starts with $15,000 already contracted can cover payroll before the phone rings. Recurring revenue also makes ad spend easier to judge, because a new customer is worth years of payments, not one ticket. ICS systems are built to feed it: Lead Engine for renewals and recall, JobSite for member booking.

## In practice

A Plymouth pest control company has 240 quarterly customers at $115 a visit: $110,400 a year, or $9,200 a month. Annual churn is 18 percent, so it must add 43 customers a year just to stay flat. Cutting churn to 12 percent with renewal reminders keeps 14 more customers, worth about $6,400 a year, every year.
