## What it means

The State Ethics Commission is a five-member independent agency created by Chapter 268B. It interprets and enforces the conflict of interest law, Chapter 268A, and the financial disclosure law, Chapter 268B. It issues advisory opinions, runs the required training for public employees, receives Statements of Financial Interests, investigates complaints, and imposes civil penalties through public enforcement actions.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A commission enforcement record is a primary, public, dated source about a public official's conduct, which is far more reliable than a newspaper summary of it. ICS checks commission dispositions on every public figure in an opposition or vulnerability file and on any South Shore board member whose votes affected a client's property. The commission's confidential files, including open complaints, are not public, and ICS does not try to learn what is in them.

## In practice

A background brief on a Weymouth contractor turns up a 2019 disposition agreement: while a part-time town employee, he had a financial interest in a $38,000 town contract and paid a $5,000 civil penalty. The agreement is six pages, signed, and public. It is cited directly, with the admitted facts quoted, rather than relying on the one-paragraph news item that mentioned it.
