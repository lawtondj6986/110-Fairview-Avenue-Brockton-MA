## What it means

The Dover Amendment is a provision of Chapter 40A that exempts religious, nonprofit educational, and licensed childcare uses from local zoning prohibitions, allowing only reasonable dimensional regulation. It reflects a state policy that these uses should be able to locate throughout the Commonwealth.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Childcare is one of the twelve industries ICS serves, and the Dover Amendment is why a licensed daycare can often occupy a South Shore commercial space that the bylaw would otherwise reserve for retail or office. Parking and dimensional rules still apply, and the local permitting path should be confirmed before a lease is signed.
