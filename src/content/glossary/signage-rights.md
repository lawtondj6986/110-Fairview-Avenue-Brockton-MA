## What it means

Signage rights define a tenant's permission to identify its business on the property: fascia signs, pylon panels, window graphics, and temporary signage. They interact with the landlord's sign criteria, other tenants' rights, and municipal sign regulations, and they directly affect how visible a business is from the road.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Visibility on Route 18, Route 28, Route 53, or Route 139 is a large part of what a South Shore tenant is paying for. A lease that is silent on pylon placement, or that gives the landlord unfettered approval, can leave a tenant invisible from the road. We put the sign position and size in the lease.

## In practice

A tenant secures the top pylon panel on a Route 18 center and a fascia sign to the maximum size the Abington bylaw allows, both written into the lease. A competitor who later leases the end cap cannot displace them.
