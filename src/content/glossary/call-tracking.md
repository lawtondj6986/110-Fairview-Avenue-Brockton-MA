## What it means

Call tracking assigns distinct phone numbers to distinct marketing sources and routes them all to the business, logging each call's origin and outcome. For businesses where the phone is the primary lead channel, it is the foundation of attribution and of response-time measurement.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore service businesses live on the phone, so call tracking is in every ICS Lead Engine. It is also how the sub-sixty-second response guarantee is measured, because a guarantee that cannot be measured is just a sentence.
