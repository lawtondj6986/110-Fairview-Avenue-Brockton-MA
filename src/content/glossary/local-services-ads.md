## What it means

Local Services Ads (LSAs) are a Google ad format for home and professional service providers. They appear at the very top of search results with a Google Guaranteed or Google Screened badge, and the business pays per qualified lead rather than per click. Eligibility requires license, insurance, and background verification, which takes a few weeks.

## Why it matters for a local business

On the South Shore, LSAs now sit above both the paid ads and the local pack for trades like plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, and cleaning. A Weymouth plumber who is not running them has given the top of the page to competitors. Lead Engine routes LSA leads through the same missed-call text-back and follow-up as every other channel, and ICS tracks LSA cost per lead by town so Market Lock clients can see where the ads pay and where the local pack already covers them.

## In practice

A Brockton HVAC company turned on LSAs across twelve towns with a $1,500 monthly budget. Leads cost between $28 and $65 depending on the service, and after disputes the net was 41 valid leads at about $37 each. Nineteen booked, with an average ticket of $520, and two became system replacements over $12,000. The owner's rule now is to answer LSA calls within four rings, because responsiveness directly affects where the ad ranks.
