## What it means

An AI hallucination is an answer from an AI system that is wrong or invented but delivered as fact. It can be a made-up price, a warranty that does not exist, a wrong phone number, or a citation to a source that was never written. The system is not lying on purpose. It is filling a gap with plausible text.

## Why it matters for a local business

A chat agent that tells a Quincy customer you offer 24-hour emergency service when you close at 5 has created a promise you now have to break. ICS builds chat and phone agents to answer only from the business's own approved facts, to say "let me get someone for that" when the answer is missing, and to pass the conversation to a human. In medical, dental, and legal settings that limit is not optional.

## In practice

A Brockton HVAC company tests its chat agent with 50 real customer questions before launch. It gets 46 right, declines 3 correctly, and invents a $79 tune-up price on 1. The fix takes 10 minutes: add the real $129 price to the approved facts and require a handoff on any pricing question not listed.
