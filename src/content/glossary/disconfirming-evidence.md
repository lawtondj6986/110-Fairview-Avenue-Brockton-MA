## What it means

Disconfirming evidence is any fact that contradicts a hypothesis. In structured analysis, the analyst identifies in advance what evidence would be inconsistent with each working theory and then actively looks for it, because the theory that survives a genuine attempt to break it deserves more confidence than one that has merely gathered supporters. The method is borrowed from scientific practice and intelligence tradecraft.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Clients come to ICS with a story already in mind: the seller is hiding something, the opponent has a conflict, the competitor is failing. The job is to test it, not decorate it. Every Deep Dossier states what was checked that could have disproved the main finding and what came back. When the disconfirming search turns up nothing, confidence goes up and the client is told why. When it turns up something, the client learns it before the deal or the campaign does.

## In practice

A buyer believes a Plymouth seller is in financial distress because the property has sat for 14 months. If true, the Registry should show recent refinancing or liens and the courts should show collection suits. The search finds a mortgage paid off in 2022, no liens, no suits, and an LLC in good standing. The seller is not desperate; the price is simply high. The buyer's offer strategy changes accordingly.
