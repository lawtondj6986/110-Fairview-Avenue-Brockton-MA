## What it means

An end cap is the unit at either end of a shopping center's main building. Its corner position gives it exposure on two sides, more signage, and often the only drive-through opportunity in the center, making it the most desirable and most expensive inline position.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

End caps on South Shore centers go to quick-service restaurants, coffee, cellular stores, and medical urgent care, and they rarely sit vacant. A tenant who wants one should expect to compete and to pay for it; a tenant who does not need one should not.
