## What it means

The Fair Credit Reporting Act governs consumer reports used for eligibility decisions about individuals. Research that would be used to decide whether to employ, house, insure, or extend credit to a person falls under it, with legal duties that distinguish licensed consumer reporting agencies from intelligence practices. ICS's methods code treats that boundary as absolute.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore landlords sometimes ask ICS to check a residential tenant; employers ask about a candidate. The answer is a referral to a licensed screening provider and an explanation of why. Drawing the line publicly is part of what makes the rest of the practice credible.
