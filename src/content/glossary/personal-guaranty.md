## What it means

A personal guaranty makes an individual, usually the business owner, personally responsible for a company's obligations under a lease or loan. Landlords and lenders require it because a small corporation or LLC can be emptied or dissolved; the owner's personal assets cannot be so easily moved.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Every South Shore landlord asks; not every one needs the full term. We routinely negotiate caps and burn-offs for tenant clients and, on the landlord side, advise on which concessions are safe to give for which tenants. The guaranty is a term, not a formality.

## In practice

A tenant negotiates a guaranty capped at twelve months of rent, burning off entirely after 36 consecutive on-time payments. Three years in, the owner's personal exposure ends while the lease continues.
