## What it means

Chapter 268A is the Massachusetts conflict of interest law. It restricts public employees from participating in matters in which they, their family, or their business partners have a financial interest, from having financial interests in contracts with their own government, from receiving gifts tied to their position, and from using their office for private gain. It also requires written disclosures in specific situations, and those disclosures are public records.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Local land use runs through boards whose members have day jobs. A planning board member in Hanover who is also a real estate broker, or a Brockton councilor whose brother owns the paving company, has disclosure obligations. ICS checks whether disclosures were filed with the clerk and compares them against corporate and Registry records. The research is public-records work only. Whether a violation occurred is a legal judgment for the Ethics Commission, not a dossier.

## In practice

A due-diligence brief for a buyer of a Rockland site finds that the zoning board chair who voted on its special permit in 2021 owned, through an LLC, a parcel 400 feet away. No 268A disclosure is on file with the town clerk. The brief reports the facts and recommends the buyer's attorney assess any permit risk before the 60-day diligence period ends.
