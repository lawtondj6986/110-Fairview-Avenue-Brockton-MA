## What it means

Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 21E is the state's counterpart to the federal Superfund law. It makes a broad set of parties strictly liable for the cleanup of oil and hazardous material releases and creates the framework, the Massachusetts Contingency Plan, under which sites are assessed and remediated.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Because 21E liability can follow the deed, South Shore buyers of older industrial, automotive, and retail property should treat environmental assessment as non-negotiable. Sellers benefit too: a clean assessment in the data room removes a buyer's biggest reason to hesitate or retrade.

## In practice

A buyer purchases a former machine shop in Avon without an assessment. Two years later a lender's Phase I finds historical oil contamination. Under 21E the new owner is a responsible party regardless of who caused it.
