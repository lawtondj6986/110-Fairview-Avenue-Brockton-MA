## What it means

A reactivation campaign is a planned series of texts, emails, or calls to former customers who have not returned within their expected cycle. It aims to win back people who already know and trust the business. It is distinct from a nurture sequence, which works on new leads, and from a newsletter, which goes to everyone regardless of status.

## Why it matters for a local business

Past customers are the cheapest revenue a local business will ever find. They need no ad spend, no introduction, and no persuasion about quality. A Weymouth auto shop with 3,000 customers in its system and 1,100 who have not visited in 18 months is sitting on a list worth more than any ad campaign. Lead Engine runs reactivation automatically against the CRM: lapsed customers get a seasonal text with a booking link, and replies go to a person. Messages go only to customers who gave consent, with a clear opt-out.

## In practice

A Quincy dental practice identified 640 patients with no visit in 14 months. A three-message sequence (text, email, text) over three weeks offered a hygiene appointment before year-end benefits expired. 112 patients booked and 94 showed, at roughly $310 per visit, about $29,000 in production. The campaign cost under $200 to send, and 31 of those patients went on to schedule restorative work.
