## What it means

Breakeven occupancy is the share of a property that must be leased and paying for income to cover all operating costs and loan payments. It is a stress measure: the higher the breakeven, the less vacancy the owner can absorb before cash flow turns negative. Lenders use it alongside the debt service coverage ratio.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

For a small multi-tenant South Shore building, breakeven occupancy makes the risk concrete. A four-unit strip center with breakeven at 80 percent survives one vacancy; one with breakeven at 95 percent does not. We compute it for every income property a client considers.
