## What it means

A red team review assigns someone who did not write the brief to attack it: find the weakest finding, propose the innocent explanation, test whether the sources really say what the brief claims, and look for what was not searched. The reviewer writes a short dissent. The authors answer each point in writing, and the brief changes where the dissent holds. The reviewer works from the same public records, not new ones gathered by other means.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Researchers fall in love with a pattern. A brief that reads a Brockton developer's 3 LLC dissolutions as flight may be looking at ordinary tax planning. The client, who is about to reprice an offer or brief a lawyer, needs the strongest version of the other side first. ICS red-teams every Deep Dossier and any brief where a finding could cost the client more than the fee.

## In practice

A draft brief concludes a Weymouth seller "has a pattern of concealing environmental conditions," based on 2 prior sales with post-closing 21E disputes. The red team pulls both dockets: one suit was dismissed with the buyer paying costs, the other settled for $18,000 on a disclosure technicality. The finding is rewritten as "two post-closing disputes, one resolved for the seller," confidence lowered from high to moderate.
