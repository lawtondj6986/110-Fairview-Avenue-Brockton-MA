## What it means

Bottom line up front is a writing discipline borrowed from military and intelligence reporting: lead with the judgment, its confidence, and its implication, then support it. It respects the reader's time and forces the analyst to commit to a conclusion rather than hide behind a pile of facts.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

A South Shore buyer reading an ICS brief the night before a P&S deadline gets the answer in the first paragraph: proceed, proceed with conditions, or walk. The forty pages behind it are there to be checked, not required to be read.
