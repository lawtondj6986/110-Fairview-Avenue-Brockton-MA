## What it means

Sales cycle length is the average elapsed time between a lead's first contact and the moment the sale is won, measured in days. It varies widely by service: an emergency furnace repair closes in an hour, a dental implant case in weeks, a home addition in months. It is tracked per lead in a CRM and averaged by job type.

## Why it matters for a local business

A local business that knows its cycle plans cash flow and follow-up correctly. A Hanover remodeler with a 40-day cycle who stops following up after five days abandons leads at the exact point where competitors are still in the conversation. Lead Engine sets the follow-up cadence to the measured cycle, so a kitchen lead receives touches across six weeks and a plumbing repair lead receives three in two days. The pipeline view in the dashboard shows where leads are stalling relative to the normal cycle.

## In practice

A Plymouth window company found its average cycle was 31 days, but won deals averaged 19 days and lost deals averaged 44. Leads not closed within three weeks rarely closed at all. The company added a day-14 call from the owner and a day-21 last check-in text. Average cycle fell to 24 days, and the close rate on quotes over $8,000 rose from 29 to 37 percent, because fewer leads drifted past the point of no return.
