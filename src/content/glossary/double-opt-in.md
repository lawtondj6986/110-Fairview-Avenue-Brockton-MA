## What it means

Double opt-in is a two-step subscription process. The person enters an email address or phone number, then receives a confirmation message and must click a link or reply to be added to the list. Single opt-in skips the second step. The confirmation proves the contact belongs to the person who gave it and creates a timestamped record of consent.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Plymouth restaurant collecting phone numbers for a text club, or a Hanover dentist building a recall email list, is handling consent that regulators and carriers care about. Typos and fake entries from a tablet at the counter poison the list and get the sender flagged. Double opt-in cleans that at the door. ICS defaults to double opt-in for every SMS and email list in Lead Engine, stores the confirmation record with the contact, and honors opt-outs automatically.

## In practice

A Brockton pizzeria ran a text club from a counter sign for a year with single opt-in: 1,340 numbers, a 22 percent delivery failure rate, and three carrier filtering incidents that blocked an entire Friday promotion. Switching to double opt-in and purging unconfirmed numbers cut the list to 910. Delivery failures dropped under 2 percent, redemption on the weekly offer rose from 3 percent to 8 percent of the list, and Friday revenue on promotion nights climbed about $600.
