## What it means

Speed to lead is the elapsed time between a prospect raising their hand, by calling, filling out a form, or messaging, and the business making meaningful first contact. It is measured in minutes and it predicts conversion better than almost any other operational metric a local business controls.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore, the homeowner who requests three roofing quotes on a Sunday night usually hires whichever roofer answers first on Monday morning, and often the one who texted back Sunday night. ICS's Lead Engine exists because the math is that simple and that unforgiving.

## In practice

Two Brockton plumbers receive the same emergency inquiry at 9 p.m. One has an automated text reply with a booking link and a call at 7 a.m.; the other sees the voicemail at 10 a.m. The first one has the job.
