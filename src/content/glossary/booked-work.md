## What it means

Booked work is the end of the funnel: appointments scheduled, contracts signed, revenue committed. It is the measure ICS uses in proposals, guarantees, and monthly reporting because it is the measure that matters to an owner, and because every other metric is only useful insofar as it moves this one.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The tagline on this site, built by an operator, measured in booked jobs, is the standard: a South Shore client's dashboard leads with jobs booked this month, by source, against last month and last year. Everything else is below the fold.
