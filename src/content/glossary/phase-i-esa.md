## What it means

A Phase I environmental site assessment is a review of historical records, regulatory databases, aerial photographs, interviews, and a site walk by an environmental professional to identify recognized environmental conditions. It involves no sampling; its purpose is to decide whether sampling is needed.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Former gas stations, dry cleaners, auto body shops, and machine shops line the South Shore's older commercial corridors, and many have been reused several times. A Phase I on a Route 18 or Route 28 property is not paranoia; it is the standard of care, and it typically takes two to three weeks.

## In practice

A Phase I on a Brockton building flags a former dry cleaner tenant in the 1980s. A Phase II finds solvent in the soil below action levels. The buyer closes with a price reduction and a documented baseline.
