## What it means

Negative keywords are terms added to a paid search campaign to prevent ads from appearing when those words are in the query. They are the filter that keeps a campaign for "water heater installation" from showing to people searching "water heater installation jobs" or "how to install a water heater yourself."

## Why it matters for a local business

Local ad budgets are small and wasted clicks are expensive. A Brockton auto shop bidding on "brake repair" pays for clicks from mechanics looking for work and from people hunting for brake parts at a Route 28 supply store. A disciplined negative list usually cuts 15 to 30 percent of spend with no loss of real leads. ICS maintains a shared negative list across Lead Engine campaigns and adds the towns outside a client's Market Lock territory so the budget stays in the towns that pay for it.

## In practice

A Plymouth roofing company's first month of ads showed 212 clicks on queries including "roofing jobs," "roofing supply," "roof repair Cape Cod," and "free roof inspection." Those clicks cost $1,100 and produced zero quotes. After adding 140 negative keywords, the next month's spend fell to $2,400 from $3,500 while quote requests rose from 18 to 24, because the budget was no longer exhausted before real customers searched in the evening.
