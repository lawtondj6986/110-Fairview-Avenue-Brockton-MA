## What it means

Conversion rate measures the share of people who move from one stage to the next: visitors who become leads, leads who become quotes, quotes who become customers. Multiplying the stages gives the share of visitors who become revenue, and improving any one stage improves the whole.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS reports all three stages monthly because the South Shore businesses we work with typically have decent traffic and poor conversion. A JobSite build targets visitor-to-lead; a Lead Engine targets lead-to-booked. The scoreboard shows the numbers.
