## What it means

Internal linking is the practice of connecting pages on the same website to each other with links in the navigation, in the body text, and in footers. These links let a visitor move from a general page to a specific one, and they show search engines which pages exist, how they relate, and which ones the site treats as most important.

## Why it matters for a local business

A local site with separate pages for each service and each town lives or dies on internal links. A "roof repair in Hanover" page that nothing links to might as well not exist. On a JobSite build, each service page links to the towns it covers, each town page links back to the services, and both link to booking. That structure is what lets 40 pages work as one site instead of 40 islands.

## In practice

A Taunton pest control company adds 18 town pages but links to them only from the sitemap. After 60 days, 11 are not indexed. Adding a "towns we serve" section on the 5 service pages and cross-links between neighboring towns takes an hour. Within 30 days, all 18 are indexed and 7 are bringing in calls.
