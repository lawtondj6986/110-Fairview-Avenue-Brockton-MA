## What it means

A video testimonial is a recorded statement from an actual customer about their experience, used on a website, in ads, or on social media. The strong ones follow a simple arc: the problem the customer had, why they chose the business, and what happened. A phone camera, decent light, and clear audio are enough.

## Why it matters for a local business

Written reviews tell. Video shows a real person with a real face from a town the viewer knows. For high-ticket work such as a $40,000 kitchen or an implant case, that removes a lot of doubt. Most owners never ask because it feels awkward. Proof Engine builds the ask into job closeout, and Reels System cuts the footage into short clips for the site, the business profile, and social feeds.

## In practice

A Plymouth bath remodeler records 6 customer testimonials over 2 months, about 10 minutes each on site, with signed releases. A 45-second clip goes at the top of the quote page. The page's conversion rate moves from 3.2% to 4.9%. On 700 monthly visits, that is 12 more quote requests.
