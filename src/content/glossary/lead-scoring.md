## What it means

Lead scoring assigns a numeric value to each lead based on attributes and behavior that predict conversion and value. It lets a small team spend its limited follow-up time on the inquiries most likely to become good work.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS configures scoring per client around what actually predicts a booked job for that trade in those towns. For a South Shore contractor, a lead inside the service area with a defined job and a working phone number outranks everything else.
