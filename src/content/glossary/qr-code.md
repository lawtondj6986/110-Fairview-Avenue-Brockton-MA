## What it means

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that a phone camera can read. Scanning it opens a web address, which can be a page, a booking form, a review screen, a payment link, or a contact card. It is a bridge from something printed or physical to something online, with no typing required.

## Why it matters for a local business

Most local marketing is still physical: trucks, signs, invoices, mailers. A QR code makes those measurable and actionable. The mistakes are common. Codes that go to the home page, codes too small to scan from the sidewalk, codes with no tracking. In ICS setups, each printed code points to a single-purpose page and carries its own tag, so the owner can see that the yard signs in Hanover produced 9 quote requests.

## In practice

A Rockland fence company puts a QR code on 25 yard signs linking to its QuoteBrain calculator. Over 3 months the signs get 140 scans and 31 completed quotes, a 22% completion rate. Six become jobs averaging $6,500. The signs cost $400 to print.
