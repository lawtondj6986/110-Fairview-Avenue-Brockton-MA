## What it means

A broker opinion of value is a written estimate of a property's likely sale price, prepared by a broker using recent comparable sales, the property's income, and its condition and location. It is less formal than an appraisal and is intended to help an owner make a decision, not to satisfy a lender.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Comparable sales on the South Shore are thin and scattered, so a credible BOV depends on knowing which sales were arm's-length and which were family transfers or distressed. That knowledge is local and current or it is not worth much. Ours comes from working the market daily.

## In practice

An owner of a 6,000 square foot Abington retail building receives a BOV of $1,050,000 to $1,150,000 supported by four sales within three miles over the past eighteen months and the building's $78,000 NOI.
