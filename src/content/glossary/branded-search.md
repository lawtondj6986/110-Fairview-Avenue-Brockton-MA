## What it means

Branded search is any query that contains your business name or a close variant, such as "Rockland Heating reviews" or a misspelling of the company name. It is the opposite of a generic search like "furnace repair near me." Google Search Console and your Business Profile insights both report it separately, and the split between branded and generic is worth watching every month.

## Why it matters for a local business

A local business with strong branded search has done the hard part: people remember the name. On the South Shore that comes from lettered trucks on Route 3, yard signs in Hanover, and reviews that mention the owner by name. Proof Engine feeds that loop by turning finished jobs into reviews and short case studies, and the branded-search line in the dashboard shows whether the reputation work is reaching people who have not hired you yet.

## In practice

A Taunton auto body shop had about 90 branded searches a month. Over six months it collected 85 new Google reviews, placed a yard sign at every completed job with permission, and posted a short video of each repair. Branded searches rose to 260 a month. Because those searchers convert at roughly 40 percent compared with about 8 percent for generic terms, the shop booked more work without raising its ad budget at all.
