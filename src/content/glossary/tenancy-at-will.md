## What it means

A tenancy at will is an arrangement where a tenant occupies space with the landlord's consent but without a lease for a fixed term. It continues until one party gives proper notice. It can be written or oral, and it commonly arises when a lease expires and the tenant stays on with the landlord accepting rent.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

A lot of small commercial space in Brockton, Whitman, and Taunton runs this way: contractor bays, garages, small offices, storage yards. It suits a start-up testing a location. It is a problem at sale or refinance, because a buyer or lender gives little credit to income that can leave in a month, and the tenant has no protection if the new owner has other plans.

## In practice

An auto repair shop in Brockton has paid $3,000 a month for six years with no lease. The building sells, and the new owner serves a written notice on March 20 ending the tenancy April 30. The shop has about 40 days to move lifts and a compressor. A three-year lease signed earlier would have survived the sale.
