## What it means

A zero-click search is one where the searcher gets what they need from the results page and never visits a website. Featured snippets, knowledge panels, the local pack, AI-generated overviews, and Business Profile cards all answer questions directly. By most estimates more than half of all searches now end this way, and the share is higher on phones.

## Why it matters for a local business

For a local business the implication is simple: the Google Business Profile and the AI answer are the website for most searchers. A Weymouth dentist's hours, rating, photos, and booking link are read on the results page; the site is visited by a minority. ICS treats the profile, structured data, and answer-engine content as first-class parts of every JobSite and local SEO build, and the dashboard counts calls, direction requests, and bookings from the profile alongside site conversions.

## In practice

A Hanover HVAC company saw site visits fall 18 percent over a year and assumed its marketing was failing. Its Business Profile told a different story: profile views were up 55 percent, calls from the profile had risen from 90 to 160 a month, and direction requests were up 40 percent. Total contacts had grown by about a third. The visits had not disappeared; they had moved to the results page, where the company was now answering the question before the click.
