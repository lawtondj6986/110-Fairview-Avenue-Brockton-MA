## What it means

Usable square feet is the area inside a tenant's own walls. Rentable square feet adds a proportional share of the building's common areas. Rent is quoted and paid on rentable; the tenant lives in usable. The ratio between them is the load factor.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The distinction catches South Shore tenants moving from a standalone storefront into a professional building in Braintree or Quincy, where a quoted 2,000 square feet turns out to be 1,700 of actual office. Ask for both numbers and the measurement standard before comparing rents.

## In practice

Two offices both quoted at $26 per rentable square foot: one at 1,200 rentable with 1,080 usable, one at 1,200 rentable with 1,000 usable. The effective rent on space you can furnish is $28.90 versus $31.20.
