## What it means

Attribution is the method of assigning credit for a conversion to the marketing activities that preceded it. It ranges from simple last-touch rules to multi-touch models. For a small business the goal is practical: know roughly which channels produce customers and which produce noise.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS dashboards show leads and booked jobs by source for every South Shore client, which is how budget moves from the channel that feels good to the channel that books work. The reporting is monthly and the conversation about it is direct.
