## What it means

The Federal Election Commission publishes itemized contribution and disbursement records for federal campaigns, parties, and political committees. Searchable by contributor and committee, it is the federal counterpart to state records like OCPF and a standard source in political and relationship research.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

A Massachusetts candidate or public figure's complete giving history lives in two places, FEC and OCPF, and a thorough vulnerability study pulls both. ICS does so under its published methods code and reports what the public record shows, nothing more.
