## What it means

The SBA 7(a) is the agency's primary loan program: a bank loan with a partial SBA guarantee that lets lenders finance small businesses they otherwise could not. Proceeds can fund real estate, equipment, inventory, working capital, or the purchase of a business, which makes it the natural choice for an acquisition that includes a building.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore business acquisitions where the seller owns the building, such as a garage, a restaurant, or a small manufacturer, are frequently financed with a 7(a) that covers both. The combination closes in one transaction and gives the buyer the location along with the customers.

## In practice

A buyer acquires a Middleborough auto repair business and its building for $1,800,000 total with a single 7(a) loan at 90 percent, folding working capital and equipment into one 25-year note.
