## What it means

Flex industrial describes buildings designed to accommodate a range of uses, typically with clear height, loading, and a front-office component, in bays from a couple of thousand square feet up. Its adaptability makes it the backbone of small-business real estate in suburban markets.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

From Avon's industrial park to Rockland's and Hanover's older complexes, flex is the South Shore's most contested product. A 4,000 square foot bay with a drive-in door and an office will often have multiple owner-user buyers before it is formally listed.
