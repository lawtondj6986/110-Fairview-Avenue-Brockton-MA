## What it means

A membership plan charges customers a recurring monthly or annual fee in exchange for a defined package: included services, a standing discount, priority scheduling, or some mix. It is a subscription for a local business. The key numbers are member count, monthly fee, churn, and what members spend beyond the fee.

## Why it matters for a local business

Members come back more often, spend more per year, and rarely shop around. For practices with many uninsured patients, a plan removes the price uncertainty that keeps people from booking. The hard parts are operational: signing people up at checkout, billing cards reliably, and catching failed payments. Lead Engine handles the enrollment offer and renewal messages, and JobSite gives members their own booking path.

## In practice

A Brockton dental practice offers a $32 a month plan covering two cleanings, exams, and X-rays, with 15 percent off treatment. It enrolls 180 uninsured patients: $5,760 a month, or $69,120 a year. Members accept treatment at 64 percent against 41 percent for uninsured non-members, adding about $58,000 a year in treatment revenue.
