## What it means

Proof Engine is the name ICS uses for the automated workflow that converts completed work into marketing evidence: reviews, imagery, and localized content. It is built on the observation that local businesses generate proof every day and publish almost none of it.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

A South Shore contractor finishes three jobs a week and tells no one. With the Proof Engine, each one produces a review request, a photo set, and a town-tagged post, which feeds both local SEO and the AI systems that increasingly recommend based on specific, recent evidence.
