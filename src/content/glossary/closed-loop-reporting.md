## What it means

Closed-loop reporting ties each marketing touch to its final result: did the lead book, did the job close, and what was it worth. Open-loop reporting stops at the lead. Closed-loop follows it through the CRM to revenue, so each channel can be judged on dollars rather than clicks or form fills.

## Why it matters for a local business

Without the loop, a Plymouth contractor sees that Facebook produced 40 leads and Google produced 25 and concludes Facebook is winning. With the loop, he sees that Facebook leads closed at 10 percent for $900 jobs and Google leads closed at 35 percent for $4,200 jobs. Lead Engine attaches a source to every call and form, and the dashboard reports revenue by channel, not just lead count. That is what lets a business cut a losing channel with confidence instead of a hunch.

## In practice

A Rockland kitchen remodeler spent $3,000 a month split evenly between two channels. After six months of logging every quote as won or lost, the numbers showed one channel produced $96,000 in closed work and the other $14,000. She moved the full budget to the winner, cut cost per closed job from $410 to $190, and for the first time knew exactly what her marketing bought.
