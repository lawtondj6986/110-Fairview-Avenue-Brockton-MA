## What it means

A recall list is the set of existing customers whose next service is coming due based on their last visit: a six-month dental cleaning, an annual eye exam, a 5,000-mile oil change, a yearly furnace tune-up. Working the list means contacting each one on schedule until they book.

## Why it matters for a local business

Recall is the cheapest revenue a practice or shop has. The customer already knows you, and there is no ad cost. Yet most offices work the list only when the schedule looks thin, by phone, during business hours when patients are at work. Lead Engine runs recall as an automatic sequence by text and email with a JobSite booking link, so the list gets worked every day without anyone picking up the phone. Patient messages should stay limited to scheduling details.

## In practice

A Weymouth dental office has 1,900 active patients and 420 overdue for a cleaning. A four-message recall sequence books 118 of them in 60 days, a 28 percent response. At $210 per hygiene visit that is $24,780, before any treatment found during those visits.
