## What it means

A lien search surveys the public record for encumbrances and claims against a property or a party. It is both a property question, what debts follow this parcel, and a counterparty question, what debts follow this person, and the second is often more informative than the first.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The Plymouth County and Norfolk County Registries of Deeds are where most South Shore liens live, and they are searchable online. ICS pairs a Registry search with court and agency records so a buyer learns about the seller's judgment lien before the P&S, not from the title examiner a week before closing.
