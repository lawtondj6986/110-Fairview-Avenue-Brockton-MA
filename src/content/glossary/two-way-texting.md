## What it means

Two-way texting is business messaging in which customers can reply to your texts and start conversations themselves, rather than receiving one-way blasts. The business number is text-enabled, messages land in a shared inbox tied to the CRM, and a person or an automated assistant responds. It is the channel most customers under 60 now prefer for scheduling.

## Why it matters for a local business

Phone tag loses jobs. A homeowner in Hanover who cannot take a call at work will answer a text during a meeting. Two-way texting is the backbone of Lead Engine: missed-call text-back opens the conversation, booking links close it, reminders keep the appointment, and review requests follow. All of it runs on the business's existing number with consent captured and opt-outs honored, which keeps the program inside TCPA rules.

## In practice

A Rockland plumbing company text-enabled its main line and routed the inbox to the office manager by day and an AI assistant after hours. In the first month it handled 380 text conversations, 140 of them started by customers. Booking by text took an average of 6 minutes versus two calls and 40 minutes of phone tag before. The company booked 52 jobs by text that month, 19 of them between 6 p.m. and 8 a.m. when no one would have answered the phone.
