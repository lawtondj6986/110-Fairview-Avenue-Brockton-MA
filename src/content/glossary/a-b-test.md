## What it means

An A/B test splits traffic between two variants that differ in one respect and compares a measured outcome, such as leads per visitor. It replaces opinion with evidence, within the limits of how much traffic a page receives.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS runs tests on client sites where traffic supports them and otherwise applies what has already been learned across South Shore trades and practices. The scoreboard reports what changed and what it did; opinions do not appear in it.
