## What it means

Net proceeds are the cash a seller receives at closing after every deduction on the settlement statement. It is the number that matters for planning a move, a retirement, or a 1031 exchange, and it is often smaller than sellers expect because commission and payoff are easy to underestimate.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore sellers call with a price in mind and no net in mind. Running the net first, with the actual Massachusetts excise and a real commission, tends to change the conversation about timing and about whether a sale-leaseback or a refinance serves them better.

## In practice

Sale price $1,100,000; payoff $420,000; commission at 5 percent $55,000; excise $5,016; attorney and recording $3,500; tax proration credit to buyer $2,800. Net proceeds: about $613,700 before income tax.
