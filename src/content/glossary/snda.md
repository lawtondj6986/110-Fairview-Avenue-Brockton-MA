## What it means

A subordination, non-disturbance, and attornment agreement is a three-party document among landlord, tenant, and lender. The tenant agrees its lease is junior to the mortgage, the lender agrees to honor the lease if it takes the building, and the tenant agrees to recognize the lender or a buyer at foreclosure as its new landlord.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Small South Shore landlords often have a local bank mortgage and have never been asked for an SNDA. The bank will usually produce its form. For a tenant spending real money on a fit-out in a single-owner building, it is cheap insurance against the one scenario that could end the lease through no fault of theirs.

## In practice

A medical practice signing a ten-year lease and spending $200,000 on build-out in a Braintree building obtains an SNDA from the owner's lender. If the owner later defaults, the practice's lease survives the foreclosure.
