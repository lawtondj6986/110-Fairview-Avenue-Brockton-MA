## What it means

A conflict wall is the set of policies and practices that prevent confidential information from one engagement from being used in or leaking to another, and that prevent a firm from working both sides of a matter. In large firms it is a structure; in a small firm it is a refusal.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The South Shore is small, and ICS sometimes knows both parties to a deal. The policy is simple: represent one, research for one, never both, and say so the moment it is apparent. Discretion in both directions is how a firm here keeps its clients.
