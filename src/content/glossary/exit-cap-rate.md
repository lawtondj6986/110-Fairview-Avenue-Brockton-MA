## What it means

The exit cap rate is the capitalization rate an investor assumes will apply when the property is sold at the end of the holding period. It converts the projected final-year NOI into a sale price, and because that price dominates a multi-year return calculation, the exit assumption often matters more than any other input.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

When a seller's broker presents a projected return on a South Shore building, the exit cap rate is the number to check first. We underwrite exits at today's cap rate plus a margin, and we show clients what happens to the return if the exit is a point worse than hoped.
