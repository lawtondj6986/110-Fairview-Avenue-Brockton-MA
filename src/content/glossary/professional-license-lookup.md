## What it means

A professional license lookup is a search of the public register maintained by a licensing board to confirm that an individual or business holds a required license, that it is current, and whether it carries conditions, complaints, or disciplinary history. In Massachusetts most trades and professions are licensed through boards under the Division of Occupational Licensure, with separate registers for contractors, real estate licensees, electricians, plumbers, and dozens of other occupations.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Licenses are the public credential a counterparty claims, and the register is the only place to test the claim. ICS verifies every license referenced in a property or vendor brief: the general contractor's CSL and HIC, the broker's real estate license, the engineer's stamp. Discipline records are primary sources about conduct. The lookup is lawful and public; its use is limited to commercial due diligence and public-interest research, never hiring or tenant screening, which sit on the other side of the FCRA line.

## In practice

A Plymouth owner is about to sign a $220,000 renovation contract. The Division of Occupational Licensure register shows the contractor's CSL expired 14 months ago, and the HIC registration carries a 2024 complaint resolved with restitution. The owner requires proof of renewal before signing and adds a payment schedule tied to inspections. The lookup took ten minutes and shaped a six-figure contract.
