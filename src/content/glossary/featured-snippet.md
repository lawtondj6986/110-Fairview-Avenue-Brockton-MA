## What it means

A featured snippet is a block at the top of a Google results page that quotes a short answer from a web page, with a link to the source. It appears for question-style queries: how much, how long, what is, which is better. It is sometimes called position zero because it sits above the ordinary results.

## Why it matters for a local business

South Shore homeowners ask Google questions before they call anyone: "how much does a mini-split cost in Massachusetts," "how long does a roof last near the ocean." A local business that owns those snippets is the first name a buyer sees, weeks before they are ready to hire. ICS writes JobSite service pages with question headings and short direct answers for this reason. The same structure feeds AI assistants that summarize answers, so one piece of writing does two jobs.

## In practice

A Plymouth HVAC company published a page answering "how much does a heat pump cost in Massachusetts" with a 50-word answer, a price range of $4,500 to $18,000, and a table of state rebates. It won the snippet in about seven weeks. The page drew 1,400 visits a month, and 3 percent of visitors requested a quote: about 40 quote requests a month from a page that took one afternoon to write.
