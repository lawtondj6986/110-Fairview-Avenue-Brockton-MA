## What it means

The Google Business Profile is the listing Google displays for a business in Maps and in local search results. It is the most important single asset in local visibility, and it is where reviews live. Its completeness and activity are ranking signals, and its content increasingly feeds AI-generated answers.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS audits the profile of every prospective client before a first call, and the result is usually the same: claimed, half-filled, last photo two years old, reviews unanswered. Fixing it is part of every build and often produces the first measurable lift before the website is even live.

## In practice

A Rockland auto shop's profile goes from 14 reviews and no services listed to 70 reviews, a full service list, weekly photos, and answered questions. Calls from the map listing double in ninety days.
