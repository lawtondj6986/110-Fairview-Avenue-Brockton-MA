## What it means

A no-show fee is a charge for an appointment the customer did not attend and did not cancel within the stated notice period, commonly 24 or 48 hours. It is sometimes taken from a card on file or from a booking deposit. It compensates for a slot that could have been sold to someone else.

## Why it matters for a local business

An empty chair is the most expensive thing in an appointment business. A Quincy dental practice with a 10 percent no-show rate on 30 daily appointments loses three slots a day. The fee only holds up if the policy was shown at booking and the customer acknowledged it. JobSite booking presents the policy before confirmation, and Lead Engine sends the reminders and the reschedule link that keep the fee from being needed.

## In practice

A Fort Lauderdale med spa books 140 appointments a week with 17 no-shows, about 12 percent. It adds a $50 no-show fee, a card on file, and two reminder texts. No-shows fall to 6 a week. At a $160 average visit, the 11 recovered appointments are worth about $1,760 a week, and the fee itself is collected only 3 or 4 times.
