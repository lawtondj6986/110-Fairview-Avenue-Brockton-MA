## What it means

Investment property is held to produce rental income and capital growth. Its value is driven by the income it generates and the risk of that income, which is why cap rates, lease terms, and tenant credit dominate the analysis. The owner is a landlord, not an occupant.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore investors favor mixed-use downtown buildings, small strip centers, and multi-bay industrial. Returns are modest compared with riskier markets, but the tenants are real local businesses and the buildings hold value. ICS represents both buyers and sellers of these assets and runs the numbers from either chair.

## In practice

A local investor buys a four-bay Avon industrial building for $1,600,000 at a 7.4 percent cap rate with four established tenants on staggered leases, financed at 70 percent with a regional bank.
