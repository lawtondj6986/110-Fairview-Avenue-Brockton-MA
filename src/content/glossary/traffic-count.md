## What it means

Traffic count, usually reported as annual average daily traffic, measures how many vehicles pass a location in a typical day. It is a standard input for retail site selection and a frequent line on commercial listings, and it is only meaningful alongside access, speed, and the type of business.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Route 18 through Abington and Weymouth, Route 28 through Brockton, Route 53 through Hanover and Norwell, and Route 139 through Rockland and Marshfield carry the South Shore's heaviest commercial traffic. We pull the actual state counts for a client's candidate sites rather than quoting the listing.

## In practice

A listing cites 28,000 vehicles per day. The state data shows that count is from a point two miles north at an interchange; the count at the property is 16,000. The buyer prices the building accordingly.
