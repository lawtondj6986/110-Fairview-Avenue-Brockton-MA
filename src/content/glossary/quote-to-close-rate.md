## What it means

Quote-to-close rate is the number of quotes that turn into booked jobs divided by the total quotes issued over a period. It isolates the selling step from lead generation. A business can have excellent lead flow and still starve if quotes go out slowly or are never followed up after they are sent.

## Why it matters for a local business

For most South Shore trades, the quote is where the money is decided, and it is the step owners track least. A Rockland remodeler who sends 20 quotes a month at $18,000 each and closes 25 percent books $90,000. Moving to 35 percent with the same quotes adds $36,000 a month, no new leads required. QuoteBrain gets the first number to the customer in minutes, and Lead Engine runs the follow-up cadence that catches quotes drifting into silence.

## In practice

A Plymouth deck builder tracked 94 quotes over a season: 24 closed, a 26 percent rate. Average time to deliver a quote was 4 days, and there was no follow-up after sending. The next season he delivered quotes within 24 hours using a template and sent three automated follow-ups over ten days. Close rate rose to 38 percent on 98 quotes: 13 more decks at about $14,000 each, roughly $180,000 in added revenue.
