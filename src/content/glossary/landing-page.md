## What it means

A landing page is a standalone web page designed for a single conversion goal, usually the destination for a specific ad, email, or campaign. It strips away navigation and competing messages so the visitor's only decision is whether to take the one action the page asks for.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS builds town-and-service landing pages for South Shore clients: emergency plumbing in Weymouth, roof replacement in Marshfield. They serve paid campaigns and rank organically, and each carries the instant-quote tool and the review proof that make the visitor act.
