## What it means

Booked rate is appointments scheduled divided by qualified inbound leads. It sits between lead volume and show rate in the funnel: a lead arrives, it gets booked or it does not, and then the customer shows up or does not. It measures the front desk and the follow-up system, not the marketing.

## Why it matters for a local business

Most owners buy more leads when the real problem is that half the leads they already pay for never reach the calendar. A contractor in Hanover paying $60 a lead with a 40 percent booked rate is paying $150 per appointment. Lead Engine attacks that number directly with missed-call text-back and follow-up, and JobSite gives the customer a way to book at 9 p.m. without waiting for a callback.

## In practice

A Rockland HVAC company gets 150 leads a month and books 63, a 42 percent booked rate. After adding text-back and a booking link, it books 87 of the same 150, or 58 percent. At a $420 average ticket and an 85 percent show rate, those 24 extra bookings are worth about $8,500 a month with no added ad spend.
