## What it means

Text-to-pay is a billing method where the business sends an invoice or deposit request as a text message containing a secure link. The customer taps, sees the amount, pays by card or bank transfer, and receives a receipt. The payment posts to the business's accounting or CRM system automatically. It replaces the paper invoice, the emailed PDF nobody opens, and the "I'll mail a check" that arrives three weeks later.

## Why it matters for a local business

Collection time is cash flow. A Hanover plumber finishing a $900 repair at 3 p.m. can text the invoice from the driveway and have the money before he reaches the next job. A Brockton dental office can collect a $200 appointment deposit the moment a new patient books, which cuts no-shows sharply. ICS wires text-to-pay into Lead Engine so the deposit request fires when the appointment is confirmed and the final invoice fires when the job is marked complete.

## In practice

A Weymouth electrical contractor averaged 27 days from invoice to payment with emailed PDFs and about 8 percent of invoices going past 60 days. He switched to text-to-pay for every residential job under $5,000. Median time to payment fell to 1 day; 71 percent of invoices were paid within four hours of the text. Past-60-day receivables dropped to under 1 percent, and the office stopped spending roughly five hours a week on collection calls.
