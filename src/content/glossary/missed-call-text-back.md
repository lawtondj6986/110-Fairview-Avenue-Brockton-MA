## What it means

Missed call text-back is an automation that responds instantly by text message to any inbound call the business fails to answer. It converts the most common lead-loss event in a small business, the unanswered phone, into a live conversation.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Every South Shore contractor misses calls because they are working. The caller moves to the next name. A text within ten seconds keeps them, and ICS includes it in every Lead Engine because it is the cheapest lead recovery in the system.

## In practice

A Marshfield electrician misses eleven calls in a week while on jobs. With text-back, eight callers reply, five book, and the owner never changed how she works.
