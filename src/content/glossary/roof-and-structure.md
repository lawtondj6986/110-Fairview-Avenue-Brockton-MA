## What it means

Roof and structure clauses allocate the cost of the building's most expensive components. Landlord-friendly leases push them to tenants through broad maintenance definitions or CAM pass-throughs; tenant-friendly leases reserve them to the landlord. The difference can be a six-figure surprise in year three.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The South Shore's commercial stock is old, and flat roofs on 1970s and 1980s buildings are reaching the end of their lives across the region. A tenant signing a five-year lease on a building with a twenty-year-old roof should know exactly who pays when it fails, because it will.

## In practice

A 6,000 square foot single-tenant lease passes all maintenance to the tenant. The roof fails in year two; replacement is $78,000. The tenant's lawyer finds the clause excludes nothing. The next lease that tenant signs reserves roof and structure to the landlord.
