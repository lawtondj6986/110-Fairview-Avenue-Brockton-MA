## What it means

A letter of intent sets out the deal in plain terms so both sides agree on the economics before anyone pays for a full contract. It is short, usually two or three pages, and it frames the negotiation that the lawyers then formalize.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore commercial deals under $3M start with an LOI drafted by the broker. The fastest way to lose a building here is to send a vague LOI and let a cleaner one arrive from another buyer while yours is being clarified. Settle the real terms first.

## In practice

Buyer offers $1,150,000, 45-day due diligence, 60-day financing contingency, closing 30 days after contingencies, seller to deliver a clean Phase I. Signed by both, the LOI becomes the outline for the purchase and sale agreement.
