## What it means

Net operating income is what a property earns after the costs of running it and before the costs of financing it. It is the numerator in a cap rate and the income side of a debt service coverage ratio. Two buyers can disagree about price and agree completely about NOI; more often, the disagreement about price is really a disagreement about NOI.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Massachusetts property taxes, snow removal, and insurance run higher than national averages, so South Shore NOI is often thinner than an out-of-state investor expects. A credible local NOI uses the town's actual tax rate and a real snow budget, not a national rule of thumb.

## In practice

Gross rent $120,000, vacancy allowance $6,000, operating expenses $38,000: NOI is $76,000. At a 7 percent cap rate, that building is worth about $1,086,000.
