## What it means

Dual agency arises when the same brokerage, or the same agent, represents both the seller and the buyer in a transaction. Massachusetts permits it only with written consent, and the dual agent becomes a neutral facilitator rather than an advocate for either party.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

In a market as relational as the South Shore, listing brokers frequently know the likely buyer. ICS discloses the relationship in writing the moment it arises and offers both parties the choice, because a deal where one side later feels unrepresented is not a good deal for anyone.

## In practice

ICS lists a Rockland industrial condo and a longtime client wants to buy it. Both sign the Massachusetts dual agency consent; ICS facilitates the transaction without advising either side on price.
