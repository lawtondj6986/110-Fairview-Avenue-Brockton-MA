## What it means

Google Analytics 4, usually shortened to GA4, is Google's free tool for measuring website and app activity. It records each interaction as an event: a page view, a scroll, a click, a form submission. The business marks its important events as key events, and GA4 reports how many happened and which traffic sources produced them.

## Why it matters for a local business

Out of the box, GA4 tells a local owner very little. It counts visits, not leads. The setup work is what makes it useful: marking phone taps and form submissions as key events, tagging campaign links, and filtering out the owner's own visits. On JobSite builds, those events are configured at launch and fed into a plain dashboard, so the owner sees leads by source without opening GA4 at all.

## In practice

A Brockton landscaper's GA4 shows 1,800 sessions last month and nothing else useful. After 2 hours of setup, the same month reads differently: 41 phone taps, 17 quote forms, and 9 bookings. Organic search produced 38 of those 67 actions. The $400 a month display ad produced 2, and gets cut.
