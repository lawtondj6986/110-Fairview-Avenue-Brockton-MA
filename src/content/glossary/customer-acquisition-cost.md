## What it means

Customer acquisition cost is total sales and marketing spend over a period divided by the number of new customers acquired in it. It is the denominator in the most important ratio in a growth business: lifetime value to acquisition cost.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

When ICS scopes a system for a South Shore business, the written outcome is framed in these terms: what a customer is worth, what one costs to acquire today, and what the system should move. That is how a $12,000 build is judged against the jobs it books, not the pages it adds.
