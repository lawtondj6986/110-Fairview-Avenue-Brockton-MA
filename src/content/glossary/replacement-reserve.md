## What it means

A replacement reserve is a non-cash expense included in underwriting to account for the periodic replacement of capital items such as roofs, HVAC, parking lots, and major systems. Lenders require it in their analysis and sometimes require it to be funded; buyers include it to avoid overpaying for income that will be consumed by inevitable capital costs.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore's aging commercial stock, a realistic reserve is not a formality. A 10,000 square foot building with a twenty-year-old roof and original rooftop units should carry a reserve toward the high end, and a buyer who omits it is pricing the building as if it were new.
