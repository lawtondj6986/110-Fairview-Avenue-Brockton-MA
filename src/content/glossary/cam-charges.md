## What it means

Common area maintenance charges reimburse the landlord for the cost of running the parts of a property every tenant uses. They are estimated at the start of the year, billed monthly, and trued up against actual costs afterward, which is where disputes happen.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Snow is the South Shore CAM wildcard. A bad winter can add dollars per square foot to a strip center's reconciliation. A lease that caps controllable costs but leaves snow uncapped is normal; a lease with no cap at all is worth a conversation.

## In practice

A tenant with 2,000 of a center's 20,000 square feet pays 10 percent of CAM. If the center's CAM is $90,000, the tenant's share is $9,000, or $4.50 per square foot.
