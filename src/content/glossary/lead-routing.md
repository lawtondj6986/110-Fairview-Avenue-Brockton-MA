## What it means

Lead routing is the automated assignment of inbound leads to the right person based on rules: service, location, source, deal size, time, or availability. It replaces a shared inbox or a single phone with a defined path, so each lead has an owner from the first minute and nothing sits unclaimed overnight.

## Why it matters for a local business

Speed decides local jobs. A homeowner in Marshfield who submits three quote requests hires the first company that calls back, and that is often within 15 minutes. Routing inside Lead Engine sends the lead to the estimator covering that town by text and email, logs it in the CRM, and escalates to the owner if it is untouched. For businesses with two or three crews, it also keeps territory and specialty straight: the commercial estimator does not get the residential leads.

## In practice

A Quincy electrical company with four electricians had leads going to one office email checked twice a day. Average first response was 5.5 hours and 31 percent of leads never got a reply. After routing by town and specialty with a 10-minute escalation, median response dropped to 7 minutes and the no-reply rate to 4 percent. Booked jobs rose 38 percent on the same lead volume and the same ad spend.
