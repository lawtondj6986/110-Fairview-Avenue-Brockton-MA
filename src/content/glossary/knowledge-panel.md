## What it means

A knowledge panel is the box of facts a search engine displays next to the results when someone searches for a specific business, person, or organization. For a local business it typically shows the name, address, hours, phone number, photos, reviews, and a directions button. The search engine assembles it automatically from the business profile and other sources it trusts.

## Why it matters for a local business

When someone searches your business name, the panel is your storefront. Many customers call, get directions, or read reviews from it and never visit the website. Wrong hours there cost real visits. Consistent name, address, and phone details across the web make the panel more reliable, and a steady flow of recent reviews from Lead Engine's review requests keeps the star rating that appears in it current.

## In practice

A Quincy restaurant's panel shows it closed Mondays, which stopped being true a year ago. The business name gets about 2,400 searches a month, roughly 340 of them on Mondays. Fixing the hours takes 5 minutes. Monday covers rise from an average of 38 to 52 over the next month.
