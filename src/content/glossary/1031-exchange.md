## What it means

Section 1031 of the tax code lets an investor sell real property held for business or investment and reinvest in like-kind real property without paying capital gains tax at the time of sale. The gain is deferred into the new property's basis rather than forgiven.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Many South Shore owners who built a business in one building want to trade into a larger one without a tax bill in the middle. The 45-day identification clock is the hard part in a thin market, which is why serious exchangers start looking for the replacement before the sale closes. ## In practice

An owner sells a $900,000 Rockland building with $400,000 of gain and buys a $1,400,000 building in Hanover through a qualified intermediary within the deadlines. The gain rides along into the new property instead of being taxed now.
