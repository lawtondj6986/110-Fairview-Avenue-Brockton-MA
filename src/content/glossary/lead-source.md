## What it means

Lead source is the record of where a lead first came from: a search, an ad, a referral, a yard sign, a repeat customer. It is stored on the contact in the CRM at the moment the lead arrives. Followed through to the sale, it shows which channels produce paying work and which only produce inquiries.

## Why it matters for a local business

Without lead source, every marketing decision is a guess. An owner in Hanover feels like the mailers work because one customer mentioned them. With it, the owner can see that referrals close at 60% and paid leads at 20%, and price each accordingly. Lead Engine stamps the source on each contact automatically from the tracked number or tagged link, so the field is filled in without relying on memory.

## In practice

A Weymouth painter tracks 80 leads over a quarter. Paid ads: 34 leads, 5 jobs, $2,700 spent. Business profile: 22 leads, 9 jobs, no ad cost. Referrals: 14 leads, 9 jobs. Mailers: 10 leads, 1 job, $1,900 spent. The mailer budget moves to a referral reward and review requests.
