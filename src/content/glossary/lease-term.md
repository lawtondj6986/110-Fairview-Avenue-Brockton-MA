## What it means

The lease term is the period during which the tenant has the right to occupy and the obligation to pay. Nearly every other term in the lease prices off it: longer terms earn more improvement dollars and free rent, shorter terms preserve flexibility. Options to renew let a tenant have both.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore landlords of small buildings often prefer a five-year term with options over a ten-year term, because they can reset rent sooner. Tenants investing in equipment and fit-out should push for the options, which cost the landlord little and protect the tenant's investment.

## In practice

A five-year lease with two five-year options at pre-agreed escalations gives a tenant up to fifteen years of control while committing to only five. The landlord's trade is a slightly higher starting rent.
