## What it means

A waitlist is a list of customers who could not get the time or service they wanted and agreed to be contacted if it opens. In a restaurant it covers tonight's tables. In a practice or service company it covers earlier appointment dates. It turns cancellations into filled slots.

## Why it matters for a local business

Cancellations are constant. What varies is whether the slot gets resold. A front desk that has to phone six people to fill a 2 p.m. opening usually does not bother, and the hour is lost. A waitlist also keeps customers from calling a competitor when you are booked out two weeks. JobSite booking lets a customer join the list for a date range, and Lead Engine sends the opening by text the moment a slot frees up.

## In practice

A Boca Raton physical therapy clinic sees 14 late cancellations a week and refills 3 by phone. With a text waitlist of about 40 patients, it refills 10. Seven extra visits a week at $115 each is $805, or about $41,800 a year, from appointments that already existed.
