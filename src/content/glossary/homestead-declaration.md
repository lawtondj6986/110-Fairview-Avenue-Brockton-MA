## What it means

A declaration of homestead is a document recorded under Chapter 188 of the Massachusetts General Laws by which a homeowner claims protection for equity in their principal residence against later unsecured creditors. An automatic homestead applies to every owner-occupied home without filing; a recorded declaration protects a much larger amount, with the dollar limits set by statute and adjusted by the Legislature. Your attorney has the current figures.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A homestead is a public statement by the owner that a specific property is their principal residence. In counterparty research, it confirms which of several properties a person actually lives in, which affects how a judgment creditor could reach equity. In a property brief, it tells a buyer whether a seller's judgment lien might be partially shielded. ICS uses it strictly as a recorded document in a lawful lien and title search, never as a route to anyone's door.

## In practice

A Brockton landlord has a $28,000 judgment against a former commercial tenant who personally guaranteed the lease. The Registry shows the guarantor owns two residential properties. A homestead declaration recorded in 2019 covers the Whitman house, not the Abington two-family. The landlord's attorney levies on the Abington property first. The homestead record saved months of pursuing protected equity.
