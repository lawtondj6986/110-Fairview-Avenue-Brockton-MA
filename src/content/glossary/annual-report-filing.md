## What it means

Massachusetts requires corporations, LLCs, and limited partnerships to file an annual report with the Corporations Division of the Secretary of the Commonwealth. The report updates the entity's officers or managers, principal address, registered agent, and, for corporations, authorized shares. Filing keeps the entity in good standing. Failure to file leads first to delinquency and eventually to involuntary dissolution, though the entity can be revived by catching up.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Annual reports are the heartbeat of a company's public record. Read across ten years, they show who ran a South Shore contractor or property holding company, when a partner dropped out, and when the address moved from Plymouth to Boca Raton. ICS pulls every available year, not just the latest, because the changes carry the story. All of it is free and public in the online database, which is the only source needed.

## In practice

A seller's LLC that owns a Route 24 warehouse lists one manager today. The 2018 through 2021 reports list two. The second manager's exit coincides with a Plymouth Superior Court case between the two, found in a litigation search. The buyer now knows to ask whether the departed partner retains any interest in the property before signing the purchase and sale agreement.
