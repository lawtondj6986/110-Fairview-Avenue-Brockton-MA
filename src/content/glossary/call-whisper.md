## What it means

A call whisper is a short audio message that plays to the person answering a tracked phone call, just before the caller is connected. It usually announces where the call came from, such as "Google Ads call" or "website call". The caller never hears it. It is a feature of call tracking, tied to the unique number assigned to each marketing source.

## Why it matters for a local business

In a small shop, the person answering is often the owner in a truck. A two-second whisper tells them this is a paid lead, not a supplier or a robocall, before they say hello. It changes the greeting, and it changes who drops what to pick up. Where Lead Engine sets up call tracking, whispers are matched to each tracked number so the source is clear on every call.

## In practice

A Brockton plumber pays about $45 per call from local ads. Before whispers, the crew let roughly 1 in 4 unknown numbers ring out. With a whisper saying "ad call, answer now", pickup on ad calls goes from 74% to 93%. On 60 ad calls a month, that is 11 more answered calls already paid for.
