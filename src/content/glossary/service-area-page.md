## What it means

A service area page targets searches that combine a service and a place, such as electrician in Rockland. Done well, it is specific to that town: the neighborhoods, the typical buildings, the recent jobs, the reviews from residents. Done badly, it is a template that search engines ignore.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

This website's own South Shore pages are the model ICS builds for clients: each written corridor by corridor, with its own FAQ and schema. A Market Lock client receives pages like these for the towns in their territory, which is how one business per category, per town, becomes visible as exactly that.
