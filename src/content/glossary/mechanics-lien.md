## What it means

A mechanic's lien is a statutory security interest in real property granted to those who improve it: general contractors, subcontractors, suppliers, and design professionals. In Massachusetts the lien is governed by Chapter 254. It is perfected by recording a notice of contract and a statement of account at the Registry of Deeds within tight statutory windows and then filing an enforcement action. It attaches to the owner's interest even when the owner paid the general contractor.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Construction is a large part of South Shore commercial activity, and mechanic's liens show up in the Registry on everything from Rockland warehouses to Plymouth medical buildings. ICS searches Registry indexes for notices of contract and statements of account on the property and in the names of the parties. A lien recorded last month on a property under agreement is a title issue. Three liens recorded against a contractor's recent jobs are a reason to ask harder questions before signing with that contractor.

## In practice

A buyer is under agreement on a renovated Hanover office building. The Plymouth County Registry shows a notice of contract recorded 40 days ago by an HVAC subcontractor claiming $87,000. The seller's general contractor was paid in full. The subcontractor was not. Counsel requires the seller to bond off or discharge the lien before the closing date, and the deposit stays in escrow until it is done.
