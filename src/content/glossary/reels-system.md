## What it means

The monthly Reels system is a production cadence included in Market Lock: a set number of short, vertical videos per month, filmed with minimal owner time, edited and published on a schedule. It keeps a business visible in its market between the moments when someone is actively searching.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

For a Market Lock client on the South Shore, the Reels system is part of owning the category in the town: the roofer people have seen is the roofer people call. It is paired with paid media and the Proof Engine so each reinforces the others.
