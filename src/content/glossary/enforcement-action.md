## What it means

An enforcement action is a formal proceeding or order by a government agency against a person or business for an alleged violation. It may be administrative, handled inside the agency, or judicial, filed in court. Outcomes range from a warning letter to penalties, license restrictions, or a consent decree.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Regulators leave a paper trail that lawsuits do not. A business can have a clean court record and still carry years of citations. We report each action with its status: alleged, contested, settled, or adjudicated. An open notice is an allegation. A final order is a finding. Mixing them up is how research becomes unfair, and unfair research gets a client in trouble.

## In practice

Before leasing 8,000 square feet in Brockton to a metal finishing tenant, a landlord asks for a background brief. Agency searches return 2 state environmental notices over 7 years, both resolved, with penalties totaling $12,500, and no federal actions. The landlord's attorney adds an environmental indemnity and a larger security deposit to the lease instead of walking away. The brief lists each notice with its date, agency, case number, and final status, so the tenant can respond to specifics.
