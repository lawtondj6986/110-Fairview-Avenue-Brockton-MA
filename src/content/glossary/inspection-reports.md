## What it means

Inspection reports are the written results of official inspections: food establishment inspections by a board of health, building and electrical inspections, fire safety inspections, and state inspections of regulated facilities. Each records the date, the inspector, what was checked, and any violations and required corrections.

## Why it matters in a dossier

They are dated, first-hand observations by a public official with no stake in the deal. For a business acquisition, they test the seller's story about how the place is run. For a property, they show what the building department already knows. We request them openly under the Public Records Law, in our own name or the client's, and report corrected violations as corrected.

## In practice

A buyer is under agreement on a restaurant in Quincy for $325,000. We request 5 years of health and fire inspection reports. They show 11 inspections, with the same refrigeration temperature violation cited 4 times and a hood suppression system flagged twice. The buyer gets an equipment quote of $18,000 and renegotiates. The records cost nothing and arrived in 8 business days.
