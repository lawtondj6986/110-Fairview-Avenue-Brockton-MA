## What it means

The HVAC clause allocates responsibility for the building's heating, ventilation, and air conditioning equipment among routine maintenance, repair, and full replacement. Because units are expensive and fail predictably with age, the replacement allocation is the economically significant part.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Rooftop units on South Shore strip centers and flex buildings are frequently past fifteen years old. A tenant accepting full HVAC responsibility on such a building is accepting a likely replacement during the term. We negotiate caps, landlord warranties at delivery, and amortized replacement cost as standard tenant protections.

## In practice

A tenant's lease caps its share of HVAC replacement at $3,000 per unit per occurrence, with the landlord covering the balance. A $14,000 compressor failure in year three costs the tenant $3,000 instead of $14,000.
