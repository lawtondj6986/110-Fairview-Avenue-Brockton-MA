## What it means

A maintenance agreement is a contract under which a customer pays a recurring fee for scheduled service: two HVAC tune-ups a year, quarterly pest control, an annual generator check. It usually adds perks such as priority scheduling and a repair discount. For the business it converts one-time customers into predictable, scheduled work.

## Why it matters for a local business

Agreements smooth out the seasons. Tune-ups can be scheduled into slow weeks, and members call you first when the system fails in January. They also raise the value of the company to a buyer or lender, because contracted revenue is easier to underwrite. Lead Engine handles the part that usually breaks: renewal reminders, visit scheduling texts, and reactivating lapsed members.

## In practice

A Hanover HVAC company sells a $228 annual plan, billed at $19 a month, with two visits and 15 percent off repairs. It has 310 members, or $70,680 in contracted revenue. Members average another $410 a year in repairs, and 88 percent renew. Adding 100 members adds about $63,800 a year in total revenue.
