## What it means

The local pack, also called the map pack, is the block of three business listings with a map that Google displays for searches with local intent, such as "dentist Quincy" or "plumber near me." The listings come from Google Business Profiles, not web pages, and show rating, review count, hours, and a call button.

## Why it matters for a local business

For a local business the pack is the front door. It appears above the organic results and captures most of the clicks and calls on a phone. A Hanover HVAC company in the pack gets the call; the one in organic position four gets the leftovers. ICS treats the pack as the first ranking goal in every Lead Engine and local SEO engagement: complete profile, correct categories, steady review velocity through Proof Engine, and consistent name, address, and phone across the web.

## In practice

A Rockland auto repair shop ranked in the pack only for its own name. Over four months ICS fixed its primary category, added 14 services with descriptions, collected 60 new reviews, and posted weekly. It entered the pack for "auto repair Rockland," "brake repair Rockland," and "oil change Rockland," and calls from the profile rose from 38 to 127 a month. No ad spend was involved.
