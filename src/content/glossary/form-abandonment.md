## What it means

Form abandonment is the share of people who begin a form, such as a quote request or booking page, and leave before submitting it. It is measured by comparing form starts to form completions. A 60 percent abandonment rate means that for every ten people who tried to contact you, six gave up partway through.

## Why it matters for a local business

These are the warmest prospects a local business has: people who already decided to reach out. A long quote form on a Brockton remodeler's site asking for budget, timeline, address, and a project description before any contact is a wall. JobSite forms ask three questions, work with one thumb on a phone, and show the next step in plain words. QuoteBrain goes further: it gives the visitor a price range in exchange for the same information, which is a reason to finish.

## In practice

A Weymouth fence company had a nine-field form with 210 starts and 58 completions a month, a 72 percent abandonment rate. ICS cut it to three fields with an instant estimate at the end. Starts stayed about the same, completions rose to 141, and the owner booked 26 more on-site estimates a month from the same traffic. At a $3,800 average fence and a 30 percent close rate, that is roughly $30,000 in added monthly sales.
