## What it means

A due diligence brief is a bounded intelligence product that answers the questions a buyer, landlord, lender, or partner should ask before committing: who am I dealing with, what is their record, and what have they not told me. It is built from public records and lawful methods and delivered as findings with graded sources and stated confidence.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore the parties to a deal often know each other by reputation, which is exactly when a quiet check matters most. ICS's brief is the step between a handshake and a signature, and the free checklist on this site lists what it covers.
