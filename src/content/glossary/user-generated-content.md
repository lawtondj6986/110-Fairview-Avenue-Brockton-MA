## What it means

User-generated content, often shortened to UGC, is anything about a business that customers create themselves: reviews, photos of finished work, social posts, videos, and answers in public question sections. The business did not write it, which is exactly why other customers trust it more than advertising.

## Why it matters for a local business

A homeowner in Weymouth choosing between two fence companies believes a neighbor's photo over either company's brochure. Customer content also keeps a business profile and website fresh, which search engines and AI answer tools both notice. The work is in asking at the right moment and getting permission. Proof Engine requests reviews and photos after each completed job and turns the approved ones into case studies and site content.

## In practice

A Hanover deck builder asks every customer for a photo and a review at final walkthrough. Out of 48 jobs in a season, 29 leave reviews and 17 send photos, all with written permission to reuse. The gallery page built from those 17 projects converts visitors at 6%, against 2.5% for the old stock-photo page.
