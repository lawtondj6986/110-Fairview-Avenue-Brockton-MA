## What it means

Market Lock is ICS's flagship system, combining the full lead-response and follow-up machine of Lead Engine with managed advertising, video, answer engine optimization, and, uniquely, a written exclusivity commitment: ICS will not run the system for a competitor in the same category in the same town during the term.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The Territory Board on this site is Market Lock's public ledger for the South Shore and for Broward County: every town, every category, open or locked. Whoever locks a territory first keeps it for the term and has first right to renew.
