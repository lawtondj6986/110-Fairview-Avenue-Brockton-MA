## What it means

Appointment booking automation connects a business's calendar to its website, texts, and chat agent so that a prospect can choose and confirm a time directly, with reminders and rescheduling handled automatically. It removes the phone tag that loses leads and the no-shows that waste days.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore practices and service businesses that add online booking see the same pattern: more after-hours bookings, fewer no-shows thanks to reminders, and front-desk time freed for patients and customers who are present. It is a standard part of the JobSite and Lead Engine builds.
