## What it means

A pad site is a separately defined parcel within a shopping center, typically along the road frontage, developed as a freestanding building with its own parking and often a drive-through. Pads are leased or sold separately from the center's main building and attract the highest-visibility uses.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The pads in front of South Shore centers along Route 18, Route 53, and Route 139 host banks, coffee, and quick-service restaurants, almost entirely national brands. For a local business the realistic path to a pad is a ground lease on a family-held parcel rather than a slot in a developer's center.
