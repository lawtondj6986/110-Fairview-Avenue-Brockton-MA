## What it means

A marketing qualified lead is a contact who has shown enough interest, through actions tracked by your marketing systems, to be worth a direct sales touch. The threshold is set by lead scoring: points for pages visited, forms used, emails opened, service area, job type. An MQL has not yet been spoken to or confirmed as a fit; that comes at the next stage, the sales qualified lead.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Hanover kitchen and bath remodeler that generates 120 inquiries a month cannot call all of them the same afternoon, and should not. Some are tire-kickers from Rhode Island, some want a $400 faucet swap, and some have used the QuoteBrain calculator twice on a $60,000 kitchen in Norwell. The MQL flag puts the third group at the top of the owner's call list. ICS builds the scoring into Lead Engine so the sort happens automatically, and low scorers still get a nurture sequence rather than silence.

## In practice

A Plymouth solar installer scored leads on five signals: in-territory address, calculator use, roof photo uploaded, two or more site visits, and a request for a specific install month. Leads scoring 60 or higher became MQLs and got a call within 15 minutes during business hours. Of 340 leads in a quarter, 88 qualified. Those 88 produced 31 site visits and 14 signed contracts, while the other 252 produced 3 contracts through automated follow-up alone.
