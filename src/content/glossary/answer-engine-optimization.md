## What it means

Answer engine optimization is the practice of structuring a business's online presence so that AI assistants and AI-powered search features can understand it, trust it, and cite it when answering a user's question. It builds on SEO fundamentals and adds machine-readable clarity: schema markup, consistent facts, published pricing, and explicit answers.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

This website is itself the demonstration: published pricing as structured data, FAQ schema on dozens of pages, a Person record for the founder, and a full-text file for AI crawlers. The same techniques, applied to a South Shore business, are a standard component of every ICS system and the core of Market Lock.

## In practice

A Stoughton electrician's site gets service pages per town, FAQ schema answering the questions customers actually ask, and consistent facts across the site and the Google profile. Within a quarter, AI search summaries begin naming the business for electrician queries in its towns.
