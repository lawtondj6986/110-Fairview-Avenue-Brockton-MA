## What it means

Average ticket is total revenue divided by the number of jobs, orders, or visits in the same period. It tells you how much a typical customer spends each time. Paired with the number of transactions, it explains all of your revenue: more tickets, bigger tickets, or both. Most owners know their job count and guess at the ticket.

## Why it matters for a local business

Local service businesses often chase more leads when the faster fix is a bigger ticket. A Weymouth auto shop doing 300 repair orders a month at $285 and the same shop at $320 are $10,500 a month apart with identical traffic. QuoteBrain calculators present tiered options so the customer self-selects into a higher tier, and the number is tracked in the monthly dashboard next to lead volume so the two levers can be compared.

## In practice

A Plymouth landscaper reviewed a year of invoices: 640 jobs, $192,000 in revenue, a $300 average ticket. He added a spring cleanup and mulch bundle to every quote as the middle option. The next season the ticket rose to $355 on roughly the same job count, about $35,000 in added revenue with no new advertising. The payback on the quote tool was under a month.
