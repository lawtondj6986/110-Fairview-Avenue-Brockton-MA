## What it means

Internal rate of return is the discount rate at which the present value of all cash a property produces, including the sale proceeds, equals the cash invested. It is the standard way institutional investors compare deals with different timing and sizes.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore sub-$3M buildings are not bought on IRR; they are bought because a business needs a home or because a local investor knows the street. That said, when a seller's broker presents a pro-forma IRR, know that it rests on an assumed exit cap rate and rent growth that may or may not survive scrutiny.

## In practice

Buy for $1,000,000 with $300,000 down, collect growing cash flow for seven years, sell for $1,250,000 net: the IRR works out to roughly 11 percent. Change the sale price to $1,050,000 and the IRR drops to about 8 percent.
