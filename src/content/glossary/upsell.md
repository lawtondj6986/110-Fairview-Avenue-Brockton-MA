## What it means

An upsell is an offer that increases the size of a sale the customer has already decided to make: a higher tier, an upgrade, an extended warranty, or an add-on service. It is measured by attach rate, the share of sales that include the offer, and by its effect on average ticket.

## Why it matters for a local business

Getting the customer is the expensive part. Once the truck is in the driveway in Hingham, adding a $150 item costs almost nothing to sell. Most small businesses leave this to whether the tech remembers to mention it. QuoteBrain builds options into the quote itself, with good, better, best pricing and checkbox add-ons, so every customer sees every option every time. A membership plan offered at checkout is the most common upsell of all.

## In practice

A Marshfield carpet cleaner averages $240 a job across 90 jobs a month. Adding a $45 fabric protector and a $60 upholstery add-on to the online quote produces a 30 percent and 15 percent attach rate. That is 27 protector sales and about 14 upholstery sales: roughly $2,050 a month, lifting the average ticket to about $263.
