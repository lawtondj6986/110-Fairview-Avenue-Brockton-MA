## What it means

Organic traffic is the set of visits that reach a website from the unpaid listings on a search engine. It excludes paid ads, and it is usually reported separately from visits that come through map and business profile listings. It splits into branded searches, where the person already knew the business name, and non-branded searches, where they were looking for a service.

## Why it matters for a local business

Organic is the channel that does not stop when the ad budget does. For a Rockland contractor, the non-branded part is what counts as new business: people who searched "deck builder near me" and found the site. Watch that number separately. A rise in total organic traffic driven only by people searching your name is word of mouth showing up in search, not SEO working.

## In practice

A Hanover kitchen remodeler gets 900 organic visits a month, 610 of them branded. After a JobSite rebuild with 9 service pages and 12 town pages, non-branded visits grow from 290 to 740 over 6 months. At a 3% inquiry rate, that is about 13 more inquiries a month with no ad spend.
