## What it means

Local SEO is the discipline of appearing when someone nearby searches for what you do: the map results, the local organic listings, and increasingly the AI summaries that draw on them. It is driven by proximity, relevance, and prominence, and a business can influence the latter two.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore the competitive unit is the town. A Whitman plumber ranks in Whitman and maybe Abington and Hanson, not the whole region. That is why every ICS build includes town-level service pages and why Market Lock is sold by town: the territory is the search.
