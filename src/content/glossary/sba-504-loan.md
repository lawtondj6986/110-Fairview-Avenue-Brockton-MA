## What it means

The SBA 504 program finances fixed assets, chiefly owner-occupied commercial real estate and heavy equipment, through a partnership between a conventional lender and a certified development company. It offers long terms, a fixed rate on the SBA portion, and a low down payment, in exchange for occupancy and eligibility requirements.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The 504 is the engine of the South Shore owner-user market. A contractor or clinic that could never save a 30 percent down payment can buy its building with 10 percent, and the fixed-rate 25-year debenture removes the balloon risk that haunts conventional loans. ICS coordinates with local 504 lenders on most owner-user purchases.

## In practice

A $1,500,000 purchase: bank first mortgage $750,000, 504 debenture $600,000 at a fixed rate for 25 years, borrower equity $150,000. The combined payment is competitive with the rent the business was paying.
