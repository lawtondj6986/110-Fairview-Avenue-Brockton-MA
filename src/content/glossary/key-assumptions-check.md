## What it means

A key assumptions check lists every assumption behind an analytic conclusion, rates how confident the team is in each and how much the conclusion depends on it, and asks what public evidence would prove it wrong. It is a 30 to 60 minute exercise done before a brief ships. The point is to find the single assumption everyone shares and nobody has tested.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Research briefs fail less often from missing records than from unexamined assumptions: that the LLC manager is the real owner, that the Hanover permit history is complete online, that a quiet docket means no disputes. ICS runs the check on every Deep Dossier and marks the assumptions that remain untested, so the client knows where the brief is strong and where it is leaning. Testing an assumption means pulling another public record, never a shortcut through private data.

## In practice

A brief concludes that a Quincy seller has no environmental exposure. The check lists 6 assumptions. One stands out: that the state's 21E site list is complete for the parcel. Confidence is medium; dependence is total. A 2-hour pull of municipal fire department records finds a 1994 underground tank removal with no closure letter. The conclusion is revised and the buyer orders a Phase I ESA for $2,800.
