## What it means

Vacancy rate is empty, available space divided by total space. For a market it signals the balance of supply and demand; for a single building it is a line in the pro forma that turns gross scheduled rent into expected rent.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The South Shore's industrial vacancy has run in low single digits for an extended period, while older downtown retail in some towns runs much higher. The averages hide the real story, which is that a well-located, well-kept small building here rarely sits long at a sensible rent.

## In practice

A four-unit strip center with one 1,500 square foot vacancy in 8,000 square feet has an 18.75 percent vacancy rate. The pro forma should show the lease-up cost and timeline, not assume the unit fills on day one.
