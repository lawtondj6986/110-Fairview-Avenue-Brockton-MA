## What it means

Buyer representation is an agency relationship in which the broker owes its duties of loyalty, disclosure, and confidentiality to the buyer or tenant. It is documented in a representation agreement and disclosed to the other side. Without it, a buyer talking to a listing broker is talking to the seller's agent.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore, the buildings worth buying often change hands before they are marketed. A buyer's broker who knows the owners on Route 28, Route 18, and the downtown blocks hears about them first. That is why ICS represents buyers as a core service, not an afterthought.

## In practice

A contractor engages ICS to find a 5,000 to 8,000 square foot building with yard space within fifteen minutes of Bridgewater. Three weeks later, an off-market Avon property surfaces through an owner conversation, and the contractor is under agreement before it is ever listed.
