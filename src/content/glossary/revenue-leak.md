## What it means

Revenue leak is ICS's term for the gap between the business a company could have booked from the demand it already attracted and the business it actually booked. It is measured in missed calls, unanswered leads, abandoned quotes, and forgotten customers, each with a dollar value attached.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The Revenue Leak calculator on this site is the first tool most South Shore prospects use, and its result is the first number in most ICS proposals: here is what is leaking, here is what the system costs, here is the gap.
