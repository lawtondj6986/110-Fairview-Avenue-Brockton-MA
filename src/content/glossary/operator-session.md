## What it means

The Operator Session is ICS's paid, in-person diagnostic: a half-day working audit of how a business actually handles demand, from the first ring to the signed job. It produces measured baselines, such as response time and quote conversion, and a written plan, and its fee is credited toward any subsequent build.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The session happens at the client's shop, office, or clinic on the South Shore, not over a video call, because the leaks are visible in the room: the phone that rings out, the quotes in a drawer, the review request nobody sends. It is the most honest first step ICS offers.
