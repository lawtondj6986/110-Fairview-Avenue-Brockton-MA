## What it means

Net effective rent is the total rent paid over a lease term, minus concessions such as free rent and tenant improvement dollars, divided by the term's length and the square footage. It converts a lease with several moving parts into one comparable number per square foot per year.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore landlords compete on concessions more than on face rent; two free months and a $10 allowance on a five-year lease can take $3 or more off the effective rate. A tenant comparing a Weymouth space at $24 with free rent against a Braintree space at $22 with none should compute both before choosing.

## In practice

Five-year lease on 2,000 square feet at $20 with 3 percent annual escalations, three free months, and a $15 TI allowance: the net effective rent is about $17.75 per square foot, not $20.
