## What it means

Job costing is the practice of recording what each job actually cost and comparing it to the price charged. It breaks cost into labor hours, materials, subcontractors, and a share of overhead. The result is gross margin per job and, across many jobs, margin per job type.

## Why it matters for a local business

Most small contractors price from memory and find out at tax time whether it worked. Material prices move, and a job that made 40 percent two years ago may make 22 percent today at the same quote. Job costing data is also what makes an instant quote safe to publish: QuoteBrain calculators are built from real cost per square foot or per unit, not from a guess.

## In practice

A Pembroke deck builder quotes a 320-square-foot composite deck at $19,200. Actual costs come in at $7,900 for materials, 96 labor hours at a $48 loaded rate for $4,608, a $350 permit, and $1,900 of allocated overhead: $14,758 total. Margin is $4,442, or 23 percent, against a 35 percent target. The next quote goes out at $22,700.
