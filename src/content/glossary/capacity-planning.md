## What it means

Capacity planning is the work of estimating how many jobs, appointments, or covers you can deliver in a period and lining that up against expected demand. The inputs are sellable hours per person, average job length, and the calendar. The output is a decision: hire, add hours, raise prices, or slow the marketing.

## Why it matters for a local business

Demand on the South Shore is seasonal. Heating calls spike in the first cold week of November, landscapers are slammed in May, and tax preparers disappear in March. A business that runs the same lead volume all year is overbooked for eight weeks and idle for twelve. Lead Engine reporting shows booked hours against capacity, so ad spend and reactivation campaigns can be turned up before the slow weeks, not during them.

## In practice

A Plymouth HVAC company has 4 technicians and 104 sellable hours a week. The schedule shows 131 hours of demand in the first two weeks of December and 61 in late February. The owner moves 40 maintenance-agreement tune-ups from December to February and adds a Saturday shift for three weeks. Utilization goes from a 126 and 59 percent swing to roughly 90 percent in both periods.
