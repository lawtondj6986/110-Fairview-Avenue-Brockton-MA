## What it means

A pro forma is a forward-looking income and expense statement for a property. Sellers use it to show potential; buyers and lenders rebuild it with their own assumptions. The gap between the two is where negotiations happen.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore the most common pro forma optimism is a tax line copied from last year without the reassessment that a sale triggers, and a management line of zero. A buyer's version adds both, and the price it supports is often 10 percent below the asking.

## In practice

Seller pro forma: $130,000 rent, $35,000 expenses, $95,000 NOI. Buyer version: 5 percent vacancy, $6,500 management, $5,000 reserves, taxes up $4,000 post-sale: NOI $73,000. At the same cap rate, that is a $300,000 difference in value.
