## What it means

A lead nurture sequence is a scripted, automated set of communications delivered over days or weeks to keep a prospect engaged and guide them to a decision. Each message has a purpose and a timing, and the sequence stops the moment the lead replies or books.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS writes sequences in the owner's voice for South Shore trades, practices, and shops, because a roofer in Abington should not sound like a software company. The sequence does the persistence; the owner gets the credit for being attentive.

## In practice

Day 0: quote sent with a booking link. Day 2: text asking if any questions. Day 5: email with a photo of a similar job in the same town. Day 9: call from the owner. Day 14: last note offering to hold the schedule slot. Conversion on quotes rises from 22 to 34 percent.
