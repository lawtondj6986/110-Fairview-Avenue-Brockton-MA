## What it means

Pretexting is the use of a false pretense to obtain information not otherwise available. It ranges from impersonating an account holder to creating fake personas to befriend a target online. Federal and state laws prohibit much of it, and the material it produces is unusable in any legitimate context.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS's methods code names pretexting as a bright line. A South Shore client who wants to know what is findable about a counterparty or an opponent gets exactly that, what is findable lawfully, and nothing obtained by deceiving anyone.
