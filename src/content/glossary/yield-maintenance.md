## What it means

Yield maintenance is a prepayment premium designed to leave the lender with the same yield it would have received had the borrower made every payment through maturity. The lower market rates are relative to the loan rate, and the more time remaining, the larger the premium. When market rates are above the loan rate, the premium usually falls to the stated minimum.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Local bank loans mostly carry simple step-down penalties such as 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 percent. Yield maintenance appears on life company, agency, and some credit union loans on larger apartment and retail properties in Quincy, Weymouth, and Plymouth. An owner who expects to sell within the loan term should weigh that exit cost against the lower rate these lenders offer.

## In practice

An owner wants to pay off $3,000,000 at 6.0 percent with 5 years remaining. The comparable Treasury yields 4.0 percent. The lender is losing about 2 points, roughly $60,000 a year. Discounted over 5 years, the premium is about $265,000, near 9 percent of the balance. Selling subject to an assumption could avoid most of it.
