## What it means

A title tag is the title of a web page as written in its code. It appears as the clickable headline in a search result, as the text on the browser tab, and as the default title when the page is shared. It is one of the strongest on-page signals telling a search engine what a page is about.

## Why it matters for a local business

Many small business sites still have a home page titled "Home" and five service pages that all share the business name as their title. That tells a search engine nothing about which page should answer which search. A title like "Roof Repair in Hanover, MA | Company Name" does. On JobSite builds, every page gets a unique title that names the service and the town.

## In practice

A Brockton tree service has 7 pages all titled with only the company name. Rewriting them takes 30 minutes: "Emergency Tree Removal Brockton MA | Company Name" and so on. In 8 weeks, the stump grinding page moves from position 22 to 7 and non-branded clicks go from 60 to 145 a month.
