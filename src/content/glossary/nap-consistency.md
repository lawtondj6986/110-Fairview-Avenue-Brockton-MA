## What it means

NAP stands for name, address, phone. NAP consistency means those three facts appear exactly the same everywhere your business is listed: your website, Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, directories, social profiles, and license records. Search engines compare these citations to decide how confident they are about your location and identity.

## Why it matters for a local business

Many South Shore businesses have moved, changed numbers, or been renamed since their first listing. A Quincy dentist who relocated from Hancock Street to a new office on Route 3A often has the old address living on a dozen sites. That inconsistency suppresses local pack rankings and sends patients to a locked door. ICS runs a citation audit at the start of every local SEO engagement and fixes the listings before building anything new, because new reviews and content cannot overcome a search engine that is unsure where you are.

## In practice

A Hanover landscaping company had three phone numbers in circulation after switching providers twice. A 45-listing audit found 19 with the wrong number and 7 with a prior address. ICS corrected them over three weeks. Within two months the company moved from position 7 to position 2 in the local pack for "landscaping Hanover," and the owner stopped hearing from customers that his number did not work.
