## What it means

Google Search Console is a free service from Google that reports on a website's presence in Google search. Its performance report lists the queries a site appeared for, with impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and average position. Its indexing reports show which pages are included in search, which are excluded, and why.

## Why it matters for a local business

It is the closest thing to hearing customers think out loud. A Taunton electrician can see that 300 people a month searched "EV charger installation Taunton" and the site showed up at position 11, on page two, with 4 clicks. That is a page worth building. It also catches silent failures, such as town pages that never got indexed. Every JobSite launch includes Search Console verification and a submitted sitemap.

## In practice

A Hanover veterinarian checks Search Console and finds 1,900 monthly impressions for dental cleaning searches at an average position of 9 and a 1.2% click rate, 23 clicks. A dedicated dental page with pricing moves the position to 4 in 10 weeks. Clicks rise to 95 a month.
