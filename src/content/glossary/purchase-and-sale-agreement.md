## What it means

The purchase and sale agreement converts the LOI into enforceable commitments: price, deposit, contingencies, what the seller must deliver, the closing date, and remedies if someone defaults. In Massachusetts, commercial deals usually go straight to the P&S after an LOI rather than through a separate offer form.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Massachusetts practice has the seller's attorney draft the P&S and the buyer's attorney mark it up. Build time for that exchange into the schedule; a week each way is normal. Both parties should have real estate counsel, and ICS works alongside yours rather than in place of them.

## In practice

P&S signed October 1 with a 45-day inspection contingency and a 75-day financing contingency; closing set for December 20. The buyer's deposit of $50,000 sits in the seller's attorney's escrow account until then.
