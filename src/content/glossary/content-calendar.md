## What it means

A content calendar is a schedule that assigns a topic, format, channel, and publish date to each piece of content. It replaces "post when we think of it" with a plan. For a small business it covers Business Profile posts, short videos, service pages, emails, and review requests, and it is usually built a quarter at a time.

## Why it matters for a local business

Google and customers reward businesses that show recent activity. A Hanover landscaper who posts a job photo every week looks open and busy; one whose last post is from 2023 looks closed. The calendar also keeps content aligned with demand: South Shore homeowners search for snow removal in November, not February. ICS builds the calendar into Reels System and the monthly content plan so the owner approves a month at a glance instead of being asked for a photo every day.

## In practice

A Marshfield roofing company planned 12 months in one sitting: 52 Profile posts, 48 short videos, 12 town pages for the Route 3 corridor, and 4 seasonal emails. The owner spent about an hour a month approving the batch. Over the year, organic visits rose from 600 to 2,100 a month and the company ranked in the local pack for roofing in nine of the twelve towns it targeted.
