## What it means

Lead response time is speed to lead expressed as an actual, tracked measurement rather than an aspiration. It is reported per lead and as a monthly average and median, so a business can see not just how fast it usually responds but how often it fails to.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

When ICS audits a South Shore business before a build, average response time is routinely measured in hours and the after-hours number is often infinite: nobody responds until morning. That audit result is usually what turns a skeptic into a client.

## In practice

Before: 47 inquiries in a month, median response 3 hours 40 minutes, 14 never answered. After the Lead Engine: median response 38 seconds, zero unanswered, and nine more booked jobs.
