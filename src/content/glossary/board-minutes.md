## What it means

Board minutes are the official record of a meeting of a public body: who attended, what was discussed, what motions were made, and how each member voted. In Massachusetts, select boards, planning boards, zoning boards, school committees, and similar bodies must keep them under the Open Meeting Law. Private company minutes are different: they are not public unless filed in court or with a regulator.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Minutes show what was said before anyone knew it would matter. A developer's promise to a planning board, a member's recusal, a 3 to 2 vote, an abutter's objection: all dated and attributable. For ballot research and site diligence they are often the best primary source available, and they are free.

## In practice

A buyer is looking at a 2-acre parcel in Hanover. We pull 6 years of planning board and zoning board minutes and find the parcel discussed at 4 meetings, including a prior owner's withdrawn application after neighbors raised drainage concerns. The buyer's engineer knows what to study before the due diligence period ends. Cost of the records: $0.
