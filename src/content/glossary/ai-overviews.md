## What it means

AI Overviews are Google's generated answers that appear above the standard results for many queries. The system reads several pages, writes a short summary, and links the pages it drew from. For a user, the question is often answered without a click. For a business, the question is whether it is one of the cited sources or one of the pages nobody scrolls down to anymore.

## Why it matters for a local business

When a Hanover homeowner searches "how much does a heat pump cost in Massachusetts," the overview answers with a range and cites three or four sources. If one of them is a Hanover HVAC company's page with real South Shore install prices and Mass Save rebate figures, that company just got the most visible placement on the page. ICS builds JobSite service pages and question pages to be citeable: direct answers in the first sentence, concrete numbers, structured data, and a Google Business Profile that matches. That is the same work as answer engine optimization, applied to Google's own summary.

## In practice

A Weymouth plumbing company published 12 question pages in plain language: tankless versus tank costs, how long a water heater lasts in a house on well water, what a Weymouth plumbing permit costs. Within four months, the site was cited in AI Overviews for 9 of those questions when searched from South Shore locations. Organic clicks on the question pages were modest, about 180 a month, but calls that mentioned "I saw you on Google" rose from 3 to 14 a month, and the pages ranked top three in the standard results beneath the overview.
