## What it means

An estoppel certificate is a document signed by a tenant that confirms the key facts of its lease: rent, term, security deposit, options, defaults, and any landlord obligations outstanding. Once signed, the tenant is prevented from later claiming otherwise, which is what gives a buyer or lender confidence.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On multi-tenant South Shore buildings, collecting estoppels is often the task that pushes a closing. Sellers help themselves by asking tenants early and by keeping clean lease files, so the certificate is a formality rather than a negotiation.

## In practice

A buyer of a three-tenant Rockland building receives estoppels from all three. One reveals the landlord owes a $6,000 improvement reimbursement; the buyer receives a credit at closing instead of inheriting the dispute.
