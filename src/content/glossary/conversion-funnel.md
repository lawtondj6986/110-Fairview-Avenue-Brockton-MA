## What it means

A conversion funnel is a model of how prospects move toward becoming customers, drawn as a series of narrowing stages with the number of people at each one. The shape shows where people leave. It is a measurement tool more than a strategy: once each stage is counted, the business can calculate stage-to-stage conversion rates and work on the weakest link instead of guessing.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Brockton roofer who says "I need more leads" often does not. ICS has seen funnels where 900 monthly site visitors produce 30 inquiries, 25 estimates, and 6 jobs: the problem is a 24 percent close rate on estimates, not traffic. Lead Engine reporting lays out the funnel by stage so the owner can see that one number. JobSite handles the visit-to-inquiry stage, QuoteBrain the inquiry-to-qualified stage, and follow-up automation the quoted-to-booked stage.

## In practice

A Hanover kitchen remodeler's funnel for a quarter: 2,400 site visits, 61 inquiries (2.5 percent), 44 qualified, 38 in-home consultations, 11 signed. The visit-to-inquiry rate was the weak point; comparable sites run 4 to 6 percent. ICS added a QuoteBrain ballpark calculator above the fold and a visible phone number with click-to-call on mobile. The next quarter: 2,300 visits, 118 inquiries (5.1 percent), 79 qualified, 62 consultations, 19 signed. Same traffic, 73 percent more signed projects.
