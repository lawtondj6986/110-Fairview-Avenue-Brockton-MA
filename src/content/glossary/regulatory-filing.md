## What it means

A regulatory filing is any document a person or business is required to submit to a government agency: a securities disclosure, an annual report, a license application, an environmental report, a lobbying or campaign finance report. The filer is responsible for its accuracy, and many are published by the agency.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Filings are a company describing itself on the record, repeatedly, over years. Reading them in sequence shows changes: a new officer, a dropped address, a revised revenue figure, a disclosure that appears one year and vanishes the next. We compare filings against each other and against what the company says in its marketing or its pitch.

## In practice

A Florida-based operator pitches a South Shore client on a joint venture and claims 12 years in business. Corporate filings show the entity was formed 5 years ago. Annual reports show 3 changes of principal address and 2 administrative dissolutions later cured. A predecessor entity explains part of the gap. The client asks about the rest. Total filings reviewed: 19. Each one is listed in the appendix with its filing date and document number, so the client can read the originals.
