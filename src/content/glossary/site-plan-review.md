## What it means

Site plan review is a planning board process that examines how a project sits on its lot: circulation, parking, stormwater, lighting, landscaping, and utilities. Unlike a special permit, it generally cannot deny an allowed use, but it can impose conditions that change the cost and timeline.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Stormwater is the South Shore's recurring site plan issue, because many commercial lots were paved decades ago under older rules and any redevelopment has to meet current standards. A buyer planning to repave or expand a lot should price that engineering before closing.

## In practice

A Stoughton owner adding 3,000 square feet to a warehouse goes through site plan review, which requires an infiltration system for the new roof runoff and 14 additional parking spaces. The project proceeds with both.
