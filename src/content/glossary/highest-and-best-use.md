## What it means

Highest and best use is the use of a property that is physically possible, legally permissible, financially feasible, and maximally productive. Appraisers determine it before valuing a property, because value follows use. Owners who understand it market to the right buyer; those who do not leave money behind.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore corridors are changing use: old auto dealerships become medical, aging motels become housing, and underused retail on Route 18 is eyed for mixed-use. An owner's tired building may be a developer's site. We assess that before recommending a price.

## In practice

A 1960s single-story retail building on a Weymouth parcel zoned to allow three stories is worth $1,100,000 as retail and $1,500,000 to a developer who will build mixed-use. The listing targets both and the developer wins.
