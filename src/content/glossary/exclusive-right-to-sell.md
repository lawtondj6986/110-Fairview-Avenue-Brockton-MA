## What it means

An exclusive right to sell agreement appoints one brokerage as the seller's agent for a set term and provides that the brokerage earns its commission if the property sells during the term, whoever produces the buyer. It is the form that lets a broker invest real marketing money in a listing.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Massachusetts requires written agency disclosure, and the listing agreement is where the relationship, the term, the fee, and the marketing commitments live. Ask the broker to put the marketing plan in writing too; a listing agreement with no marketing obligations is a promise to wait by the phone.

## In practice

A seller signs a nine-month exclusive right to sell with ICS at an agreed fee. The agreement lists the deliverables: a full OM, drone and virtual tour, targeted outreach, a property page with its own search presence, and weekly reporting.
