## What it means

Clear height is the vertical distance from the finished floor to the lowest overhead obstruction, such as a beam, duct, or sprinkler line. It defines the usable cube of an industrial space and is a primary specification for storage, distribution, and any use involving lifts or tall equipment.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Older South Shore industrial stock often has 12 to 16 foot clear heights, which suits trades and light assembly and rules out modern distribution. The few buildings with 20-plus feet command a premium and move fast. Knowing the client's real height requirement avoids chasing the wrong inventory.
