## What it means

The grantor-grantee index is the registry of deeds' master index by party name. Every recorded instrument, including deeds, mortgages, discharges, liens, attachments, and declarations of trust, is indexed under the names of the parties on each side, with the document type, date, and book and page.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Property records are organized by name, not by a single owner ID. To find everything a person or LLC holds in a county, you run the name both ways and read the results. The index also surfaces what is not a deed: a tax taking, an attachment, a notice of contract. A common name, or an owner who uses trusts, means more searches and careful matching.

## In practice

We search one principal and 4 related LLCs across Plymouth and Norfolk counties. The index returns 63 instruments. Sorting them shows 9 parcels acquired, 3 sold, 11 mortgages, 7 discharges, and 1 recorded attachment for $85,000. The attachment appeared only under the grantor name, with a middle initial the other records lacked. Total time: about 3 hours. The report lists every search string used, so a second researcher could repeat the work and get the same 63 results.
