## What it means

A litigation search surveys court records for cases involving a person or entity as plaintiff or defendant. Combined with the docket details, it reveals disputes, judgments, and behavior patterns that are among the most predictive signals in counterparty due diligence.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Plymouth and Norfolk Superior Court dockets, District Court civil records, and Land Court cases are standard pulls in an ICS brief on any South Shore counterparty. A pending case against a seller's entity is found in minutes and changes a negotiation.
