## What it means

Chain of custody, in an intelligence context, is the documented trail from each finding back to its primary source: what was retrieved, from where, when, and how it was preserved. It is what separates a defensible research product from a collection of assertions, and it is why thorough reports have long appendices.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS reports footnote every finding and archive every source, because a South Shore campaign or dealmaker may need to produce the document behind a claim long after a website has been edited. The sample pages on this site show the footnoting standard.
