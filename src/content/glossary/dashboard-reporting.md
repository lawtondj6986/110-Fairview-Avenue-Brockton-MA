## What it means

Dashboard reporting is the measurement layer in every ICS system: a single, always-current view of the metrics that connect marketing activity to revenue, reviewed with the client on a fixed cadence. It replaces the monthly PDF of impressions with the numbers an owner would ask about anyway.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore owners are not interested in charts for their own sake, and neither is ICS. The dashboard exists so that the monthly conversation can be about three numbers: how fast we answered, how many quotes we won, and how much work we booked.
