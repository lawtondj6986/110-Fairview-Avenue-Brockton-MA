## What it means

A sales qualified lead is a prospect a salesperson or owner has personally vetted and confirmed is worth pursuing: the need is real, the timing is near, the budget is workable, and the location is in your service area. It is the handoff point between marketing and selling. An SQL enters the pipeline as an opportunity with a stage, a value, and a next step.

## Why it matters for a local business

For a Weymouth paving contractor, the gap between leads and SQLs is where the afternoon goes. Fifty inquiries might yield 20 worth a site visit, and a site visit costs two hours and a truck. Qualifying on the phone first, with a short script and a QuoteBrain range, protects the calendar for jobs that can close. ICS tracks the MQL-to-SQL rate in Lead Engine because a falling number usually means the marketing is attracting the wrong people, not that sales got lazy.

## In practice

A Marshfield fence company received 64 leads in May. The office manager qualified each on a five-minute call: job type, footage, timing, town, and a price range from the calculator. Thirty-one became SQLs and got a scheduled estimate. Twenty-six estimates happened, 17 signed, average job $9,200. The 33 that did not qualify went into a text sequence; four came back in the fall as real jobs.
