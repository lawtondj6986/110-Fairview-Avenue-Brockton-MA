## What it means

QuoteBrain is the instant-quote engine ICS builds into client websites. A visitor answers a few questions about their job and receives a price range immediately, in exchange for contact details that flow straight into the CRM and the follow-up sequence. It converts price-curious visitors into qualified leads.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Across South Shore trades, QuoteBrain pages are consistently the highest-converting pages on a client's site, because they answer what every visitor wants to know first. The demo on this site shows how it feels from the customer's side.
