## What it means

The deeds excise is Massachusetts' tax on conveying real property, calculated on the purchase price and collected when the deed is recorded. It is a seller cost in nearly all transactions and one of the fixed items in a seller's net proceeds calculation.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

At $4.56 per thousand, the excise is small next to commission but not trivial on a seven-figure building. Our Seller Net Proceeds calculator includes it automatically so a South Shore owner sees the real walk-away number before listing.

## In practice

On a $1,250,000 Brockton sale the deeds excise is $5,700. With a 5 percent commission, attorney fees, and a $600,000 payoff, the seller nets roughly $575,000 before taxes on the gain.
