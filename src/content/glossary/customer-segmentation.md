## What it means

Customer segmentation is the practice of splitting a contact database into groups defined by shared attributes or behavior: what they bought, where they live, when they last did business, how much they have spent, how they found you. Each segment then receives messaging, offers, and timing tuned to it. The alternative, sending every contact the same message, wastes attention and trains people to ignore you.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Brockton auto repair shop's list includes someone who bought four tires last month and someone who has not been in since a 2022 inspection. The same "10 percent off tires" text is noise to the first and probably irrelevant to the second. Segmented, the first gets a rotation reminder in six months and the second gets a reactivation offer now. ICS builds segments into Lead Engine from fields the CRM already captures, so campaigns in Proof Engine and reactivation sequences go to the right people without manual sorting.

## In practice

A Plymouth landscaper had sent one spring email to all 2,300 contacts for years, with about 2 percent booking. ICS split the list: active maintenance accounts (680), past one-time customers by service (hardscape 310, cleanup 540, planting 190), and leads who never bought (580). Each got a different message: a renewal with a loyalty rate, a related upsell, a comeback offer, a limited spring slot. Overall booking rose to 6.4 percent, and the hardscape segment alone produced 14 patio and walkway jobs averaging $11,000.
