## What it means

A vanilla shell, also called a white box, is a generic level of finish a landlord delivers to a space before a tenant's specific build-out. It sits between a cold dark shell, with nothing but structure and utilities stubbed, and a fully finished second-generation space. The exact specification is set by the lease's work letter.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore landlords renovating older strip centers often deliver vanilla shell to new tenants and offer a modest improvement allowance on top. Tenants should get the shell specification in writing, with a delivery date, because the gap between a landlord's idea of finished and a tenant's can be thousands of dollars.
