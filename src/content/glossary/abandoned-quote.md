## What it means

An abandoned quote is a price estimate that a prospect requested, started, or received and then did not act on. It covers two cases: the person who quit an online quote calculator halfway through, and the person who got a full number and never answered. Either way, the business did the work of pricing and got no decision back.

## Why it matters for a local business

Most owners treat a silent quote as a lost job. Usually it is a stalled one. The homeowner in Hanover is waiting on a second bid, or the estimate landed in a spam folder. QuoteBrain asks for contact details before it shows the price, so an unfinished quote still becomes a lead, and Lead Engine runs the follow-up by text and email without anyone having to remember.

## In practice

A Weymouth roofer sends 40 quotes a month at an average of $14,000 and closes 10. Of the 30 silent ones, a three-touch follow-up over 7 days restarts 4 conversations and signs 2 jobs. That is $28,000 a month in work that was already priced and sitting in the sent folder.
