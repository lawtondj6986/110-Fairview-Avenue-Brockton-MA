## What it means

An assessor database is a municipality's public record of every parcel: owner of record, mailing address, assessed land and building value, lot size, square footage, year built, use code, and sales history. In Massachusetts each town runs its own, most on Vision, Patriot Properties, or AxisGIS. The data is public by law and free to search.

## Why it matters in a dossier

It is the fastest lawful way to turn an address into an owner and an owner into a portfolio. Brockton, Rockland, and Hanover each publish theirs online, so a researcher can map every parcel a holding company owns on Route 139 in an afternoon. ICS uses it for property and entity questions only, never to profile private individuals.

## In practice

A tenant wants to know who really controls a 14,000 square foot retail strip in Weymouth before negotiating a renewal. The assessor card lists an LLC with a Quincy mailing address; the same address appears on six other parcels across three towns, assessed at $9.1 million combined. Secretary of the Commonwealth filings then name the manager, and the tenant walks in knowing the landlord's full footprint.
