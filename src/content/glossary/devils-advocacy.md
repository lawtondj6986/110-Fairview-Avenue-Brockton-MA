## What it means

Devil's advocacy is a structured analytic technique in which a team member is formally assigned to argue against the prevailing conclusion, using the same evidence. The advocate's job is not to be contrarian for effect but to find the strongest alternative reading of the facts, the weakest links in the evidence chain, and the assumptions nobody questioned. The exercise is written up and considered before the product is finalized.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Research teams fall in love with their findings, especially the dramatic ones. A dossier that goes to a client, a lender, or a campaign without a hostile reading has not been tested. ICS runs devil's advocacy on every finding rated high confidence in a Deep Dossier. The advocate works only from the lawfully gathered record, which is also the only record an opponent could lawfully use, so the exercise previews the real counterargument.

## In practice

A due-diligence brief concluded that a Weymouth seller had concealed a Chapter 21E release at the site, based on a 2016 DEP filing. The devil's advocate pointed out that the filing named a different parcel number and that the seller bought the property in 2018. The lot numbering was checked at the Registry: the filing concerned the abutter. Two hours of advocacy prevented a false accusation that could have killed a $1.6 million deal.
