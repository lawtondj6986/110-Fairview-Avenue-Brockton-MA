## What it means

A review response is the reply a business posts publicly under a customer's review on a search listing or review site. It is visible to everyone who reads the review afterward. Responding to positive reviews shows the business is paying attention. Responding to negative ones shows how it behaves when something goes wrong.

## Why it matters for a local business

Shoppers read the bad reviews first, then the owner's answer. A one-star complaint with a measured reply often does less damage than one left hanging. A defensive reply does more. Responses should also sound like a person, not a template pasted 40 times. Lead Engine alerts the owner when a review posts and drafts a reply for approval, so nothing sits unanswered for months.

## In practice

A Weymouth restaurant has 212 reviews and has replied to 9. The owner starts answering every new review within 48 hours, about 14 a month, spending 20 minutes a week. One reviewer changes a 2-star rating to 4 stars after a phone call. Over 6 months the average moves from 4.1 to 4.4.
