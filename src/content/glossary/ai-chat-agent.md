## What it means

An AI chat agent is a conversational assistant, on a website, by text, or on a phone line, that handles first contact: answering common questions, qualifying the inquiry, collecting details, and booking or routing. It extends a small business's responsiveness to every hour of the week.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

For South Shore trades and practices, the agent's job is narrow and valuable: respond in seconds at any hour, capture what the job is and where, and get it on the calendar. ICS builds it on the business's actual information and tests it against the questions real customers ask.
