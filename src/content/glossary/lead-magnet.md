## What it means

A lead magnet is a free resource or tool offered on a website or ad in exchange for contact information. Guides, checklists, calculators, and sample reports are common forms. The exchange turns an anonymous visitor into a lead you can follow up with, and the content of the magnet signals what the person is shopping for.

## Why it matters for a local business

Most visitors to a local business site are not ready to call today. A lead magnet gives a Hanover homeowner researching a bathroom remodel a reason to leave a number now, so the business can follow up when the project becomes real. QuoteBrain is the lead magnet ICS builds for contractors: the visitor answers four questions and gets a price range, and the business gets a qualified lead with the job details attached. It converts researchers at two to three times the rate of a plain contact form.

## In practice

A Weymouth window company offered a Massachusetts window replacement cost guide and an instant estimator on its JobSite. In 90 days the guide collected 140 emails and the estimator collected 310 phone numbers with project details. Follow-up over the next quarter converted 42 of the estimator leads into installed jobs averaging $9,200, about $386,000 in work that started with a free price range.
