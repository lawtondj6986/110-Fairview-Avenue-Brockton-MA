## What it means

Debt service is the cash required each year to pay principal and interest on the mortgage. It sits below net operating income on the property's statement; NOI minus debt service is the owner's cash flow before taxes.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

With South Shore cap rates and borrowing costs often within a point of each other, debt service can consume most of a building's NOI. That is survivable for an owner-user who was going to pay rent anyway and risky for an investor counting on cash flow. Know the number before you offer.

## In practice

A $900,000 loan at 6.75 percent amortized over 25 years requires about $74,600 a year in debt service. If the property's NOI is $92,000, DSCR is 1.23 and annual cash flow is $17,400.
