## What it means

Customer lifetime value is the net profit attributable to the entire future relationship with a customer. It reframes marketing from cost to investment and justifies the systems that keep customers coming back: reminders, recall, loyalty, and consistent follow-up.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The South Shore's best small businesses are repeat businesses: the same families, the same buildings, the same trucks every year. ICS systems are built around recall and retention for exactly that reason; the cheapest customer to win is the one you already have.
