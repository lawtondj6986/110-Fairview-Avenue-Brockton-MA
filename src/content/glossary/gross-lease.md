## What it means

A gross lease rolls the landlord's ownership costs into a single rent, so the tenant's monthly cost is predictable and the landlord carries the risk of rising taxes and maintenance. Pure gross leases are most common in small office buildings and older multi-tenant properties.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore office space above storefronts in towns like Whitman, Abington, and Rockland is frequently quoted gross, which makes it feel expensive next to a NNN industrial bay until you add the bay's NNN charges. Our Lease Deal Analyzer puts both on the same footing.

## In practice

An 1,100 square foot office at $22 gross is $24,200 a year all in. A nearby 1,100 square foot space at $15 NNN with $8 of charges is $25,300, more than the gross space despite the lower rate.
