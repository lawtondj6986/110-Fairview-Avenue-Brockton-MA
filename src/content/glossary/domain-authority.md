## What it means

Domain authority is a predictive score, typically 1 to 100, published by SEO software companies to estimate how well a website should rank based mainly on the number and quality of sites linking to it. Each vendor computes its own version. It is a comparison tool between sites, not a measurement Google produces or uses directly.

## Why it matters for a local business

For a dentist in Hanover or a body shop in Taunton, domain authority is mostly a competitive yardstick. If your site scores 12 and the three competitors in the local pack score 25 to 30, your backlink profile is a gap worth closing. If you are already even, time is better spent on reviews, Google Business Profile activity, and service pages. ICS looks at it during JobSite planning to decide whether a client needs a link-building effort or just a better site.

## In practice

A Rockland flooring company had a domain authority of 8 after ten years with a brochure site. Over six months it earned 14 local links: the Rockland chamber, two supplier dealer locators, a South Shore home show sponsor page, a Patch article on a charity floor install, and a few trade directories. The score moved to 19. More useful, the site went from page two to the top three for "hardwood flooring Rockland" and "flooring installer Hanover," and monthly inbound calls from search rose from about 6 to 15.
