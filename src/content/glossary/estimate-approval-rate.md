## What it means

Estimate approval rate is approved estimates divided by estimates sent, measured by count or by dollar value. The two versions tell different stories: you can approve 60 percent of estimates by count and only 35 percent by dollars if the large jobs are the ones getting away.

## Why it matters for a local business

An estimate costs real money to produce: a site visit, an hour of pricing, a drive down Route 18. A roofer in Weymouth sending 40 estimates a month at a 25 percent approval rate is doing 30 unpaid site visits. QuoteBrain gives a ballpark before the visit, which screens out price shoppers, and Lead Engine runs the follow-up on every open estimate so none of them go quiet by accident.

## In practice

A Braintree painting company sends 52 estimates in a quarter worth $410,000 and gets 17 approved, worth $118,000: 33 percent by count, 29 percent by dollars. After adding a three-touch follow-up and a one-tap approve button, the next quarter shows 23 of 50 approved, or 46 percent. Six more jobs at an average of $6,900 is about $41,000 in added work.
