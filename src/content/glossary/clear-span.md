## What it means

Clear span describes a building, or a bay within one, where the roof structure runs wall to wall with no interior columns. More loosely, it is the open distance between supports. A listing with "40 by 50 column spacing" is describing how much clear floor sits between each set of columns.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Older mill and multi-story buildings in Brockton and Taunton often have columns every 20 feet or so, which limits them to storage, small shops, or conversion. Pre-engineered metal buildings in the industrial parks off Route 24, Route 3, and Route 139 can offer 60 to 100 feet of clear span. For indoor sports, auto and truck service, and fabrication users, that single spec decides the shortlist.

## In practice

An indoor training operator needs a 70 by 120 foot turf field. A 12,000 square foot building in Hanover at $11 a square foot has columns every 30 feet and does not work at any price. A 10,000 square foot clear-span building in Rockland at $14 does. The operator pays $140,000 a year for the smaller building.
