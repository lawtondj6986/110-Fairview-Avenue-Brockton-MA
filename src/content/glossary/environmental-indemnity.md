## What it means

An environmental indemnity is a contractual promise by one party to protect another from losses arising from contamination at a property. In purchases it allocates responsibility for known or unknown conditions; in loans it protects the lender. Its worth depends on the indemnitor's solvency and on how clearly the covered conditions are defined.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On South Shore deals involving former automotive, industrial, or dry-cleaning sites, the environmental indemnity is often negotiated alongside a Phase II and a price credit. The clean solution is usually a defined credit now rather than a promise to pay later from a seller who is retiring to Florida.

## In practice

A Phase II on a former Brockton gas station site finds residual petroleum below reportable levels. The seller provides a two-year indemnity for that condition capped at $75,000, backed by a $40,000 escrow, and the buyer closes.
