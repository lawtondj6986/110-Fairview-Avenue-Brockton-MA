## What it means

Beneficial ownership refers to the natural persons who ultimately own or control a legal entity, as distinct from the nominees, managers, or shell entities that appear on paper. Mapping it is a core task of commercial intelligence and the foundation of knowing who is across the table.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Many South Shore commercial properties are held in single-purpose LLCs, often layered. ICS maps the ownership chain as a standard part of a due diligence brief, because the names at the end of the chain are the ones whose record predicts how the deal will go.
