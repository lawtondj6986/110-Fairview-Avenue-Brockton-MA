## What it means

Percentage rent is an additional rent equal to a percentage of the tenant's gross sales over an agreed breakpoint. It aligns landlord and tenant interests in a center's success and requires the tenant to report sales, which many small operators prefer to avoid.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore, percentage rent shows up mainly in larger centers along Route 18, Route 53, and the Route 3 corridor and in some restaurant deals. If a landlord proposes it for a small storefront, treat it as a lever: accept it only in exchange for a materially lower base rent.

## In practice

Base rent $60,000, percentage rent 6 percent over a $1,000,000 natural breakpoint. A tenant doing $1,300,000 in sales pays $18,000 in percentage rent for the year, $78,000 total.
