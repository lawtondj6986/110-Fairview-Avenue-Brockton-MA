## What it means

Ballot question research is the application of political research methods to an issue campaign rather than a candidate. It documents the landscape, who is for and against, who funds them, what they have said, and what happened when similar measures were tried, so a campaign can plan with evidence.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Massachusetts towns decide tax overrides, zoning changes, and local questions at the ballot every year, and the South Shore is no exception. ICS's issue landscapes serve committees and organizations on either side, built from public filings and the public record.
