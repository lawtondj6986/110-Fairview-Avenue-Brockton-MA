## What it means

The intelligence cycle is the standard model for research work: direction (what does the client need to decide), collection (gathering from public and licensed sources), processing (organizing and resolving entities), analysis (grading, corroborating, testing assumptions), dissemination (the brief), and feedback (what the client learned and what remains). It is a loop because the first answer usually sharpens the question.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A dossier that skips direction answers the wrong question; one that skips analysis is a document dump. ICS runs every engagement, from a 3-day counterparty check on a Rockland seller to a multi-week ballot question file, through the same cycle. Each stage has a lawful-methods check built in: public sources, no pretexting, no surveillance of private individuals, no employment, credit, or tenant screening uses.

## In practice

A lender needs to decide in 10 business days whether to fund a $3.1 million loan on a Brockton mixed-use building. Direction: 4 questions about the borrower's record and the property's title. Collection: 9 public sources over 5 days. Analysis: 2 findings corroborated, 1 unresolved. Dissemination: a 6-page brief with a BLUF. Feedback: the lender asks one follow-up on a lien, answered in 24 hours.
