## What it means

The Massachusetts Office of Campaign and Political Finance administers the state's campaign finance law and publishes contribution and expenditure data for state and most municipal candidates and committees. Its database is a foundational public source for political research and for understanding relationships around local development and contracting.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore zoning boards, select boards, and city councils decide what gets built. OCPF records show who funded whom, which is relevant to a candidate studying their own exposure, to a campaign studying an opponent, and sometimes to a business trying to understand the landscape around a permit.
