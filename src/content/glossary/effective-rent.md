## What it means

Effective rent is the true average rent over a lease once concessions are spread across the term. The face rate, or asking rent, is what the lease states. The effective rate is what the deal is actually worth per square foot per year.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Landlords in softer segments, such as suburban office in Quincy and Braintree, often protect the face rate and give ground on free rent and allowances, because face rates feed appraisals and the next comparable. A tenant comparing a space in Hanover with one in Rockland on asking rent alone can pick the more expensive deal. Effective rent puts both offers on one line.

## In practice

A tenant leases 4,000 square feet for 5 years at $20 a square foot with 4 months free. Total rent is $400,000 less $26,667, or $373,333. Divide by 5 years and 4,000 feet: $18.67 effective. A competing offer at $19 with no free rent costs more, even though its face rate is lower.
