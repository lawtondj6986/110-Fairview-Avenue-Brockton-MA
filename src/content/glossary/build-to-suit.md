## What it means

In a build-to-suit the landlord develops a building, or substantially renovates one, to the tenant's design, and the tenant signs a long-term lease that repays the investment. The tenant gets a custom facility without owning it; the landlord gets a long, predictable income stream.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Land in the South Shore's commercial districts is scarce, so true ground-up build-to-suits are rare below a few million dollars. More common is a landlord gutting and rebuilding an older building on Route 28 or Route 139 for a single tenant on a fifteen-year lease.

## In practice

A regional veterinary group signs a fifteen-year lease on a Pembroke parcel. The landlord builds a 6,000 square foot clinic to the group's plans; rent is set to amortize the cost at an agreed return.
