## What it means

A curb cut is the point where a driveway crosses the curb and sidewalk to connect a property to a public road. Because it affects traffic safety and flow, adding or altering one requires a permit from the road's owner, and on state highways that permission is difficult to obtain.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Much of the South Shore's commercial frontage sits on state-numbered routes, where the state highway department controls access. A buyer planning to reconfigure a site should confirm what the existing curb cuts permit before assuming a new layout is possible.

## In practice

A buyer of a Route 28 parcel in Brockton plans a drive-through that needs a second access point. The state denies the new curb cut. The site plan is redesigned around the existing one, with a longer stacking lane.
