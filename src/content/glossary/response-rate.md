## What it means

Response rate is the share of messages that receive a reply. It is measured in two directions. The business's response rate is the percentage of inbound calls, forms, texts, and messages that someone answered. The customer's response rate is the percentage of the business's outbound texts or emails that got a reply back.

## Why it matters for a local business

Most owners believe they answer everything. The logs usually say otherwise: web forms that sat over a weekend, social messages nobody checks, calls that hit a full voicemail box. Each one is a lead that was paid for and dropped. Lead Engine puts every channel in one inbox and sends an instant first reply, so the inbound response rate is 100% even when the crew is on a roof in Plymouth.

## In practice

A Brockton auto body shop gets 120 inquiries a month across phone, forms, and messages. An audit shows 84 were answered, a 70% response rate. With missed-call text-back and one shared inbox, the rate reaches 100%. Of the 36 recovered inquiries, 9 become repair jobs averaging $1,800.
