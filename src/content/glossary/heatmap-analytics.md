## What it means

Heatmap analytics is a tool that records where visitors click, tap, move, and scroll on a web page and renders the results as a color overlay: red where attention concentrates, blue where it fades. Most heatmap tools also offer scroll maps, which show how far down the page people reach, and session recordings, which replay individual visits anonymously.

## Why it matters for a local business

Most small-business websites were designed to look good to the owner, not to convert a visitor on a phone at 7 p.m. A heatmap exposes the gap. A Plymouth roofer's homepage may bury the "get an estimate" button under three paragraphs about the company's history that almost nobody reads. ICS runs heatmaps on JobSite landing pages after launch and again after each change, because the data settles arguments about layout faster than opinion does.

## In practice

A Brockton dental practice's new-patient page had a 2.1 percent conversion rate. The scroll map showed 64 percent of mobile visitors never reached the booking form, which sat below a 900-word insurance section. The click map showed heavy tapping on an unlinked "Book now" graphic. ICS moved the booking link into the header, made the graphic a working button, and collapsed the insurance text. Conversion rose to 5.8 percent within a month, about 11 additional new patients.
