## What it means

In a sublease the tenant becomes a landlord to a subtenant for some or all of the premises and some or all of the remaining term, while the original lease with the building owner stays in force. It is the most common way a growing or shrinking business manages space it no longer fits.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore small businesses move for growth more often than failure, and a two-year tail on an old lease is a common drag. The flexibility to sublease the old bay while you build out the new one is worth negotiating on day one, when you have leverage.

## In practice

A Hanover contractor with three years left on a 2,500 square foot bay moves to a 6,000 square foot building and subleases the bay to an electrician at the same rent, with the landlord's written consent.
