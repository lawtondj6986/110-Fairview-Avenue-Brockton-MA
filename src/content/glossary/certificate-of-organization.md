## What it means

A certificate of organization is the formation document for a Massachusetts limited liability company, filed with the Corporations Division of the Secretary of the Commonwealth. It sets out the LLC's name, address, resident agent, managers, and the people authorized to sign for it. It is public, searchable online by entity or individual name, and free to view.

## Why it matters in a dossier

It is the starting point for figuring out who is behind an LLC. The formation date, the organizer, the address, and the real estate signatory all connect to other records. The same address or signatory across six LLCs is how a portfolio gets mapped. What it cannot do is prove ownership: members are frequently not listed. We say that plainly in the report.

## In practice

A Brockton mixed-use building is owned by an LLC with a generic name. The certificate, filed 9 years ago, lists one manager and one real estate signatory at a Rockland office address. Searching that signatory by individual name returns 7 more LLCs. Matching those to registry records takes about 2 hours and produces a sourced portfolio map.
