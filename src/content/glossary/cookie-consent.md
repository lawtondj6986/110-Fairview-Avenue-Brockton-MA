## What it means

Cookie consent is the practice of asking a visitor for permission before a website stores non-essential cookies on their device, usually the ones used for analytics and advertising. It normally appears as a banner with accept and decline choices. Cookies the site needs to function, such as keeping a booking form working, are generally treated differently from tracking cookies.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Weymouth contractor serving only local homeowners faces different obligations than a Boca Raton practice with seasonal patients from Europe and Canada. The rules vary by state and country and keep changing, so this is a question for your attorney, not a plugin default. The practical point: a banner that fires tracking before the visitor chooses is worse than no banner, because it claims a choice the site does not honor.

## In practice

A South Shore med spa adds a proper consent banner. About 35% of visitors decline analytics cookies, so reported sessions fall from 4,000 to 2,600 a month while bookings stay flat at 85. The owner stops judging marketing by sessions and tracks booked appointments by source instead.
