## What it means

Cost segregation is an engineering-based tax analysis that separates a building's components into asset classes with shorter recovery periods than the standard 39-year commercial schedule. The result is accelerated depreciation, lower taxable income in the early years of ownership, and improved after-tax cash flow.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Owner-users buying their South Shore building and investors acquiring income property both benefit, and few are told about it. We raise it in every acquisition conversation and leave the analysis to the client's tax advisor, where it belongs.
