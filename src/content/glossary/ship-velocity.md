## What it means

Build velocity is the rate at which a firm converts agreed work into live, measurable results. For a client, it determines how quickly an investment starts returning and how responsive a partner will be when the market shifts. It is a function of scope discipline and accountability, not headcount.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS is one builder with AI tooling, which is why a South Shore client's requests do not enter a queue behind forty other accounts. The 21-day JobSite guarantee is the public commitment; the monthly review is where the rest of it shows.
