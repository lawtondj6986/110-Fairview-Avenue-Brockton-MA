## What it means

Topical authority is the credibility a website earns with search engines by covering a subject thoroughly, accurately, and in an interconnected way. Rather than one page targeting one keyword, the site becomes the place that answers every reasonable question in a topic cluster. Search engines reward that depth with rankings across the whole cluster, including for pages that have few backlinks of their own.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Plymouth septic contractor competing for "septic system replacement Plymouth" is fighting national lead-gen sites with huge link profiles. Topical authority is how a small firm wins anyway: pages on Title 5 inspections, pumping schedules, leach field failure signs, costs by system type, and town-by-town permit notes, all linked together. ICS builds JobSite content around topic clusters, not keyword lists, and feeds the same answers into structured data so AI Overviews and chat tools cite the client.

## In practice

A Hanover HVAC company had a 6-page site and ranked for its own name and little else. Over eight months ICS built 34 pages: 9 service pages, 12 town pages, and 13 question pages (heat pump rebates in Massachusetts, when to replace versus repair, oil-to-gas conversion costs). No paid links. Organic search visits rose from about 400 to 2,900 a month, the site ranked top three for 41 service-and-town combinations, and organic calls went from 11 to 48 a month.
