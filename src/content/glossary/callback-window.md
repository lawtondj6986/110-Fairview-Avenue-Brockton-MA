## What it means

A callback window is the time frame you tell a customer to expect a return call: "within 15 minutes," "by end of day," "between 2 and 4." It is a promise with a clock on it. A window that is stated and kept builds trust. A vague "we'll get back to you" does not count as one.

## Why it matters for a local business

A homeowner with a leaking water heater in Brockton is not waiting. If your voicemail says nothing about when you will call, the next plumber gets the job. Lead Engine sends an immediate text on a missed call that states the window, then tracks whether the callback happened inside it. That turns a promise into something you can measure by day and by employee.

## In practice

A Quincy electrician sets a 15-minute callback window from 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. and a 9 a.m. next-day window after hours. In the first month, 82 of 96 missed calls are returned inside the window, or 85 percent. The 14 misses cluster between noon and 1 p.m., which tells the owner exactly where to add lunch coverage.
