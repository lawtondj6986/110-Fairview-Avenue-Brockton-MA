## What it means

Zoning divides a town into districts and sets what uses, building sizes, setbacks, parking counts, and signage are permitted in each. A use that is allowed by right can proceed with a building permit; others need discretionary approvals or are simply not allowed.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The South Shore is 29 towns with 29 bylaws. The same auto repair use is by right on one stretch of Route 28 in Brockton and needs a special permit a mile away in Avon. Confirming the use for the specific parcel is the first thing we do on any site search, before anyone falls in love with a building.

## In practice

A restaurant buyer finds the perfect Hanson building, then learns the district requires a special permit for food service with a liquor license. The deal proceeds with a permitting contingency and a six-month timeline instead of a sixty-day one.
