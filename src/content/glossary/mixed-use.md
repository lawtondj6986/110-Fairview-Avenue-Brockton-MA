## What it means

Mixed-use buildings stack uses vertically: commercial at street level, residential or office above. They are the historic fabric of New England downtowns and an increasingly favored form in zoning reform because they put housing where services already are.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The South Shore's downtown blocks are a mixed-use market in their own right, trading largely among local investors. ICS represents buyers and sellers of these buildings and brings the same institutional-style offering memorandum to a three-unit Whitman building that a larger firm would reserve for a shopping center.

## In practice

A three-story Rockland building with a storefront insurance office and four apartments produces $118,000 in gross rent and sells for $1,150,000, a GRM of 9.7 and a cap rate near 7 percent after realistic expenses.
