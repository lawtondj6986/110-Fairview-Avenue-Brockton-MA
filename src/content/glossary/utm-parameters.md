## What it means

UTM parameters are short text tags appended to a URL that identify the source, medium, campaign, and content of the link. Analytics tools read them and attribute the visit accordingly, which is how a business learns which marketing actually produced the lead.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Every link ICS deploys for a client carries them, including the QR codes on truck wraps and yard signs. When a Pembroke roofer asks whether the yard signs work, the dashboard answers with a number.
