## What it means

Need-to-know is the principle that access to sensitive information is granted only to those whose role requires it, regardless of seniority or curiosity. In an intelligence practice it governs who receives a report, how it is transmitted, and what is said about the engagement afterward: as little as possible.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

For a South Shore owner commissioning a quiet check on a partner or a candidate studying their own record, the value of the work depends on nobody knowing it happened. ICS's handling rules exist to protect that, and they are published so clients can hold the firm to them.
