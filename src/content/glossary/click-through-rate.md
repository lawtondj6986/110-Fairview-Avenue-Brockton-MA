## What it means

Click-through rate is clicks divided by impressions, expressed as a percentage. If 1,000 people see your ad and 60 click, the CTR is 6 percent. It applies to search ads, organic listings, Business Profile results, emails, and social posts, and it measures how well the message matches what the viewer wanted at that moment.

## Why it matters for a local business

A local business pays for impressions in attention even when it does not pay in dollars. A Weymouth electrician whose ad says "Licensed electrician, Weymouth, same-day service" will outpull a generic "Electrical services" headline every time, because it answers the searcher's question before the click. ICS tests headlines in pairs inside Lead Engine campaigns and keeps the winner, and tracks CTR by town so a Market Lock territory is not paying for clicks from Boston.

## In practice

A Brockton auto repair shop ran two search ads for "brake repair Brockton." The generic version earned a 2.8 percent CTR. The version with the town name, an "$89 brake inspection" offer, and the review count earned 7.4 percent. On 4,000 monthly impressions that is 184 more clicks, and because the cost per click fell as the ad quality rose, the cost per booked job dropped from $62 to $38.
