## What it means

A commercial appraisal is an independent, licensed valuation prepared to a regulated standard, typically using the income approach, the sales comparison approach, and sometimes the cost approach, then reconciling them. Lenders rely on it to set the loan amount; buyers and sellers encounter it as the step that can reprice a deal late.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore appraisers work with thin comparable data, and reasonable professionals can land $100,000 apart on the same building. A well-documented offering memorandum and a current rent roll give the appraiser what they need to support the contract price rather than guess below it.

## In practice

A $1,250,000 contract on a Weymouth building appraises at $1,180,000. The lender's 75 percent loan drops by $52,500. The buyer's broker submits two additional recent sales the appraiser missed, and the value is revised to $1,235,000.
