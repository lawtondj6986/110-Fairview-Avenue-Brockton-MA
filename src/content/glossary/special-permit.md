## What it means

A special permit authorizes a use or dimension that the zoning bylaw allows at the board's discretion, usually with conditions on hours, traffic, screening, or operations. It is the normal path for many small commercial uses such as auto repair, drive-throughs, daycare, and self-storage.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

In South Shore towns the granting authority is often the planning board for commercial projects and the zoning board for others; the bylaw says which. The hearing is public and neighbors come. The strongest applications we have seen were the ones where the applicant met the abutters before the hearing, not at it.

## In practice

A Bridgewater contractor buys a yard that allows contractor storage only by special permit. The application proposes a fence, screened dumpster, and 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. hours; the board approves with those conditions written in.
