## What it means

Chapter 40B, the comprehensive permit law, allows developers of qualifying affordable housing to obtain a single permit from the zoning board of appeals that overrides local zoning in municipalities where less than 10 percent of housing is affordable. It is a powerful tool that affects the highest and best use of land across the state.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Several South Shore towns sit below the threshold, which means aging commercial parcels on their corridors may be worth more as 40B housing sites than as retail or industrial. An owner deciding whether to sell should know whether their town is subject to 40B and whether their parcel is a candidate.
