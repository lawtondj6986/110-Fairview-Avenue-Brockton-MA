## What it means

Debt service coverage ratio measures how comfortably a property's income covers its loan payments. A ratio of 1.0 means income exactly equals debt service; lenders require more, so a shortfall in rent or a surprise expense does not immediately threaten the payment.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore, DSCR often binds before loan-to-value does: a lender willing to go to 75 percent of price may still cut the loan because the building's income cannot carry it at today's rates. Knowing your DSCR before you offer tells you how much cash you really need.

## In practice

NOI $90,000, proposed loan payments $75,000 a year: DSCR is 1.20. The lender wants 1.25, so the loan shrinks until payments are $72,000, and the buyer brings about $40,000 more cash to close.
