## What it means

Frontage is the linear measure of a parcel's edge along a public way. In commercial real estate it is shorthand for visibility and access: how much of the property passing traffic can see and how easily it can reach it. It is a zoning measurement and a marketing attribute at once.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On the South Shore's commercial corridors, frontage on Route 18, Route 28, Route 53, Route 123, or Route 139 is what retail and service tenants pay for. Interior parcels and rear buildings rent for less and suit uses, such as contractors and warehousing, that do not need to be seen.

## In practice

Two similar 5,000 square foot buildings in Weymouth: one with 150 feet on Route 18 at a signalized intersection, one on a side street behind it. The first leases to a medical tenant at $26 NNN; the second leases to a contractor at $15 NNN.
