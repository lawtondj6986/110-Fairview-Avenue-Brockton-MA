## What it means

Rapid response is the real-time arm of campaign research: a standing capacity to verify, document, and contextualize claims as they arise so a campaign responds with facts rather than instinct. It depends on prior research being organized and on a disciplined process for sourcing under time pressure.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

For Massachusetts campaigns, ICS's desk runs from the opposition book and the client's own vulnerability study, with primary sources ready. A debate claim about a vote or a contribution is checked against the record and answered with a footnote, not a hunch.
