## What it means

An anchor tenant is a large, well-known retailer or service, such as a supermarket, pharmacy, or big-box store, whose presence draws customers to a shopping center and supports the rents of the smaller tenants around it. Anchors negotiate favorable terms in exchange for the traffic they generate.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore centers along Route 18, Route 53, and Route 3A are anchored by grocers, pharmacies, and home-improvement stores. For a small tenant choosing between two centers, the health and lease term of the anchor is often more important than the rent difference.

## In practice

A salon chooses a Hanover center anchored by a supermarket with twelve years left on its lease over a cheaper center whose anchor space has been dark for a year. Eighteen months later the cheaper center has lost two more tenants.
