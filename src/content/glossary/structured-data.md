## What it means

Structured data is a standardized vocabulary, schema.org, embedded in a web page's code to describe its content explicitly: this is a business, here are its hours, this is a question and its answer, this service costs this much. Search engines and AI systems read it to build answers and rich results.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

This site carries structured data for the business, the founder, every published price, every FAQ, every article, and every listing. ICS applies the same to client sites so that when an AI assistant is asked about a plumber in Hanson, the facts it needs are not inferred from prose but read directly.
