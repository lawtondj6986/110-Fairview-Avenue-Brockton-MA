## What it means

Amortization is the gradual repayment of a loan's principal through scheduled payments. Commercial loans are typically amortized over a longer period than their term, so a borrower makes payments sized for 25 years but must refinance or pay off the balance after 5, 7, or 10.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Local banks and credit unions on the South Shore commonly offer 5- or 10-year terms on 20- or 25-year amortizations. SBA 504 debentures fully amortize over 20 or 25 years with no balloon, which is one reason owner-users favor them.

## In practice

$800,000 at 7 percent: a 20-year amortization costs about $74,400 a year; a 25-year amortization costs about $67,800. The 25-year schedule improves DSCR from 1.21 to 1.33 on a $90,000 NOI.
