## What it means

A court records search checks the public dockets for every case in which a person or entity appears as a party. In Massachusetts the state system is MassCourts, the public case search covering District, Superior, Housing, Land, and Probate and Family Courts. Federal civil, criminal, and bankruptcy cases are on PACER. Both are public; PACER charges a small per-page fee.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Litigation history is the best public predictor of how a counterparty behaves under stress. A Brockton seller with four Superior Court contract suits in six years, or a prospective anchor tenant with a Chapter 11 in another district, changes the deal. ICS runs these searches on business counterparties and public-facing principals, and never for employment, credit, or tenant screening.

## In practice

Before a $1.8 million sale-leaseback in Rockland, the buyer asks for a docket check on the seller and its two managers. MassCourts returns 7 matters: 3 small claims as plaintiff, 2 evictions as landlord, 1 mechanic's lien discharged, and 1 open contract suit for $212,000 in Plymouth Superior Court. PACER is clean. The open suit goes into the purchase agreement as a seller representation and an indemnity.
