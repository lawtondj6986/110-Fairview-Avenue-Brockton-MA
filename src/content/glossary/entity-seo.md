## What it means

Entity SEO is the practice of establishing your business as a distinct entity in search engines' knowledge systems, with consistent attributes they can trust: name, location, services, people, credentials, and relationships. It relies on structured data, consistent citations, and authoritative references rather than repeating a keyword across pages.

## Why it matters for a local business

Search is shifting from ten blue links to direct answers, and answer engines pull from entities they recognize. A Hanover electrician who exists as a clear entity, with matching data on JobSite, Google, the state license lookup, and local directories, is far more likely to be named when someone asks an assistant for an electrician in Hanover. One whose name is spelled three ways across the web is a guess, and assistants do not like to guess.

## In practice

ICS audited a Rockland plumbing company and found four name variants, two phone numbers, and no schema. Over 60 days it standardized the name across 38 listings, added LocalBusiness and Service schema to the site, and linked the state license number. Within a quarter the company had a knowledge panel for its branded search and began appearing in AI-generated answers for "plumber Rockland MA," which it had never done before.
