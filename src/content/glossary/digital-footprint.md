## What it means

A digital footprint is everything a company or public-facing person has published or caused to be published online and that remains lawfully accessible: websites, domain registrations, business listings, press releases, public social media accounts, job postings, reviews, and archived versions of all of them. It does not include private accounts, hacked data, or anything obtained by deception.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A footprint shows what a party says about itself, and how that story has changed. A Hanover contractor whose site claimed "since 1998" in 2022 and "since 1991" in 2024 is worth a second look. ICS maps footprints for business entities and public figures in public roles, not private individuals, and uses the result for commercial and civic questions only, never for employment, credit, or tenant screening.

## In practice

A landlord in Weymouth is weighing a 5-year lease with a tenant who claims 12 locations. The footprint check finds 4 live location pages, 3 of them with no Google Business Profile, a domain registered 19 months ago, and archived snapshots showing a different company name on the same site in 2023. The landlord asks for a personal guaranty and 6 months of security instead of 2.
