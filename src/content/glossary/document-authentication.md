## What it means

Document authentication is confirming that a document presented in a deal or a dispute matches the version held by the issuing authority. A deed is checked against the Registry of Deeds book and page. A certificate of good standing is checked against the Secretary of the Commonwealth. A judgment is checked against the docket. The question is not whether the document looks right but whether the official public record agrees with it.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Data rooms are assembled by the party selling. Most errors are honest: an unsigned amendment, a superseded plan, a lease missing its exhibit. Some are not. A Brockton buyer who closes on a "recorded" easement that was never actually recorded owns a problem. ICS authenticates against public registries and licensed databases only, never by pretext, and flags every mismatch.

## In practice

A seller's data room for a Plymouth retail property includes a 2019 lease amendment extending the anchor tenant to 2031. The recorded notice of lease at the Registry shows the original 2026 expiration and no amendment. The estoppel certificate from the tenant confirms 2026. The 5-year difference moves the buyer's valuation by roughly $340,000, and the price follows.
