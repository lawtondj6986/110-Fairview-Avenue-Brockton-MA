## What it means

Marketing automation is the use of software to trigger communications based on behavior and time: a form submission, a missed call, a quote that has not been accepted, a job that just finished. It is how a small team delivers the consistency of a large one.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore businesses lose work not for lack of leads but for lack of follow-through. Automation is the follow-through: the second, third, and fourth touch that converts a quote into a job while the owner is on a roof or in a treatment room.

## In practice

A Plymouth dental practice automates appointment reminders, post-visit review requests, and six-month recall. Review count triples in a quarter, and recall bookings fill the schedule that used to have gaps.
