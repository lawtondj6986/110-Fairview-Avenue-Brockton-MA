## What it means

A long-tail keyword is a search phrase that is longer and more specific than a broad head term. "Dentist" is a head term. "Emergency dentist open Saturday Quincy" is long-tail. Each one is searched only a handful of times a month, but together these specific phrases make up most searches, and they state exactly what the person wants.

## Why it matters for a local business

A small company will not outrank national directories for "plumber". It can own "tankless water heater install Hanover MA". Local long-tail phrases have little competition and strong intent, and they are also how people phrase questions to voice assistants and AI search tools. JobSite service and town pages are written to answer these specific searches one page at a time.

## In practice

A Brockton garage door company builds 12 pages for phrases like "broken garage door spring repair Brockton". Each phrase gets only 20 to 50 searches a month. Together the pages bring 310 visits and 28 calls monthly at a 9% call rate, against 2% on the home page's broad traffic.
