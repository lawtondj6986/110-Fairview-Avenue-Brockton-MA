## What it means

An omnichannel inbox is a shared software view where every inbound message from every channel lands in one place: SMS, phone call notes and voicemails, website chat, email, Google Business Profile messages, Facebook and Instagram. Each customer gets one thread regardless of how they reached out, and staff reply from the same screen in the channel the customer used.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Hanover dental office hears from patients by phone, text, the website form, Facebook Messenger, and Google. Without a shared inbox, the front desk checks five tabs between patients and misses the Google message from a new family that moved to town. ICS makes the inbox the center of Lead Engine. Every conversation, whether started by an AI chat agent, a missed-call text, or a human, lives there, so the owner can see at 6 p.m. exactly what is still waiting for an answer.

## In practice

A Plymouth auto body shop consolidated its channels into one inbox and set a 10-minute response target during business hours. Before, the shop estimated it answered Facebook and Google messages "within a day or two." After, median response time across all channels was 6 minutes. Monthly estimate requests rose from 44 to 67 with the same ad budget, and the owner stopped checking his personal phone for shop messages at dinner.
