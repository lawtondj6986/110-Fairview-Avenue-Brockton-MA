## What it means

Follow-up cadence defines the rhythm of outreach after a lead or quote: the sequence of days and channels. A deliberate cadence, executed by automation with human steps at the right points, is the difference between a business that converts a quarter of its quotes and one that converts half.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS writes the cadence into every Lead Engine and reports quote conversion monthly. For a South Shore business, the gain is rarely from more leads; it is from no longer losing the ones already quoted.
