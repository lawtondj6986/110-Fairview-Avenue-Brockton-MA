## What it means

A loading dock is a raised platform at trailer bed height, with or without levelers, for unloading semi-trailers. A drive-in door is a grade-level overhead door that allows vehicles to enter the building. Which a building has, and how many, determines what operations it can support.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most sub-10,000 square foot industrial buildings in Avon, Rockland, Hanover, and Stoughton offer drive-in doors, which suits the contractors and service companies who dominate the market. A tenant needing dock-high loading is searching a much smaller inventory and should start early.

## In practice

An HVAC contractor needs two drive-in doors to house vans and receive equipment; a 5,000 square foot Avon bay with two 12-foot doors is ideal. A regional distributor needing two docks finds nothing suitable under 20,000 square feet in the same towns.
