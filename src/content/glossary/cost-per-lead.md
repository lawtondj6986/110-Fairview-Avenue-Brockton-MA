## What it means

Cost per lead is a channel efficiency metric: what it costs, on average, to produce one inquiry from a given source. It is the first step toward understanding acquisition economics but incomplete on its own, since a cheap lead that never books is not cheap.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS dashboards show cost per lead by source for every client, and the pattern across South Shore trades is consistent: paid leads cost real money, organic and review-driven leads cost almost nothing marginally, and the system that converts both is where the return lives.
