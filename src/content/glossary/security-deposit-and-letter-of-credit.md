## What it means

A security deposit is cash the tenant posts and the landlord holds against default and damage. A letter of credit is a bank instrument that serves the same purpose without tying up cash. Both are negotiated on amount, conditions for drawing, and whether they step down over the term as the tenant proves itself.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore landlords of small buildings almost always take cash deposits and often ask for two or three months from a new business. Tenants should negotiate a step-down, with a month returned after twelve or twenty-four months of on-time payment, and interest or a defined account where the lease is long.
