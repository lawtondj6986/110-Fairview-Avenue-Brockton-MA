## What it means

Click-to-call is a phone number or button on a website, ad, or business listing that dials the business when tapped on a mobile phone. It removes the step of memorizing or copying a number. Each tap can also be counted as a conversion event, so the business knows which pages and ads produce calls.

## Why it matters for a local business

For urgent trades, the phone call is the sale. Most local searches happen on a phone, and a visitor who has to pinch, zoom, and copy a number often taps the back button and calls the next result instead. Every JobSite build puts a click-to-call button in a fixed bar on mobile. Pair it with missed-call text-back, because a tap that reaches voicemail is still a lost lead.

## In practice

A Rockland towing company gets 900 mobile visits a month. With the number as plain text in the footer, 27 visitors call, or 3%. After adding a sticky call button, 63 call, or 7%. At a $140 average tow and a 70% close rate, those 36 extra calls are worth about $3,500 a month.
