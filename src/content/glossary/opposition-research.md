## What it means

Opposition research is the disciplined compilation and analysis of everything in the public record about a political opponent, organized so a campaign can make decisions and respond to attacks with documentation. The product is a research book: findings, sources, context, and an honest assessment of what each item is worth.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS's political desk serves Massachusetts races, from select board to legislature, under a methods code published on this site. Every finding is footnoted to a primary source. The same standards apply when the client is studying themselves.
