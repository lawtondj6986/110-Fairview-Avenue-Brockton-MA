## What it means

A warranty callback is a second trip to a finished job to correct a defect covered by the company's workmanship warranty or a manufacturer's part warranty. The customer pays nothing. The business absorbs the labor and drive time, and it loses the paid job that slot would have held.

## Why it matters for a local business

Callbacks are a hidden cost that rarely shows up in pricing. One callback can erase the profit on three good jobs once the lost slot is counted. They are also where reputations are made: a homeowner in Duxbury who waits nine days for a callback writes the one-star review. Tracking callbacks by tech and job type in the same system as bookings shows the pattern. Lead Engine flags a repeat contact from a recent job so it gets priority.

## In practice

A Brockton plumbing company completes 2,400 jobs a year with 108 callbacks, or 4.5 percent. Each costs about $140 in labor and travel plus a $310 lost job slot: roughly $48,600 a year. Tracing 40 of them to one fitting supplier and one new hire, the owner changes both and brings the rate to 2.5 percent, saving about $21,600.
