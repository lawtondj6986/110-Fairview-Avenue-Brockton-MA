## What it means

Deferred maintenance is the accumulated backlog of upkeep and replacement an owner has put off. It is invisible in a seller's NOI, because money not spent does not appear as an expense, and it becomes the buyer's capital cost the day after closing unless it is found and priced during due diligence.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Owner-managed South Shore buildings held for decades often carry a long list: a roof patched rather than replaced, a parking lot past resurfacing, original electrical panels. A thorough inspection on a $1,200,000 building commonly produces $30,000 to $80,000 in documented items and a corresponding negotiation.

## In practice

An inspection of an East Bridgewater retail building identifies a roof with three years of life, two failing rooftop units, and a lot needing resurfacing, totaling $112,000 in estimates. The buyer negotiates a $70,000 credit and closes.
