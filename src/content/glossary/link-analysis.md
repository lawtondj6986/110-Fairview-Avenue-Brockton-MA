## What it means

Link analysis takes the relationships found in public records (this person manages that LLC, which owns that parcel, which was financed by that lender, which also financed this other entity) and lays them out as a network so patterns become visible. Each link is a documented record, not an inference. The chart is a reading aid; the evidence is the filings behind it.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A single Registry search tells you who owns one building. Link analysis tells you that the same three people, through 11 entities, control 30 parcels along Route 18 and share a registered agent with the firm bidding against your client. ICS maps business entities and public-role principals from registries, deeds, and filings; it does not map private individuals or their personal associations, and nothing is used for employment, credit, or tenant decisions.

## In practice

A town-meeting group asks who is behind a 40B proposal in Hanover. Secretary of the Commonwealth filings name 2 managers across 4 LLCs; the Registry links those LLCs to 7 land purchases totaling $6.4 million over 3 years; OCPF filings show $9,500 in contributions from the managers to 3 local campaigns. The chart fits one page, and every node cites a public record.
