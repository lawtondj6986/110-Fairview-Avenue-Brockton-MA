## What it means

Free rent is a period, typically at lease commencement, during which base rent is abated. It compensates the tenant for the time and cost of preparing the space and is one of the main concessions that make a lease's headline rent differ from its net effective rent.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore landlords compete on concessions more than on face rent, and free rent is the most common one. Two free months on a five-year lease on 2,000 square feet at $20 is worth about $6,700, or roughly $0.67 a foot off the effective rate, which our Lease Deal Analyzer computes automatically.
