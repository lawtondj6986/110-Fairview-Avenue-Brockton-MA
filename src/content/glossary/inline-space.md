## What it means

Inline space refers to the standard storefront units within the main building of a shopping center, flanked by other tenants. They share a facade, a parking field, and usually a pylon sign, and they trade visibility for lower rent compared with end caps and freestanding pads.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore small-business retail leases are inline units in neighborhood centers. The rent difference between a center's inline and end-cap space often runs several dollars a foot, and for many service businesses the inline unit, with a good pylon panel, is the better value.
