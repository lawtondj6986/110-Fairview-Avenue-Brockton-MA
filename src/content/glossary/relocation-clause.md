## What it means

A relocation clause permits the landlord to require a tenant to move to different premises within the property during the term, typically to accommodate a larger tenant or a renovation. For a tenant, the risk is a less visible or less suitable space; the negotiation is about limits and costs.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Relocation clauses show up in small South Shore plazas because the landlord's attorney used a regional mall form. Most small landlords will strike it when asked, because they have no plan to use it. If they refuse, that itself is information.

## In practice

A tenant accepts a relocation clause limited to comparable frontage, with the landlord paying all costs including new signage and a sixty-day rent abatement for the disruption, and no relocation in the final eighteen months of the term.
