## What it means

A bankruptcy search checks federal court records for cases filed by or against a subject: Chapter 7 liquidations, Chapter 11 reorganizations, and Chapter 13 repayment plans. A proper search covers the individual, the operating company, prior entities, and name variations, and reads the docket instead of stopping at a hit or no-hit answer.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A filing is not a character judgment. Plenty of good operators went through one. What matters is the pattern and the candor. Two dissolved LLCs, each with a Chapter 7 and the same unpaid trade creditors, is a pattern. A single filing from 2010 that the subject discloses up front is context. We use these records for business due diligence only, never for employment, credit, or tenant screening.

## In practice

An investor is asked to put $250,000 into a restaurant group expanding to Quincy. The PACER search finds a prior entity, same principal, that filed Chapter 7 four years ago listing 31 unsecured creditors and $410,000 in debt. The pitch deck did not mention it. PACER charges were under $15. The investor asks the question directly and gets to weigh the answer.
