## What it means

Hood and ventilation refers to the kitchen exhaust system for cooking equipment: the hood over the line, grease duct, rooftop exhaust fan, makeup air unit that replaces the exhausted air, and the fire suppression system built into the hood. Cooking that produces grease vapor needs a grease-rated hood. Steam and heat alone need a lighter one.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

This is usually the most expensive single item in a restaurant build-out and the one most likely to kill a deal. Older downtown buildings in Brockton, Quincy, and Plymouth often have apartments or offices above the storefront, which makes the duct run long and costly. The lease must give roof access and penetration rights, say who maintains the roof seal, and address odor and noise. Fire department and building department sign-off are both required.

## In practice

A pizza operator compares two 1,800-square-foot spaces in Quincy. One is second-generation with a 14-foot hood in place. The other is vanilla shell with two floors above, where a new hood, a 40-foot duct run, makeup air, and suppression are quoted at $68,000. The first rents for $4 a square foot more, or $7,200 a year. Over a ten-year term the existing hood still wins.
