## What it means

Corporate registry research reconstructs a person's business footprint from public entity filings: what they formed, when, with whom, what happened to it. It is the skeleton of counterparty due diligence and the primary way beneficial ownership is mapped when no one has to disclose it.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Massachusetts filings are the starting point for a South Shore counterparty, but many have Florida, New Hampshire, or Delaware entities as well. ICS searches across jurisdictions and presents the result as a single timeline, graded and footnoted.
