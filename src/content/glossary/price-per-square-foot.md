## What it means

Price per square foot divides a price or an annual rent by the property's square footage to produce a comparable unit figure. It is the most quoted number in commercial real estate and one of the least informative on its own, because it strips away everything that makes one building different from another.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore buyers often anchor on a per-square-foot figure they heard about a different type of building in a different town. We put the number in context: what it was for comparable buildings on the same corridor, and what the subject's income, land, and condition do to it.

## In practice

A 5,000 square foot Stoughton building at $1,000,000 is $200 per square foot. On a one-acre lot with excess yard, the land alone explains part of the premium over a $160 per square foot comp on a quarter acre.
