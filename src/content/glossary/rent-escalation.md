## What it means

Rent escalation clauses set how and when rent rises over the lease. Fixed percentage increases, fixed dollar steps, and index-linked increases are the common forms. Over a long term, the escalation schedule can matter as much as the starting rent.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore landlords ask for 3 percent annually; many settle for 2.5 or for a flat step every other year. On a ten-year lease the difference between 2 and 3 percent is roughly 10 percent of the final year's rent, so the clause deserves attention.

## In practice

A $48,000 starting rent with 3 percent annual escalations reaches $62,600 in year ten. At 2 percent it reaches $57,400. Over the term the difference is about $27,000.
