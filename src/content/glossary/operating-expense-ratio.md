## What it means

The operating expense ratio divides a property's operating expenses by its effective gross income. It offers a fast check on whether the expenses in a seller's statement are plausible and whether a building is expensive to run relative to its peers, with the caveat that lease structure changes the baseline.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore sellers' statements commonly understate expenses, and the ratio is the quickest way to notice. When a gross-leased mixed-use building shows a 25 percent ratio, the missing management, reserve, and snow lines are usually worth a conversation before the offer.
