## What it means

A meta description is a one or two sentence summary of a web page placed in the page's code. Search engines may display it as the gray text under the blue title in a search result. It is not visible on the page itself. Around 150 to 160 characters is the practical length before it gets cut off.

## Why it matters for a local business

It is ad copy you do not pay for. Two plumbers rank side by side for a Weymouth search. One snippet reads "Home | Welcome to our website". The other reads "Licensed Weymouth plumber. Same-day repairs, upfront pricing, 400+ local reviews. Call or book online." The second gets the click. Every JobSite page ships with its own written description, not a duplicated default.

## In practice

A Plymouth chiropractor's new-patient page sits at position 4 with 1,500 impressions a month and a 2.1% click-through rate, about 32 clicks. Rewriting the description to mention same-week appointments and accepted insurance lifts it to 3.6%, or 54 clicks, with no change in ranking.
