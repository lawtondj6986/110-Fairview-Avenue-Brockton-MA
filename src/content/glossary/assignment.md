  - q: "What is the difference between assignment and sublease?"

## What it means

Assignment transfers all of a tenant's rights and obligations under a lease to a new party. It is the mechanism by which a business sale carries the location with it. Landlords typically require consent and often use the moment to update terms or confirm the new tenant's credit.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

When a South Shore restaurant, salon, or shop changes hands, the lease assignment is usually the slowest piece of the sale. Starting the landlord conversation early, with the buyer's financials ready, keeps a sixty-day business sale from becoming a four-month one.

## In practice

A Plymouth salon owner sells the business. The landlord consents to assigning the remaining four years to the buyer, keeps the seller liable for twelve months as a bridge, and releases her after that.
