## What it means

First-party data is the information a business collects directly from its own customers and prospects, as opposed to data bought from or inferred by third parties. As privacy rules tighten and tracking weakens, it has become the durable foundation of effective marketing.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Every ICS system puts the client's data in accounts the client owns, by contract and by design. A South Shore business that leaves ICS takes its CRM, its list, its reviews, and its site. That is the ownership guarantee, and first-party data is most of what it protects.
