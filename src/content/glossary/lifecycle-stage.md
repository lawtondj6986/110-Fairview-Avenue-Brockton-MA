## What it means

A lifecycle stage is a field in a CRM that records how far a contact has progressed in their relationship with the business. Typical stages run from subscriber or lead, through qualified lead and opportunity, to customer, then repeat customer, inactive, or lost. The stage changes as the contact takes actions, and each stage can trigger different messages and tasks.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Weymouth electrician has one list of 2,400 names in his phone and no idea who is a past customer, who got a quote and vanished, and who is a supplier. Lifecycle stages sort that pile into groups you can act on. Leads from last month need a follow-up. Customers from 18 months ago need a reminder that panels and smoke detectors age. ICS sets stages in Lead Engine so automation fires by stage instead of blasting everyone the same message.

## In practice

A Rockland landscaping company tagged its 1,900 contacts by stage. It found 410 leads who had received a quote in the past two years and never booked, and 680 one-time customers who had not returned. A three-text sequence to the stale leads booked 23 jobs averaging $1,800. A spring cleanup offer to the inactive customers brought back 94 of them. Together that was roughly $130,000 in work from contacts the company already had.
