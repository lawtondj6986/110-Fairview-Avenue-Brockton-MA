## What it means

In a triple net lease the tenant reimburses the landlord for the three big ownership costs, usually as a per-square-foot estimate billed monthly and reconciled once a year. The landlord's rent is therefore mostly net, which is why investors like NNN buildings and why their cap rates are lower.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore retail and medical leases are NNN or close to it. Industrial and small office space is more often modified gross. Whatever the label, read the expense definitions: a lease that calls itself NNN but excludes roof and structure from the tenant's share is a very different deal from one that includes them.

## In practice

A 2,400 square foot storefront at $20 NNN with $7 in NNN charges costs $64,800 a year, or $5,400 a month, before utilities and before any annual escalation.
