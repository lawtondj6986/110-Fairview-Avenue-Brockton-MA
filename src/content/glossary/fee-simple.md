## What it means

Fee simple, or fee simple absolute, is full ownership of real estate with no end date. The owner holds every right the law recognizes in the property, subject to public rules and whatever is recorded against the title. It is what most people mean when they say they own a building.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most commercial sales in Brockton, Plymouth, and the towns between are fee simple, but not all. Pad sites in front of shopping centers are often ground leased, so the bank or restaurant owns its building and rents the dirt. Commercial condominium units are fee ownership of a unit plus a share of common areas. Know which interest is being sold before comparing prices.

## In practice

Two coffee drive-thru properties on Route 139 are offered at similar income. One is fee simple at $2,400,000. The other is a leasehold with 22 years left on the ground lease at $1,500,000. The second buyer owns nothing when the ground lease ends. A title search at the registry of deeds confirms which is which.
