## What it means

A timeline gap is a period in a chronology where nothing documents what a person or company was doing. It shows up once events are laid out by date: a business history that stops in 2014 and resumes in 2018, or a property that changes hands with no recorded deed in between.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Laying out a chronology is how missing pieces become visible. A gap often marks where the research has not looked yet: another jurisdiction, a prior entity name, a predecessor company. Sometimes it marks the thing the subject left out of the pitch. Either way, an honest dossier states the gap, says what was searched, and does not fill it with guesses.

## In practice

A prospective partner's bio lists ventures from 2008 to 2015 and from 2019 on. Massachusetts records show nothing for the 4 years between. The dossier flags the gap. A Florida corporate search then finds 2 entities in Broward County formed in 2015 and dissolved in 2018, one with a $120,000 judgment. The gap is closed with records, and the client has a specific question to ask. The report also lists both states searched.
