## What it means

Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40A is the Zoning Act, the enabling statute under which municipalities regulate land use. It defines the mechanics of zoning bylaws, boards of appeals, special permits, variances, nonconforming uses, and appeals, creating the framework within which each town's local bylaw operates.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The protection of pre-existing nonconforming uses is the 40A provision that most affects South Shore commercial deals: an auto repair shop or contractor's yard that predates the current bylaw can continue, but changing or expanding it triggers a review.
