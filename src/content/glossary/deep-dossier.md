## What it means

The Deep Dossier is the top tier of ICS's intelligence practice: a comprehensive, analytically disciplined file on a person, entity, or deal, built from public sources and lawful methods and presented with the tradecraft of a professional intelligence product. The fictional sample on this site shows every element of the format.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

When a South Shore deal or race is large enough that being wrong about a person would be expensive, the dossier is the appropriate instrument. It is delivered under need-to-know handling, with a conflict check first and a published methods code throughout.
