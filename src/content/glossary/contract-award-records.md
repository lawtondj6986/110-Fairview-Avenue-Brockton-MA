## What it means

Contract award records document the outcome of a government purchase: who got the contract, the amount, the start and end dates, and the awarding agency. They include the award notice, the signed contract, and later amendments. They are distinct from the procurement file, which covers how the decision was made.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Awards show who actually gets paid with public money, and how much of a vendor's business depends on one customer. For opposition and ballot research, they can be lined up against campaign finance filings by date. That comparison shows timing. It does not prove a connection, and we label it that way. For due diligence, awards verify a company's claimed government work.

## In practice

A paving company tells a prospective buyer that municipal work is 40 percent of revenue. We pull award records from 5 South Shore towns for 3 fiscal years and find $1.9 million in contracts, about 22 percent of the stated revenue, with 2 contracts expiring within 12 months. The buyer adjusts the valuation question before signing a letter of intent.
