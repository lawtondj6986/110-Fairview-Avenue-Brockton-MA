## What it means

The Massachusetts Land Court is a statewide trial court with jurisdiction over registered land and most real property disputes. Its records fall into two groups. Registration records, kept through each Registry of Deeds' registered land department, show certificates of title and the documents filed against them. Case records, searchable through the state trial court system, cover zoning appeals, tax takings, partition, and title litigation. Both are public.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A South Shore parcel may be registered land, recorded land, or a mix, and the research path differs. For registered land, the certificate of title at the Plymouth or Norfolk Registry's Land Court department is the authoritative owner record. For a zoning fight over a Route 3 site, the Land Court docket holds the full pleadings, the board's decision, and often the judge's findings about what was promised at hearings. ICS checks both before any property brief.

## In practice

A buyer's brief on a Plymouth site finds the parcel is registered land with a 2015 certificate of title and an encumbrance listed for a 20-foot drainage easement not mentioned in the offering memorandum. A separate Land Court docket shows a 2022 appeal of the site's special permit that was dismissed after 11 months. Both are disclosed to the buyer's counsel before the purchase and sale agreement is signed.
