## What it means

Territory exclusivity is a contractual promise by a marketing or lead-generation provider not to serve competing businesses in the same category within a defined geography for a defined term. It is common in franchising and rare in marketing, where serving many competitors in one vertical is the normal business model.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

ICS's Market Lock is exclusivity made concrete: 29 South Shore towns times twelve categories, 348 territories, each held by at most one business. The same structure runs in Broward County. The Territory Board on this site shows what is locked and what is open.
