## What it means

An owner-user purchases commercial property primarily to house its own operations rather than to collect rent from others. Owner-users value a building for how it serves the business, which lets them justify prices an investor cannot, and they qualify for financing programs investors do not.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The South Shore sub-$3M market is an owner-user market. Contractors, medical practices, auto shops, and small manufacturers buy the buildings they work in, often with SBA financing, and hold them for decades. That is why vacancies are rare and why the right building is worth moving quickly on.

## In practice

An HVAC company paying $7,500 a month in rent buys a $1,300,000 building with 10 percent down through an SBA 504 loan. Its total monthly occupancy cost rises slightly, and it now owns the asset and controls its future.
