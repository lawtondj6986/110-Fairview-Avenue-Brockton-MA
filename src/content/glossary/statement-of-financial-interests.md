## What it means

The Statement of Financial Interests is the annual disclosure required under Chapter 268B of the Massachusetts General Laws. Filers report sources of income over a threshold, business positions, real estate holdings other than a primary residence, securities, creditors, and gifts, in dollar ranges rather than exact amounts. The filing is public on request through the State Ethics Commission, which notifies the filer that a request was made.

## Why it matters in a dossier

An SFI is the fastest way to learn whether a state legislator holds property in a district or sits on a company's board. For a vulnerability study of a South Shore state representative, ICS compares five years of SFIs against Registry of Deeds records and corporate filings to catch what was reported late or not at all. Requesting an SFI is lawful and routine; the filer learns who requested it, so clients should expect that.

## In practice

A candidate's self-study finds that an LLC they joined in 2022 appears on the 2023 and 2024 SFIs but not the 2022 one. The gap is probably an oversight, but an opponent would frame it differently. The brief recommends a corrected filing before the campaign begins, at a cost of a few hours with counsel rather than a news cycle.
