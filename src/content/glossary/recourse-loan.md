## What it means

A recourse loan lets the lender look past the collateral to the borrower's or guarantor's other assets if the property does not repay the debt. In commercial lending the borrower is usually an LLC, so recourse comes through a personal guaranty signed by the owners. Full recourse covers the whole debt. Partial recourse covers a stated share.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

This is how most local commercial real estate is financed. Community banks and credit unions lending in Brockton, Plymouth, and Quincy typically require guaranties from anyone owning 20 percent or more, and SBA loans require them by rule. Owners sign them at closing with little discussion. The guaranty is worth reading as carefully as the rate.

## In practice

Two partners each guarantee a $1,500,000 loan on a Hanover building, jointly and severally. The anchor tenant leaves, the loan defaults, and the foreclosure sale brings $1,150,000. The $350,000 deficiency plus costs can be collected from either partner in full, not half from each. The partner with more assets is the easier target.
