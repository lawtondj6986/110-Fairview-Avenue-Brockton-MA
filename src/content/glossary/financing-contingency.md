## What it means

A financing contingency makes the buyer's obligation conditional on securing a mortgage commitment on defined terms by a defined date. It allocates the risk of the credit market to the seller during the window and to the buyer after it. Well-drafted, it names the amount, rate, and term so a bad offer counts as a failure.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Owner-user purchases on the South Shore frequently rely on SBA 504 loans, which involve two lenders and a longer approval path. Buyers should negotiate a contingency that fits that reality rather than accepting a residential-style thirty days and hoping.

## In practice

A buyer's contingency specifies a $900,000 loan at no more than 7.25 percent over 25 years within 60 days. The bank offers $820,000 at 7.5. The buyer may terminate, or use the shortfall to negotiate a price reduction.
