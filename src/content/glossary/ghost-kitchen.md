## What it means

A ghost kitchen, also called a dark kitchen or virtual kitchen, is a licensed commercial kitchen that sells only through delivery apps, online ordering, and pickup. It has no seating and little or no signage. One kitchen may run several brands from the same line.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

The real estate need is different from a restaurant. Visibility barely matters. Delivery radius, driver parking, and kitchen infrastructure matter a great deal. That makes flex space off Route 18 or a closed restaurant in Brockton workable at a fraction of prime retail rent. The hurdles are the board of health permit, the hood and grease trap, and a lease use clause that allows it. Landlords should think about driver traffic, odors, and trash before agreeing.

## In practice

An operator leases 1,200 square feet of second-generation restaurant space in Weymouth at $20 a square foot, or $24,000 a year, against $38 for comparable frontage space. The existing hood and grease trap save an estimated $70,000 in build-out. At $55,000 in monthly sales and a 22 percent average platform commission, $12,100 a month goes to the apps, six times the rent.
