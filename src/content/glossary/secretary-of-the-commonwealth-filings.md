## What it means

The Secretary of the Commonwealth's Corporations Division maintains the public record of Massachusetts business entities. Its filings establish who formed and manages an entity and whether it legally exists today, and they are the first stop in any ownership or counterparty inquiry.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Before ICS puts a South Shore client under contract with an LLC, the entity's status and filing history are checked. A seller entity revoked three years ago for non-filing is a closing problem waiting to happen, and a manager who runs six revoked entities is a pattern.
