## What it means

Deconfliction is the step before a research engagement is accepted: checking the subject, the client, and the opposing parties against the firm's existing and recent matters. If ICS is advising a buyer on a Quincy property, it will not take an assignment to research that buyer for the seller. Where a conflict exists, the work is declined or walled off with the client's informed consent.

## Why it matters in a dossier

The South Shore is a small market. The same 40 developers, lenders, and law firms show up in Brockton, Hanover, and Plymouth deals year after year. A dossier is only as trustworthy as the independence of the researcher who wrote it. Deconfliction is how ICS protects clients from a brief shaded by a relationship they did not know about, and it sits alongside the standing rules: public sources, no pretexting, no FCRA uses.

## In practice

A lender asks for a due-diligence brief on a borrower buying a 22,000 square foot Taunton warehouse. The deconfliction check shows ICS represented the seller's tenant in a lease renewal 9 months earlier. The lender is told, the prior client is told, and the engagement proceeds with a written conflict wall: no shared files, no shared staff, and the earlier matter's documents sealed from the new one.
