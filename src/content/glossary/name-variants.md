## What it means

Name variants are all the forms under which a person or business may appear in records: full name, initials, nicknames, maiden and married names, transliterations, common misspellings, and for companies, the legal name, trade names, and abbreviated versions. A search plan lists them before searching begins.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Two errors hide here. Search too narrowly and you miss the lawsuit filed under a misspelling. Match too loosely and you pin someone else's bankruptcy on your subject, which is worse. Common surnames on the South Shore make the second risk real. We document which variants were searched in which systems, so the client knows how far "nothing found" actually reaches.

## In practice

A subject does business as "Mike" but is "Michael J." on deeds and "M. Joseph" on one LLC filing. His company appears as an LLC, with and without a comma, and under a DBA filed in Rockland. We run 9 variants across 6 record systems. Seven of the 23 relevant records, including a $52,000 judgment, appear only under a non-primary form. The search log goes in the appendix: every variant, every system, every date searched, including the combinations that returned nothing.
