## What it means

Impression share is the number of times your ad was shown divided by the number of times it was eligible to be shown. If people in your target area made 1,000 qualifying searches and your ad appeared on 400, your impression share is 40%. The remaining 60% is split between auctions lost to budget and auctions lost to ad rank.

## Why it matters for a local business

It answers a question owners rarely ask: of the people searching for my service in my town today, how many even saw me? A Weymouth plumber at 25% is invisible to three of every four searchers and a competitor is taking those calls. For a business holding a Market Lock territory, impression share on the core searches in that town is one of the numbers to watch each month.

## In practice

A Rockland HVAC company spends $1,500 a month and holds 38% impression share on "furnace repair" searches, with 45% lost to budget. Cutting three low-intent keywords frees $350, which goes to the repair searches. Impression share rises to 61% with no added spend, and booked repair calls go from 14 to 21.
