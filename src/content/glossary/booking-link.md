## What it means

A booking link is a web address that opens a scheduling page connected to the business's real calendar. The customer sees only open slots, chooses one, enters contact details, and receives a confirmation. The appointment appears on the business's calendar and in the CRM, and reminder messages fire automatically. It replaces the phone tag of "what time works for you" with a thirty-second self-service step.

## Why it matters for a local business

Most local customers are deciding at night, on a phone, between three or four options. The business that lets them lock in a Tuesday 10 a.m. estimate right then wins, because the decision is made and the competitor's callback tomorrow arrives too late. ICS builds the booking link into every JobSite and includes it in the Lead Engine text-back, so a missed call at 9 p.m. still ends in a confirmed appointment before the customer goes to bed.

## In practice

A Hanover dental practice added a booking link for new-patient exams and hygiene visits to its site, Google profile, and after-hours text. In the first 90 days, 141 appointments were self-booked, 62 of them between 6 p.m. and 7 a.m. when the office was closed. Phone volume for scheduling dropped about 20 percent, freeing the front desk. Automated reminders with a reschedule option cut no-shows on self-booked visits to 4 percent, below the practice's 9 percent average for phone-booked ones.
