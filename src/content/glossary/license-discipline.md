## What it means

License discipline is formal action by a licensing board against someone it regulates: a contractor, physician, attorney, broker, engineer, or electrician. Sanctions include reprimands, fines, probation, suspension, and revocation. The board's order typically states the conduct at issue and the terms imposed.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A license is a claim that can be checked. Is it active? Has it ever lapsed? Has the board acted against it, here or in another state? A suspended construction supervisor license matters when that person is about to run your $600,000 build-out. We report the board's order as written, including dates and whether the license was later restored in good standing.

## In practice

A medical practice in Hanover is considering a merger with a 2-doctor group. License lookups for both physicians show active status. One has a 9-year-old board action: a reprimand and a $5,000 fine over recordkeeping, with no further action since. The practice's attorney gets the order itself, 6 pages, and the principals discuss it openly before proceeding.
