## What it means

A ground lease separates the land from what sits on it. The tenant leases the parcel, constructs and owns the building during the term, and pays ground rent. At expiration the improvements usually revert to the landowner. It is a tool for families who want to keep land and operators who want to control a site.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Along the South Shore's older highway corridors, some of the best commercial parcels are held by families who will never sell. A ground lease is how a franchise restaurant or a bank branch ends up on land it does not own, and how the family keeps the land while earning from it.

## In practice

A family in Stoughton ground leases a Route 138 corner to a quick-service restaurant for 25 years with two ten-year options. The restaurant builds its own building; the family receives ground rent with periodic increases and keeps the land.
