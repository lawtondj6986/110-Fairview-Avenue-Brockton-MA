## What it means

Intent keywords are search phrases classified by what the searcher is trying to accomplish: informational (how, why, what), navigational (a specific business name), commercial (best, reviews, versus), and transactional (book, near me, cost, emergency, same day). The words around the service term reveal where the person is in the buying process.

## Why it matters for a local business

Ad budgets in a local business are small, and intent is how they stretch. "Furnace" is a dictionary entry. "Furnace repair Hanover same day" is a customer. ICS builds Lead Engine search campaigns around transactional intent paired with town names across the Market Lock territory, and writes JobSite pages for the informational questions so the business is present at both ends. Negative keywords strip out the job seekers, DIY researchers, and parts shoppers.

## In practice

A Rockland plumber spent $1,800 a month on broad terms and booked 11 jobs. Rebuilt around 60 transactional phrases paired with eight South Shore towns, the same budget drew fewer clicks, 340 instead of 520, but booked 27 jobs. Cost per booked job fell from $164 to $67, and the owner stopped getting calls from people looking for a plumbing supply store on Route 18.
