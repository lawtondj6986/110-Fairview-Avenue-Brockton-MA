## What it means

A prompt is the text given to an AI system that tells it what to do. It can be a single question typed by a user, or a long set of standing instructions written by whoever set the system up: the role, the facts it may rely on, the rules, and the format of the answer. The quality of the output follows the quality of the prompt.

## Why it matters for a local business

When an AI chat agent or phone agent represents a business, its standing prompt is the training manual. A vague one produces a generic assistant that guesses. A specific one produces an agent that knows the Brockton shop closes at 4 on Saturdays, does not service commercial units, and books estimates only on weekdays. ICS writes these from an intake session with the owner and tests them against real customer questions before launch.

## In practice

A Boca Raton pool service starts with a 3-sentence prompt and its chat agent answers 58% of test questions correctly. The rebuilt prompt runs about 900 words: 14 services with prices, 9 cities served, 6 handoff rules. On the same 40 test questions it scores 95%, with the other 2 handed to staff.
