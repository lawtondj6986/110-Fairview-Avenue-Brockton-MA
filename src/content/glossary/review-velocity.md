## What it means

Review velocity is the rate at which a business accumulates new reviews. Search and AI systems weigh recency and consistency, so a profile earning steady reviews outranks one with a larger but stale total. It is a leading indicator of both marketing health and operational quality.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Across the South Shore, most businesses have fewer than thirty reviews and have not received one in months. The automated review request is the simplest, highest-return piece of every ICS system: a text an hour after the job, a link, and a thank-you when it lands.

## In practice

A Bridgewater HVAC company with 31 reviews installs an automated post-job request. Twelve months later it has 140, averaging nine a month, and it holds the top map position in three towns.
