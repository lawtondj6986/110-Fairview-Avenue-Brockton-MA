## What it means

Procurement records are the documents a government body creates when it buys something: the request for proposals or invitation for bids, addenda, the list of bidders, each submission, evaluation sheets, and the award decision. Where contract award records show the result, procurement records show the process.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Process is where the questions are. Who else bid? Was the low bidder passed over, and why? Was there one bidder three years running? For ballot and opposition research, the file documents how public money was committed. For a vendor, a competitor's winning proposal is a lawful, public look at pricing. We report what the file shows and stop short of inferring motive.

## In practice

A client bidding on a town's 3-year facilities contract lost the last round. We request the prior procurement file. The town responds in 9 business days with 140 pages at no charge. The winner bid $412,000 against our client's $455,000, and scored higher on references. The client knows exactly where to sharpen the next proposal. The evaluation sheets show the client lost 8 of 100 points on references alone, which is fixable. Nothing in the file required anything beyond a written request to the town.
