## What it means

Zoning decision records are the files a zoning board of appeals creates when it hears a case: the application, plans, abutter notices, hearing minutes, and the written decision with its findings and conditions. Under M.G.L. c. 40A the decision is filed with the clerk, which starts a 20-day appeal period.

## Why it matters in a dossier

A property's legal use is often defined by an old decision, not the zoning map. Conditions can limit hours, parking, signage, or the type of business allowed, and they bind later owners. Denials matter too: they show what the board has already refused on that site. We pull every decision for the parcel and flag conditions the current use appears to exceed.

## In practice

A buyer wants a 6,500 square foot building in Weymouth for a contractor yard. The file shows a 1998 special permit for the current use, with 5 conditions: no outdoor storage, operations limited to 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. The buyer's plan needs both changed. That means a new hearing, roughly 90 days, before the deal makes sense. The decision was also recorded at the registry, so the conditions will show up in the title work regardless.
