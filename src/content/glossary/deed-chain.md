## What it means

A deed chain is the ordered list of recorded transfers for a parcel: each grantor, each grantee, the date, the stated consideration, and the book and page. Massachusetts deeds usually recite the price paid and the prior deed reference, which makes the chain easy to follow backward on the registry's public site.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Ownership history answers questions that a current owner's name cannot. How long have they held it? What did they pay? Was there a transfer for $1 to a related LLC or trust? Did the property move right before a lawsuit or a divorce filing? The chain gives dates and parties, which we then match against court and corporate records.

## In practice

A client is negotiating to buy a 12,000 square foot industrial building in Rockland. The chain shows the seller's LLC took title 14 months ago for $1.1 million from an estate. The asking price is $1.85 million. The $750,000 spread is a fair question for the seller, and the building permit history shows only $60,000 of permitted work since. The client negotiates with that in hand.
