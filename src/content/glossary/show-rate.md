## What it means

Show rate is the share of scheduled appointments that actually happen: the patient arrives, or the homeowner is there when the estimator pulls up. Its inverse is the no-show rate. It is measured per week or month and by appointment type, and it is one of the most direct measures of wasted capacity in a service business.

## Why it matters for a local business

A no-show costs twice: the slot is lost and the technician drove to Marshfield for nothing. A dental practice in Quincy with 320 appointments a month and a 78 percent show rate loses 70 visits, about $21,000 in production, mostly to forgetting rather than cancellation. Appointment booking automation inside JobSite and Lead Engine sends the confirmation and reminders by text, handles the reschedule, and flags unconfirmed slots so the front desk can fill them from a waitlist.

## In practice

A Weymouth HVAC company booked 180 estimates a month with a 74 percent show rate, so estimators made 47 wasted trips. ICS added a three-text reminder sequence with a one-tap reschedule. Show rate rose to 91 percent within two months. That is 31 recovered estimates a month; at a 35 percent close and a $6,800 average install, roughly $74,000 in monthly sales that was already booked and simply was not being kept.
