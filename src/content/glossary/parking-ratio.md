## What it means

The parking ratio expresses parking supply relative to building area, usually as spaces per 1,000 square feet. Zoning bylaws require minimum ratios by use, so a property's ratio determines which uses can legally occupy it. It is one of the first things to check in any site search.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

South Shore commercial parcels on older corridors were often built with parking for a 1970s retail use. Converting one to medical, a restaurant, or a fitness studio frequently fails on parking before anything else. We check the ratio against the intended use before a client visits a building.

## In practice

A 4,000 square foot Rockland building has 16 spaces, a ratio of 4 per 1,000. The buyer's intended urgent-care use requires 6 per 1,000 under the bylaw, or 24 spaces. The deal needs a shared parking agreement with the neighbor or a different building.
