## What it means

A review QR code is a printed square barcode that, when scanned with a phone camera, opens the business's review form directly, usually the Google Business Profile "write a review" link. It removes the search, the tapping, and the forgetting that stop most satisfied customers from ever posting. The code can be on cards, invoices, table tents, vehicle magnets, or the back of a receipt.

## Why it matters for a local business

Review count and recency drive the Google local pack, and the local pack drives calls. A Weymouth plumber with 38 reviews is losing to one with 210, even if the work is identical. Most customers will leave a review when asked at the right moment and given a one-tap path. ICS prints review QR codes as part of every Proof Engine setup, paired with a text request after the job, so there are two chances to catch the customer while they are pleased.

## In practice

A Taunton auto repair shop averaged two Google reviews a month. It added a QR card stapled to every invoice and a counter sign, and the front desk started saying "if we did a good job, that code takes ten seconds." Reviews rose to 14 a month. Over six months the profile went from 61 reviews to 146, the shop moved into the top three for "auto repair Taunton," and new-customer calls rose about 30 percent.
