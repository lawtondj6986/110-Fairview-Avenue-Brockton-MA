## What it means

Lead Engine is ICS's system for the stage after a lead arrives: instant response at any hour, a CRM and pipeline, automated follow-up cadences in the owner's voice, booking, and the Proof Engine that turns finished work into reviews and content. Its outcome is measured in quotes converted and jobs booked.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

When a South Shore owner says they get plenty of leads but not enough jobs, Lead Engine is the answer. The audit that precedes it measures response time and quote conversion; the dashboard that follows it measures the same two numbers every month.
