## What it means

A registered agent, called a resident agent in Massachusetts, is the individual or company designated by a business entity to accept service of process and official notices on its behalf. The agent must have a physical street address in the Commonwealth. The designation is made at formation and updated through annual reports, and it is public. Many entities use their attorney, an owner, or a commercial registered agent company.

## Why it matters in a dossier

The agent field is one of the best pivot points in entity research. The same attorney in Hanover appearing as agent for a dozen property LLCs is a pattern worth following in the Registry of Deeds. A change of agent from the founder to a commercial service often marks a sale or a dispute. ICS uses the agent only as a public-record link between entities. It does not contact agents to learn about their clients, which they would not disclose anyway.

## In practice

A buyer wants to know whether the seller of a Taunton industrial lot also owns the abutting parcels. The three abutting LLCs have different names and different managers listed, but all three share the same registered agent and the same principal office suite in Brockton. Registry of Deeds mortgages show one guarantor for all three. The buyer negotiates knowing the seller controls the whole block.
