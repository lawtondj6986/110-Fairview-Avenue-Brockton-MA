## What it means

A Uniform Commercial Code financing statement is filed by a creditor to perfect a security interest in a debtor's personal property. Because it is public and names both parties, a UCC search reveals a business's borrowing relationships and, often, its financial condition.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Before a South Shore landlord signs a five-year lease with a new tenant, or a buyer purchases a business and its building, ICS runs the UCC search. A stack of recent short-term lender filings is a warning that no financial statement will volunteer.
