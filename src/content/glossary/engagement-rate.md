## What it means

Engagement rate is the percentage of visits, or of people who saw a post, that involved real interaction. On a website, it is the share of sessions where the visitor stayed, clicked, or completed an action. On social media, it is likes, comments, shares, and saves divided by reach or followers. The definition changes by platform, so always check which one a report uses.

## Why it matters for a local business

Traffic is not the goal. A Plymouth roofer with 2,000 visits and a 35% engagement rate is paying for people who leave in seconds. The number is most useful as a comparison: which traffic source, which page, which Reels System video holds attention. A low rate on one landing page often points to a slow load, a mismatch between the ad and the page, or no clear next step.

## In practice

A Hanover dentist's site shows 64% engagement on visits from search and 31% on visits from a $600 a month social ad. The ad promised whitening and landed on the home page. Pointing it to a whitening page with a booking button lifts engagement to 55% and bookings from the ad from 3 a month to 8.
