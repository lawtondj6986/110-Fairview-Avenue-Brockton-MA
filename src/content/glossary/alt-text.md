## What it means

Alt text is a short written description attached to an image in a web page's code. Screen readers speak it aloud to visitors who cannot see the image, browsers show it when an image fails to load, and search engines read it to understand what the picture shows. It is invisible to most visitors and read by the audiences that matter for access and search.

## Why it matters for a local business

A contractor's site is mostly photos: kitchens, roofs, driveways. Without alt text, a search engine sees a page of blank rectangles. With it, each project photo says what the work was and where, which supports local search without stuffing the page. It also reduces accessibility risk. JobSite builds require alt text on every project photo before the page goes live.

## In practice

A Plymouth landscaper uploads 60 project photos named IMG_4471 and so on. Writing alt text for each takes about 45 minutes: "Bluestone patio with fire pit, Plymouth backyard." Within 90 days, image search sends 30 to 40 extra visits a month to the gallery, and the site passes its accessibility check on images.
