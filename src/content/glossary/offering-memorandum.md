## What it means

An offering memorandum is the document a listing broker produces to present a property to prospective buyers. It assembles everything a buyer needs to make an informed first offer, from the rent roll to the roof age, in a form that is easy to read and hard to misunderstand.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most sub-$3M South Shore listings go to market with a two-page flyer. ICS produces a full institutional-style OM on every listing, built with AI tooling and checked by a human, because the extra two hours of work consistently produces more and better offers. Every listing page on this site links its OM request.

## In practice

A 12-page OM for a mixed-use Whitman building includes the rent roll for three apartments and a storefront, three years of expenses, a pro forma, photos, a zoning summary, and the lease abstracts. Four offers arrive in two weeks.
