## What it means

An independent expenditure is money spent to expressly advocate for or against a candidate without cooperation, consultation, or coordination with that candidate or their committee. Because it is not coordinated, it is not a contribution and is not subject to contribution limits. The spender files its own disclosure report instead, naming the amount, purpose, and target. In Massachusetts those reports go to OCPF; federal races report to the FEC.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Local races on the South Shore are cheap to influence. A $4,000 mailer can swing a Hanover or Rockland select board seat, and the independent expenditure report tells you who paid for it. ICS reads these filings alongside OCPF contribution records to map who funds whom around a zoning fight or a ballot question. Everything comes from the public database; nobody is followed or called.

## In practice

A Plymouth County developer wants to know why a rezoning vote went sideways. The dossier finds two independent expenditure reports totaling $11,200 for digital ads against the incumbent who supported the project, paid by a committee organized three weeks before the vote. Its donor list, also on OCPF, shows four abutters. That is a public, lawful, and complete explanation.
