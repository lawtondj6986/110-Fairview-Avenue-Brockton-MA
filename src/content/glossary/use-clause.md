## What it means

The use clause specifies the purposes for which the tenant may use the premises. It interacts with zoning, with other tenants' exclusive rights, and with the tenant's ability to evolve or sell the business. Drafted narrowly, it is a trap; drafted broadly, it is freedom.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

On South Shore strip centers, use clauses are often cut and pasted from the last tenant's lease. A new tenant should read theirs as if their business will change, because over a five-year term it will. The lease should permit where you are going, not just where you are.

## In practice

A tenant's lease permits only the retail sale of running shoes. Two years later the business wants to add a physical therapy partner and group classes; the landlord can refuse. A clause permitting athletic retail and related fitness services would have allowed both.
