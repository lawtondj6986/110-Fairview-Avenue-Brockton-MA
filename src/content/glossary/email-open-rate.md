## What it means

Email open rate is the percentage of successfully delivered emails that recipients opened, measured by a tiny tracking image loading in the message. It was once the standard first-look metric for any campaign. Privacy changes, especially Apple Mail Privacy Protection, now preload that image for many users whether they read the email or not, so the number runs high and is less trustworthy than it was.

## Why it matters for a local business

A Marshfield auto repair shop with 1,800 past customers on its list has a real asset, but only if the emails get read. Open rate tells you whether the subject line and sender name are working and whether your messages are landing in the inbox at all. A sudden drop from 45 to 20 percent usually means a deliverability problem, not a boring subject line. ICS uses open rate in Lead Engine as a smoke alarm, and uses clicks and bookings as the real scorecard.

## In practice

A Hingham dental practice sent a six-month recall reminder to 620 overdue patients. Open rate was 48 percent, click rate 9 percent, and 31 patients booked a cleaning within two weeks, about $6,500 in scheduled production from one email. A second send to the non-openers with a different subject line a week later added 11 more bookings. The two sends together cost under $40 to deliver.
