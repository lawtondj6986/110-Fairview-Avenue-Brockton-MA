## What it means

Loan-to-value is the lender's primary measure of how much equity cushions the loan. Lower LTV means more borrower equity and less lender risk. Together with the debt service coverage ratio, it determines how much you can borrow.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

For an owner-user buying a South Shore building, the SBA 504 program's 90 percent financing is often the difference between buying and continuing to rent. Our Lease vs. Buy tool models exactly that comparison with your rent, your price, and your down payment.

## In practice

Purchase price $1,200,000, bank loan at 50 percent and SBA 504 debenture at 40 percent: combined LTV 90 percent, buyer equity $120,000 instead of the $300,000 a conventional 75 percent loan would require.
