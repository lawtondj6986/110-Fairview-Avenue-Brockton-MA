## What it means

A right of first refusal gives its holder the chance to step into a deal on the same terms a third party has offered. In commercial leases it most often covers the purchase of the building or the lease of adjacent space, giving a tenant a path to expand or to own.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Many South Shore owner-operators rent from an aging landlord who will eventually sell. A ROFR or right of first offer negotiated into the lease is how a tenant turns that moment into an acquisition rather than a new landlord. We have seen it change the trajectory of a business.

## In practice

A Stoughton tenant holds a ROFR on its building. The landlord receives a $1,400,000 offer; the tenant matches within the 15-day window and buys the building it has operated from for twelve years.
