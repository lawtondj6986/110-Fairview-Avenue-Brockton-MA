## What it means

Core Web Vitals are three user-experience metrics Google measures on real visits: how quickly the main content appears, how quickly the page responds to interaction, and how much the layout jumps while loading. They are reported in Search Console and influence both rankings and visitor behavior.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

Most South Shore business sites built on heavy page builders fail these metrics on a phone, which is where most local searches happen. Every ICS build is measured against them before launch and monitored afterward.
