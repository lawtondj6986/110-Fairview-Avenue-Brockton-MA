## What it means

A trademark search is a review of registered and pending trademarks to identify marks that are identical or similar to a name, logo, or slogan. The primary source is the federal register maintained by the United States Patent and Trademark Office. Massachusetts maintains a state register as well. A search report lists the mark, owner, filing and registration dates, status, and the classes of goods or services claimed.

## Why it matters in a dossier

Trademark records are a public map of corporate ownership and intent. A filing by a Fort Lauderdale holding company for a mark matching a South Shore restaurant's name tells you about an expansion plan, a franchise relationship, or a coming dispute. For ICS clients launching a JobSite or Market Lock brand, the search comes before the domain purchase. For a dossier, the owner-of-record field often connects a brand to a parent entity that the corporate registry alone does not reveal.

## In practice

A Brockton dental practice wants to rebrand under a two-word name. The federal search finds a live registration for the same words in the dental services class, owned by a Florida group that registered in 2019 and opened its first Massachusetts office in Quincy this year. The practice spends $200 on an attorney's opinion, then chooses a different name instead of facing a rebrand after launch.
