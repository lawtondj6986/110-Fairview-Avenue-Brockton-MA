## What it means

Analysis of competing hypotheses is an analytic technique developed in the intelligence community to reduce confirmation bias. By evaluating evidence against a full set of hypotheses simultaneously and focusing on disconfirmation, it produces judgments that are more defensible and more honest about what the evidence cannot settle.

## Why it matters on the South Shore

A pattern of LLC transfers on a South Shore property might mean estate planning, lender pressure, or something worse. ICS's practice is to lay out all three, test each against the record, and tell the client which survives and how confidently, rather than lead with the most dramatic reading.
