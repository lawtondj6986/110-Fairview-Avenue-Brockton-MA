## What it means

Service radius is the geographic limit of where a business will send a crew or accept a customer, measured from the shop, the owner's home, or a central point. It may be a hard cutoff or a tiered one, with a trip charge or a minimum ticket beyond a certain distance.

## Why it matters for a local business

Every mile past the edge is unpaid time. A 45-minute drive each way turns a $180 service call into a loss. The South Shore makes this sharper: Route 3 and Route 24 run north to south, so east to west trips, such as Taunton to Marshfield, take far longer than the map suggests. Market Lock territories are defined town by town for this reason, and QuoteBrain can add a trip charge by ZIP code automatically.

## In practice

A Rockland handyman takes jobs within 30 minutes. A review of 200 jobs shows that the 31 jobs beyond 25 minutes averaged $38 an hour after drive time, against $71 inside it. He sets a $250 minimum outside the 25-minute line. Out-of-area jobs fall to 12, and their average ticket rises from $210 to $460.
