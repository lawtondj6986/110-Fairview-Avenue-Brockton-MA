Ask a homeowner in Coral Springs where she lives and she will not say Broward County. She will say Coral Springs, or off Wiles near University. Ask a man in a gated community west of the Turnpike with a Boca Raton address and he will say Boca, or West Boca, and he may not know that his street is outside the city limits.

County names are for tax bills, courts, and school districts. Customers do not think in them, and they do not search in them. That matters, because the words on your website and your Google Business Profile are what decide whether you appear when someone asks a search engine or an AI assistant for an electrician near them.

This is a guide to how people on each side of the county line describe where they are, and how to turn that into pages that get found.

## Four ways people say where they are

Listen to a week of inbound calls and you will hear the same four patterns.

- **The city.** Plantation, Hollywood, Delray, Jupiter. This is the default, and it is what people type.
- **The side.** East of 95. West of the Turnpike. East of Federal. On the barrier island. In South Florida the highways run north to south, so east and west carry real meaning: age of housing, lot size, flood exposure, traffic.
- **The community.** The name on the gate. In HOA country, from Coconut Creek to Wellington, people identify with the community before the city.
- **The road.** Off Glades. On Las Olas. By Sawgrass. Pines and Flamingo. An intersection is the most precise thing most customers can give you.

The county comes fifth, if at all. A page titled plumber serving Broward County matches almost nothing a customer says. A page that says we work in Plantation, mostly in the neighborhoods between Broward Boulevard and Peters Road, east and west of University Drive, matches all four.

## Broward: cities, sides, and the big roads

Broward is almost entirely incorporated. Nearly every address is inside one of the county's cities, and people know which one. That makes Broward straightforward: the city name is reliable.

What changes inside it is the vocabulary.

- In [Fort Lauderdale](/broward/fort-lauderdale/) people name districts: Las Olas, Flagler Village, the 17th Street area, the beach. It is the most competitive local-search market in the county, so a page has to be specific to compete.
- In the west and southwest, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Weston, people name the boulevard and the community: Pines Boulevard, Flamingo Road, Weston Road.
- Along State Road 7, which everyone calls 441, the road is the identity. A shop in Margate, North Lauderdale, or Lauderhill is on 441, and customers say so.
- At the north edge, [Deerfield Beach](/broward/deerfield-beach/) businesses serve two counties from one address. Their customers in Boca Raton do not care which side of the line the shop is on.

One caution on names. Fort Lauderdale is also used loosely for the whole area, especially by seasonal residents and visitors. A caller who says Fort Lauderdale may be in Oakland Park or Wilton Manors. Ask.

## Palm Beach County: the name that means three things

Palm Beach County is harder, for two reasons.

First, the name. Palm Beach can mean the island town, the county, or West Palm Beach said quickly. A business that writes serving Palm Beach on its homepage has told a search engine very little. Name the cities.

Second, mailing addresses do not match city limits. Large areas west of Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, and Lake Worth Beach carry those city names on the mail but are unincorporated county. Residents use the city name anyway, usually with west in front of it. A business that only describes the incorporated city can miss a large share of the households that call themselves Boca.

The county also has regional names that locals use constantly:

- **South county.** [Boca Raton](/palm-beach/boca-raton/), Delray Beach, Boynton Beach.
- **Central county.** West Palm Beach, Lake Worth Beach, Greenacres, Palm Springs.
- **North county.** Palm Beach Gardens, [Jupiter](/palm-beach/jupiter/), Juno Beach, North Palm Beach, Tequesta.
- **The western communities.** Wellington and Royal Palm Beach.

A customer in Tequesta says north county or Jupiter area. Use those phrases in the copy of the pages they apply to. They are how real people talk, and they are what real reviews say.

## The county line is not a wall

Businesses treat the line between Deerfield Beach and Boca Raton as a border. Customers cross it without noticing. The drive from Hillsboro Boulevard to Palmetto Park Road is about ten minutes.

A worked example. A garage door company based in an industrial park off Powerline Road in Deerfield Beach has a website that says serving Broward County. Its call records for a quarter show 400 jobs:

- 110 in Deerfield Beach, Pompano Beach, and Lighthouse Point.
- 90 in Coral Springs, Coconut Creek, and Parkland.
- 150 in Boca Raton and West Boca.
- 50 in Delray Beach.

Half the revenue comes from a county the website never mentions, all of it from referrals and repeat customers. Nobody new in Boca finds the company by search, because nothing on the site or the profile says Boca.

The fix is two real pages, Boca Raton and Delray Beach, plus those cities added to the profile's service area and review requests that go to those 200 customers. If that adds even 8 new jobs a month at $650, it is about $62,000 a year from words on a page.

## How to write a place page that works

A place page earns its position by being about the place. The test: if you swapped the city name for another, would the page still read as true? If yes, it is a template, and both search engines and AI assistants discount templates.

A real page has:

- **The words residents use.** West Boca, east of Federal, off Sample Road, the western communities.
- **The housing or building stock you actually see.** High-rise condos with association rules in Hallandale Beach. Acreage lots in Davie. Older single-family homes in Lake Worth Beach.
- **Roads and districts, not invented landmarks.** The corridors a local would name.
- **Recent work there.** Three jobs with the month, the neighborhood, and what was done.
- **Reviews from there.** A review that says came out to our house in Parkland the same day does more than anything you write.
- **Plain logistics.** Response time to that city, any trip fee, hours.

For a business with a storefront or an office, add how to get there in local terms. If the question of buying or leasing that space comes up, note that ICS's real estate brokerage is Massachusetts-licensed and a Florida property matter is handled alongside a licensed Florida broker. In Florida ICS builds systems only.

## Ask for the city first

The same thinking belongs on the phone and the form. The first question on any inquiry should be what city are you in, followed by the nearest main road. That one answer drives everything after it:

- Which technician or crew is closest.
- Whether a trip fee applies.
- Which page and which reviews to send with the quote.
- Which season calendar the customer lives on. A condo owner on the coast is seasonal. A family in Cooper City runs on the school year.

It also builds your data. After three months you will know, by count, where your customers are and what they call it. That list is your page plan. The season notes for both counties are at [/this-month/?market=fl](/this-month/?market=fl).
