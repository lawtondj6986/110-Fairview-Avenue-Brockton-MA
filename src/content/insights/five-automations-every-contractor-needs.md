We build AI systems for South Shore trades businesses every week, and the same five automations deliver the fastest payback almost every time. None of them are exotic. All of them are things you already know you should be doing — the automation just makes them actually happen.

## 1. Instant lead response

The data on this is brutal: respond to a lead within five minutes and you're dramatically more likely to win the job than at thirty minutes. An automated first-touch — text plus email, acknowledging the enquiry and asking one qualifying question — runs 24/7, including when you're on a roof or in a crawlspace.

## 2. Quote follow-up sequences

Most contractors send a quote and wait. The winners follow up at day 2, day 5, and day 10 — but almost nobody has time to track that manually. A CRM sequence does it flawlessly, in your voice, and stops the moment the customer replies.

## 3. Review requests after every job

Reviews are the currency of local search — and now of AI recommendations too. An automatic request sent the day a job closes, with a direct link to your Google profile, will typically multiply your review velocity several times over. Rating and volume both climb, and so does your ranking.

## 4. Booking and estimate scheduling

Every phone-tag round with a customer is time you're not billing. A booking link that syncs to your real calendar — with buffer time and service areas built in — turns three calls into zero.

## 5. Invoice chasing

The least glamorous automation is often the one owners love most. Polite, persistent, automatic reminders on unpaid invoices — day 7, day 14, day 21 — improve cash flow without a single awkward phone call.
