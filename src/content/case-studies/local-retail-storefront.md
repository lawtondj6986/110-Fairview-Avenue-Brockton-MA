## The Challenge

A successful single-location retailer signed a lease on a second storefront — with no website for the new location, four Google reviews, and a lead follow-up process that lived in one person's head.

## What We Built

**Lead Engine system**, live before opening day:

- New location website with inventory highlights, hours, and a booking/enquiry flow
- Google Business Profile built out and optimized so the store surfaced in local searches from day one
- Automated review request sequence for every purchase and appointment
- CRM implementation with email/SMS nurture so walk-ins and web enquiries get followed up automatically

## The Results

Forty-seven qualified leads in the first 60 days, twenty-three new Google reviews in ninety days, and average lead response time down from a week to two days — measured in the same dashboard the owner checks every Monday morning.

## What's Next

Seasonal AI-generated campaign creative through the **AI Marketing** add-on, timed to the store's holiday peak.
