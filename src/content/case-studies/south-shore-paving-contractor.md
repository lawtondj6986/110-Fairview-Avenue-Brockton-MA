## The Challenge

Strong word-of-mouth reputation, but a digital presence that didn't match the quality of the work. Quoting was manual and email-heavy, there was no systematic review collection, and a new commercial yard location needed immediate visibility to justify the investment.

## What We Built

**Launch + Operate package**, delivered in three weeks:

- New website with dedicated service pages, a before/after project gallery, and a structured quote request form
- Google Business Profile optimization for the new yard location, targeting the towns the crews actually serve
- Automated review requests triggered after every completed job
- A lightweight CRM with lead tracking and automated quote follow-up sequences — no lead goes cold in an inbox

## The Results

Within the first 90 days: quote requests tripled versus the prior period, review velocity jumped and the Google rating climbed from 4.2 to 4.7, and the owner reports roughly 12 hours per week back from admin and quoting follow-up.

## What's Next

The company is now evaluating the **Scale** tier — proposal automation and seasonal campaign management — as it adds a second crew.
