## The Challenge

Solar sales are won on speed and professionalism. This installer was hand-building every proposal — site data, incentives, financing options — and taking eleven days on average from enquiry to signed contract. Competitors were getting there first.

## What We Built

**Scale package**:

- Custom proposal-generation workflow: property data, system sizing inputs, and current incentive programs assembled into a branded proposal in minutes instead of days
- Document automation for contracts and interconnection paperwork
- Automated follow-up sequences at every stage of the pipeline — enquiry, proposal sent, decision pending
- Quarterly strategy reviews against a shared KPI dashboard

## The Results

Average quote-to-close time fell from eleven days to four. The same team now sends twice as many proposals per week, every lead gets a same-day first response, and the owner has roughly eighteen hours a month back for site visits and hiring.

## What's Next

Extending the automation into post-install customer care — service reminders, referral requests, and review collection.
