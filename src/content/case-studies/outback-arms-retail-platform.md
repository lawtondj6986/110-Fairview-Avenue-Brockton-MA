## The Challenge

Outback Arms is a family-run firearms dealer in Plympton, Massachusetts — established 2012, known for FFL transfers, Mass-approved safety classes, and a counter staffed by people who actually shoot. Their problem wasn't the business. It was the internet's posture toward the category:

- **Shopify and the major site builders prohibit or restrict firearm sales** — so the standard playbook every other retailer uses was off the table.
- **Meta and Google won't run product ads** for the category, closing the paid-traffic shortcut.
- **Mainstream payment processors routinely drop firearms merchants**, sometimes mid-stream.
- And like most independent retailers, what was actually on the shelf lived in the shop's head — not on the web, where customers were looking first.

A satisfied walk-in customer base, and a digital storefront that couldn't show them anything.

## What We Built

A **custom retail platform on open code the owner fully owns** — no platform landlord, no policy rug-pull:

- **Real-time inventory on the website.** Stock, pricing, and photos synced to the floor. Customers check what's actually in the case before they drive to Plympton — and call about specific items instead of "what do you have?"
- **An owner-run admin panel.** Listing a product, changing a price, marking something sold — done from the counter in minutes, no developer, no ticket queue.
- **Reserve-and-pickup built around the compliance process.** Customers commit online; background checks, transfers, and paperwork happen at the counter, per the shop's license and process — exactly where the law says they should.
- **Product-level SEO and answer-engine visibility** for the searches the ad networks won't sell: "who has X in stock near me" now finds a live shelf, not a brochure.

## The Results

The store reports **business up 300% since the platform launched** — and the owner continues to run the storefront personally, day to day, from the panel. The website now does what the category's restrictions were supposed to make impossible: it sells around the clock, on infrastructure nobody can evict them from.

That figure is the store's own report, and we're happy to walk through exactly what changed on a call. Better yet: visit [outbackarms.com](https://outbackarms.com) and watch the live shelf working right now — it's the one case study you can browse yourself.

Full disclosure, made proudly: **our founder works this counter.** The platform was built by someone who has to live with it every shift — which is exactly why it fits the way a counter actually runs.
