## 2. Methods and standards (the "Methods Code")

All work is performed from **public records and lawful open sources only.** ICS does not and will not:
pretext, impersonate, or use deception to obtain information; access private financial, banking, phone,
or medical records; conduct surveillance, hacking, device access, or location tracking; or research
private individuals' personal lives lacking any nexus to a public role or the matter. ICS does **not**
provide "consumer reports" and this engagement may **not** be used for employment, tenant-screening, or
credit decisions governed by the Fair Credit Reporting Act. Every finding is sourced and assigned a
reliability grade and confidence level; material ICS judges non-usable or outside these limits is
withheld and identified as withheld.

ICS works **one client per contest/matter.** ICS has run a conflict check for this matter and is clear to
proceed. ICS will not take an opposing engagement in the same contest during this engagement. Client
confirms it is authorized to engage ICS for this matter.
