# ICS Intelligence & Research — Intake-to-Delivery SOP

> Internal operating procedure for the OSINT / political / dossier practice. Keep it boring and
> repeatable — the discipline is the product. Not legal advice; see the engagement-letter template's
> banner for the legal/insurance/licensing prerequisites that must be in place before Stage 4.

The pipeline has **8 stages and 3 gates.** A matter never skips a gate.

---

## Stage 1 — Inquiry received
**Source:** website intake form (`/political/`), the Deep Dossier inquiry (`/intelligence/dossier/`), email, or phone.
**Do:**
- Open a matter line in the **Conflict Registry** (below). Assign a Matter No.
- Capture: requester, their role, subject/contest, jurisdiction, timeline, budget band.
- Acknowledge within one business day (same day in an election cycle).

## 🚦 GATE 1 — Conflict check  *(before any substantive conversation about findings)*
- Search the Conflict Registry for the **same contest or the same subject**.
- **One client per contest. One side per subject in a dispute.** If we already hold that side, decline politely and log it.
- Confirm the subject is a **public figure, official, organization, or a principal in the matter** — not a private individual's personal life. If it fails the Methods Code, decline and log the reason.
- Record: `CLEAR` / `CONFLICTED` / `DECLINED (reason)`. Only `CLEAR` proceeds.

## Stage 2 — Scoping call (30 min, confidential)
**Do:**
- Define the deliverable (Audit / Book / Rapid-Response / Issue Landscape / Integrity Scan / Dossier), subjects, depth, jurisdictions, timeline.
- Set expectations out loud: public record only; findings graded and sourced; some items will come back `DO NOT USE`; we tell you what we don't know.
- Draft a one-page **written scope** (what's in, what's out, fee, timeline).

## 🚦 GATE 2 — Engagement letter + payment  *(before any research begins)*
- Send the **engagement letter** (attorney-reviewed template) with the written scope attached.
- Do **not** open sources until the signed letter **and** the initial payment are both received.
- File the signed letter under the Matter No. Update the registry to `ENGAGED`.

## Stage 3 — Research (to tradecraft standard)
**Do, logging every source as you go:**
- Work the record categories in priority order (see `What the record can document` on `/political/`): public record, court history, financial exposure, conflicts/self-dealing, public-money signals, résumé/narrative, compliance file; plus the dossier stack for business subjects.
- **Grade every source** on the A–F / 1–6 scale (reliability of source / credibility of information) — see appendix.
- **Capture the exhibit** (PDF/screenshot with URL + retrieval date) for every claim. No exhibit → not a finding.
- Keep **fact separate from assessment** in your notes from the start.

## 🚦 GATE 3 — Methods-Code QA review  *(before anything leaves the building)*
Self-review (or second reader) against this checklist. **All must be YES:**
- [ ] Every finding has an exhibit, a source grade, and a confidence level.
- [ ] Every finding passes the nexus test (bears on the public role / the matter).
- [ ] Each finding carries a usability rating: `USE` / `USE WITH CONTEXT` / `DO NOT USE`.
- [ ] `DO NOT USE` and out-of-scope material is sealed in the annex, not in the body.
- [ ] No characterization the record can't carry (no "bribery"/"criminal" unless adjudicated).
- [ ] Assumptions and gaps are stated. Competing (innocent) explanation addressed for each major item.
- [ ] Nothing obtained by prohibited means. Subject is in-scope per the Methods Code.

## Stage 4 — Delivery
**Do:**
- Produce the deliverable using `dossier-template.md` — **BLUF** executive summary first.
- Deliver on a **need-to-know** basis, confidentially (secure link / in person). No bcc, no shared drives.
- Walk the client through it personally. Flag the `DO NOT USE` items and *why*.

## Stage 5 — Close
- Collect balance. Update registry to `DELIVERED`.
- Offer the standing option: Rapid-Response retainer (campaigns) or annual Integrity Scan refresh (officials).
- Retain the working file per the engagement letter's handling clause, then destroy/archive on schedule.

## Appendix A — Source reliability & credibility scale (Admiralty / NATO)

| Reliability of source | | Credibility of information |
|---|---|---|
| **A** Completely reliable | | **1** Confirmed by other sources |
| **B** Usually reliable | | **2** Probably true |
| **C** Fairly reliable | | **3** Possibly true |
| **D** Not usually reliable | | **4** Doubtful |
| **E** Unreliable | | **5** Improbable |
| **F** Cannot be judged | | **6** Cannot be judged |

Grade every finding, e.g. **(B2)**. Public primary records (court/registry/filings) are typically A–B/1–2.
Anonymous or single-sourced web claims are rarely better than C–D/3–4 and are flagged as such.
